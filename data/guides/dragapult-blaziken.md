Dragapult Blaziken is Dragapult with an Energy engine. Blaziken ex's Seething Spirit attaches a Basic Energy from your discard pile every turn, so the Fire and Psychic on a Knocked Out Dragapult ex come straight back. Blaziken ex is also a 320 HP Fire attacker: Smolder-sault does 200, and the Grass and Metal decks of the format are Weak to Fire. Three Team Rocket's Watchtower turn off the Colorless engines of the Mega decks. The Phantom Dive basics are in the [Dragapult guide](guide.html?id=dragapult).

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** Dragapult with two Stage 2 lines. Slower to set up, much harder to run out of Energy.
- **Wins by:** Phantom Dive every turn with Seething Spirit paying half of it, and Smolder-sault for 400 against Fire-Weak Pokémon.
- **Prize profile:** Dragapult ex, Blaziken ex, Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2. Everything else gives up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two with Rare Candy (Dragapult ex or Blaziken ex); otherwise Phantom Dive on your third turn.
- **What beats it:** early pressure on a two-line setup, Water attackers (Blaziken ex, Combusken, Torchic and Chi-Yu are Weak to Water), and the Dragapult answers: Lillie's Clefairy ex, Hide 'n' Sneak and Battle Cage.

## Card by card

### The Blaziken line

- **Blaziken ex (320 HP, Fire, Weak to Water, Retreat 2).** Evolves from Combusken. Seething Spirit: once during your turn, attach a Basic Energy card from your discard pile to one of your Pokémon. Smolder-sault (Fire and one more) does 200, and Blaziken ex can't attack during your next turn.
- **Combusken (100 HP, Weak to Water, Retreat 2).** One copy; Rare Candy usually skips it.
- **Torchic (70 HP, Weak to Water, Retreat 1).** Collect (one Energy): draw a card.

### The Dragapult line

- **Dragapult ex (320 HP, no Weakness).** Phantom Dive (Fire and Psychic): 200 to their Active and 6 damage counters on their bench. It is a Tera Pokémon: on your bench it takes no damage from attacks.
- **Drakloak (90 HP).** Recon Directive: look at the top 2 cards, keep 1.
- **Dreepy (70 HP).** Four copies; two of them should be on your bench by the end of turn one.

### Support Pokémon

- **Chi-Yu (110 HP, Fire, Weak to Water).** Allure (one Energy): draw 2. Ground Melter (Fire and one more): 60, plus 60 while any Stadium is in play, then discard that Stadium. It is your only attacker that isn't a Pokémon ex, and it removes a Stadium as it attacks.
- **Munkidori** (two), **Budew**, **Fezandipiti ex** and **Meowth ex** work as in the Dragapult guide. Meowth ex is a Colorless Pokémon, so your own Watchtower turns its Ability off: bench it before the Watchtower goes down.

### Trainers

- **Team Rocket's Watchtower** (three): Colorless Pokémon in play have no Abilities. That turns off Mega Kangaskhan ex's Run Errand, Mega Rayquaza ex's Ruler's Roar, Dudunsparce's Run Away Draw, Fan Rotom and Meowth ex. Talonflame ex's Excited Dive still works, because it is used from the hand.
- **Rare Candy** (two): Torchic into Blaziken ex, or Dreepy into Dragapult ex, skipping the Stage 1.
- **Ultra Ball** (four): its two discards are Seething Spirit's fuel.
- **Crispin** (two), **Judge** (two), **Unfair Stamp**, **Night Stretcher** (two), **Poké Pad** and **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** (four each).

### Energy

Three Psychic, three Fire, two Darkness. Seething Spirit only moves Basic Energy, and all eight are Basic.

## The first two turns

### Going second

1. Bench two Dreepy and a Torchic. Buddy-Buddy Poffin finds any two of them; all have 70 HP.
2. Crispin for Fire and Psychic on a benched Dreepy.
3. On turn two, Rare Candy on whichever line gives you the attack you need first: Blaziken ex for Smolder-sault against a Fire-Weak deck, Dragapult ex otherwise.

### Going first

Bench Dreepy, Dreepy and Torchic, attach Fire to Torchic, and keep Budew in your hand for turn two.

> Two Stage 2 lines are a lot to build. If only one can be ready by turn three, build Dragapult: Seething Spirit makes the second Dragapult ex faster, but Phantom Dive is the attack the deck is built on.

## The middle game

### Seething Spirit every turn

One Basic Energy from your discard pile, every turn, from the bench. Put it where the next attack needs it: the second Energy for Phantom Dive, a Darkness for Munkidori, a Fire for Smolder-sault. Ultra Ball and Crispin put Energy in the discard pile on purpose.

### Smolder-sault against Fire Weakness

| Target | HP | Smolder-sault |
|---|---|---|
| Mega Excadrill ex (Weak to Fire) | 340 | 400: Knocked Out |
| Hydrapple ex, Teal Mask Ogerpon ex (Weak to Fire) | 330, 210 | 400: Knocked Out |
| Genesect ex (Weak to Fire) | 220 | 400: Knocked Out |
| Crustle (Weak to Fire) | 150–170 | Nothing: Mysterious Rock Inn stops attacks by Pokémon ex. Use Chi-Yu. |

Blaziken ex can't attack the turn after Smolder-sault. Alternate with Dragapult ex, or move Blaziken ex to the bench, which clears the effect.

### Watchtower against Colorless engines

Watchtower is your best card against Mega Rayquaza (no Ruler's Roar, no Run Errand) and the Dudunsparce decks (no Run Away Draw). Against decks with no Colorless Abilities, keep it in your hand as a Stadium that replaces theirs, or as fuel for Chi-Yu's Ground Melter.

## Closing the game

- **Two Mega Knock Outs are six prizes.** Seething Spirit keeps Dragapult ex attacking through the trade.
- **Boss's Orders, not Crispin.** With Seething Spirit paying half of Phantom Dive, your Supporter is free for the last prizes.
- **Smolder-sault for a full-HP Fire-Weak Mega**, Phantom Dive for everything else.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Which line first.** Dragapult, unless they are Weak to Fire and Smolder-sault takes 3 prizes on turn two.
2. **Rare Candy target.** You have two. One per line is usually right.
3. **Watchtower or Meowth ex.** Your own Watchtower turns off Meowth ex. Bench Meowth ex and take its Supporter first.
4. **Smolder-sault or Phantom Dive.** Smolder-sault when Weakness makes it a Knock Out; Phantom Dive when the counters matter more.
5. **Seething Spirit's target.** Whatever next turn's attack is missing.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Smolder-sault into Mega Excadrill

```play
setup: You go second against Mega Excadrill. Mega Excadrill ex (340 HP) is Weak to Fire, so Blaziken ex's Smolder-sault (Fire and one more) does 400. Seething Spirit attaches a Basic Energy from your discard pile each turn, so a Blaziken ex can attack the turn it evolves.
opp 1: Drilbur Active; Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches two Beldum, and they attach Metal to Drilbur.
you 1: Dreepy A Active. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Torchic and Dreepy B; attach Fire to Torchic. Lillie's Determination draws 8.
opp 2: Drilbur becomes Mega Excadrill ex and both Beldum become Metang. Metal Maker puts two more Metal on Excadrill. Undermine (90) Knocks Out Dreepy A and discards your top 2 cards. 1 prize. You promote Torchic.
you 2: Ultra Ball, discarding a Fire and a Psychic, finds Blaziken ex. Rare Candy: the Active Torchic becomes Blaziken ex. Seething Spirit attaches the Fire from your discard pile: two Fire pays for Smolder-sault. 200, doubled to 400: Excadrill is Knocked Out, 3 prizes. Attach a Fire from your hand to Dreepy B.
opp 3: Their second Mega Excadrill ex comes down with three Energy. Maximum Drilling does 200 to Blaziken ex (120 left).
you 3: Blaziken ex can't attack this turn, but Seething Spirit still works: the Psychic in your discard pile goes onto Drakloak B, evolved from Dreepy B, which already has a Fire. Next turn it is a Dragapult ex that can Phantom Dive.
lesson: Fire Weakness makes Smolder-sault 400, a full Mega Excadrill ex in one attack. Discard Basic Energy on purpose with Ultra Ball: Seething Spirit puts it back wherever the next attack needs it.
```

### The rebuild that saves your Supporter

```play
setup: Mid-game against Mega Rayquaza. You have taken 3 prizes for a Mega Kangaskhan ex. Their Mega Rayquaza ex A (280 HP) is on their bench with 80 left after your last Phantom Dive; Mega Rayquaza ex B is Active. Blaziken ex sits on your bench, and Drakloak B has no Energy.
opp 4: Storm Emerald from Rayquaza B with 7 Fire and Lightning in play: 350 Knocks Out Dragapult ex A. 2 prizes for them. Its Fire and Psychic go to your discard pile. You promote Drakloak B.
you 5: Drakloak B becomes Dragapult ex. Seething Spirit puts the Fire from your discard pile on it; attach the Psychic from your hand. Your Supporter is still free: Boss's Orders brings up Rayquaza A, and Phantom Dive Knocks it Out. 3 prizes: you win.
lesson: A Dragapult ex that evolves after a Knock Out usually needs Crispin to attack that same turn, and Crispin is your Supporter for the turn. Seething Spirit pays half the cost from your discard pile, so the Supporter can be Boss's Orders for the last prizes.
```

### Chi-Yu against Crustle

```play
setup: You go second against Crustle. Mysterious Rock Inn stops all damage from Pokémon ex attacks, so neither Dragapult ex nor Blaziken ex can hurt an Active Crustle. Chi-Yu (110 HP) can: it isn't a Pokémon ex, Crustle is Weak to Fire, and Ground Melter (Fire and one more) does 60, plus 60 while a Stadium is in play, then discards that Stadium.
opp 1: Dwebble A Active with Growing Grass Energy; Dwebble B benched.
you 1: Chi-Yu Active with a Fire. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Dreepy A and Torchic. Lillie's Determination draws 8, and Allure draws 2 more.
opp 2: Hilda finds Crustle: Dwebble A evolves, 170 HP with Growing Grass. They bench Mega Kangaskhan ex.
you 2: Attach a Psychic to Chi-Yu. Play Team Rocket's Watchtower, which also turns off Kangaskhan's Run Errand. Ground Melter: 120, doubled to 240. Crustle is Knocked Out, 1 prize, and the Watchtower is discarded.
opp 3: They promote Dwebble B; Ascension evolves it into Crustle B (150 HP).
you 3: A second Watchtower, then Ground Melter again: 240 Knocks Out Crustle B. 2 prizes.
lesson: Against Crustle your best attacker is a 110 HP Basic. Each Ground Melter costs a Stadium, so save your three Watchtowers for it, and put Phantom Dive's counters on Dwebble: counters aren't damage, so Mysterious Rock Inn never stops them.
```
