Dhelmise is a graveyard deck. Its attacker, Dhelmise, does 170 once four Pokémon with Hide 'n' Sneak are in your discard pile, and the deck plays thirteen of them. Hide 'n' Sneak itself is the second half of the plan: those Pokémon ignore every effect of your opponent's attacks and Abilities, so damage counters from Phantom Dive, Cursed Blast, Munkidori and Powerful Hand never land on them.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a single-prize deck that fills its own discard pile on purpose, then attacks for one Energy.
- **Wins by:** Vengeful Anchor (170) from Dhelmise, Banette's Puppet Pull (80 and a search), Sinistcha's Matcha Spin (4 damage counters on every one of their Pokémon), and Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail against decks full of Pokémon ex.
- **Prize profile:** almost everything gives up 1. Lillie's Clefairy ex, Dudunsparce ex, Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex and Latias ex give up 2.
- **Fastest attack:** turn one going second, if Gwynn and Ultra Ball put four Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon in your discard first; otherwise turn two.
- **What beats it:** big single hits that don't care about effects (Talonflame ex, Mega Rayquaza ex), Darkness attackers (Dhelmise, Shuppet and Banette are Weak to Darkness), and Legendary Pyroduct, which turns off Hide 'n' Sneak on Banette and Sinistcha (they are Evolution Pokémon).

## Card by card

### Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon

Hide 'n' Sneak: prevent all effects of your opponent's Pokémon's attacks and Abilities done to this Pokémon. Damage is not an effect, so attacks still hurt them; damage counters placed by an attack or Ability are an effect, so those don't.

- **Banette (Stage 1, 80 HP).** Puppet Pull (one Psychic): 80 damage, then search your deck for any card. Your early attacker and your tutor.
- **Shuppet (50 HP).** Banette's Basic.
- **Poltchageist (30 HP, Retreat 0).** Furtive Drop puts 1 damage counter on their Active for one Energy. Mostly discard fuel.
- **Sinistcha (Stage 1, 60 HP).** Matcha Spin (one Energy): if 6 or more Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon are in your discard pile, put 4 damage counters on each of your opponent's Pokémon.

### The attackers without Hide 'n' Sneak

- **Dhelmise (140 HP, Weak to Darkness, Retreat 3).** Vengeful Anchor (one Psychic): 30, plus 140 if 4 or more Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon are in your discard pile. Dhelmise is your main attacker and the Pokémon your opponent will aim at.
- **Dudunsparce ex (270 HP).** Tenacious Tail (one Energy): 60 for each of your opponent's Pokémon ex in play. Destructive Drill (three Energy): 150, not affected by effects on their Active.
- **Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex.** Blood Moon does 240 and costs one fewer Energy for each prize your opponent has taken.
- **Lillie's Clefairy ex.** Fairy Zone gives their Dragon Pokémon Psychic Weakness. Dhelmise and Banette are Psychic Pokémon, so against Dragapult every Vengeful Anchor does 340.

### Filling the discard

| Card | Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon it can discard |
|---|---|
| Gwynn | up to 2 Pokémon from your hand (without a Rule Box), and you draw 3 for each |
| Ultra Ball | 2 cards from your hand |
| Prism Tower | 2 cards from your hand, draw 1 (once a turn for each player) |
| Explorer's Guidance | whatever is in the 4 of your top 6 cards you don't keep |
| Knock Outs | every Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon they Knock Out |

Gwynn is the best card in the deck: discarding two Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon draws six.

### The rest

- **Latias ex.** Skyliner: your Basic Pokémon have no Retreat Cost. Dhelmise (Retreat 3) retreats for free.
- **Dunsparce and Dudunsparce.** Run Away Draw draws 3 and shuffles Dudunsparce back into your deck.
- **Telepathic Psychic Energy** benches two Basic Psychic Pokémon (Shuppet, Dhelmise, Clefairy ex) when attached to a Psychic Pokémon. **Legacy Energy** (provides any type) makes the Pokémon it's on give up 1 fewer prize when Knocked Out by an attack, once per game.
- **Night Stretcher** (three) brings Dhelmise and Banette back. Don't use it on Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon you need in the discard.

## The first two turns

1. **Turn one:** Telepathic Psychic Energy on a Psychic Pokémon benches Shuppet and Dhelmise. Gwynn, discarding two Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon, draws six. Ultra Ball discards two more if you have them. Count: four in the discard turns Vengeful Anchor on.
2. **Turn two:** Dhelmise attacks for 170 with one Psychic. Shuppet becomes Banette as a second attacker.
3. **Keep the bench honest.** Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon are safe from counters; Dhelmise, Clefairy ex and Dudunsparce are not. Against spread decks, bench only what attacks next.

> Going second, Dhelmise can attack on turn one: put a Psychic Energy on it, then Gwynn and Ultra Ball for four Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon. Going first, build the same discard pile on turn one and attack on turn two.

## The middle game

- **Choose the attacker by what they can't answer.** Dhelmise for 170; Banette when 80 is enough and you want the search; Dudunsparce ex when they have four or more Pokémon ex in play (240 or more).
- **Matcha Spin at six.** Four counters on every one of their Pokémon is 40 a turn to the whole board. Two spins Knock Out every Basic with 80 HP or less; it ignores Tera, which only stops damage.
- **Let Hide 'n' Sneak take the hits.** Banette Active against Dragapult or Alakazam means their extra effects do nothing; they have to beat 80 HP with plain damage.
- **Replace their Stadium.** Prism Tower discards Battle Cage, Legendary Pyroduct and Team Rocket's Watchtower when you play it.

## Closing the game

- **Single prizes win long games.** They need six Knock Outs; keep your 2-prize Pokémon (Clefairy ex, Dudunsparce ex, Ursaluna, Latias) out of play unless they are doing work.
- **Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex** costs one Energy once they have taken 4 prizes: 240 to close.
- **Boss's Orders** onto a benched Pokémon ex is often 2 prizes for one attack.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **What to Gwynn away.** Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon first; never your last Banette or Dhelmise.
2. **Dhelmise or Banette in front.** Banette when their attacks rely on effects; Dhelmise when you need 170.
3. **Whether to bench Clefairy ex.** Only against Dragons, and only when Dhelmise is about to attack.
4. **Dudunsparce ex timing.** Count their Pokémon ex in play before you commit three Energy to it; Tenacious Tail needs only one.
5. **Stadium.** Prism Tower is both a discard outlet and the answer to their Stadium; keep one for the turn they play theirs.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Built for Dragapult

```play
setup: You go second. Phantom Dive's 6 counters can't be put on Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon, and Lillie's Clefairy ex's Fairy Zone gives their Dragons Weakness to your Psychic attackers.
opp 1: Budew Active, three Dreepy benched.
you 1: Telepathic Psychic Energy on your Active Shuppet benches a Dhelmise and another Shuppet. Gwynn discards Banette and Poltchageist: draw 6. Ultra Ball discards two more Poltchageist and finds Lillie's Clefairy ex for the bench. Four Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon in your discard.
opp 2: Two Drakloak. Itchy Pollen: no Items for you next turn.
you 2: Shuppet becomes Banette. Boss's Orders a Drakloak and attack with Banette: Fairy Zone makes Puppet Pull's 80 into 160. Knocked Out, 1 prize. Puppet Pull searches for Latias ex.
opp 3: Dragapult ex. Phantom Dive does 200 to Banette (Knocked Out; that's a fifth Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon in your discard) and the 6 counters must go on Dhelmise or Clefairy ex: they choose Clefairy (60).
you 3: Dhelmise was promoted after Banette's Knock Out. Attach Psychic. Vengeful Anchor does 170, doubled by Fairy Zone to 340: Dragapult ex is Knocked Out. 3 prizes taken.
lesson: Clefairy ex turns every Psychic attack into a one-hit Knock Out on Dragapult ex, and Hide 'n' Sneak means Phantom Dive's counters can only go on the few Pokémon you choose to bench without it. Keep that number small.
```

### Tenacious Tail into Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: Every Pokémon in a Mega Rayquaza deck is a Pokémon ex. Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail does 60 for each Pokémon ex they have in play, for one Energy. Their Pyroduct turns off Hide 'n' Sneak on Banette and Sinistcha (Evolution Pokémon), but not on Shuppet and Poltchageist.
opp 2: Mega Rayquaza ex Active; Mega Kangaskhan ex, Ethan's Ho-Oh ex, Latias ex and Meowth ex benched. Storm Emerald Knocks Out your Dhelmise.
you 2: Promote Dunsparce (benched last turn) and evolve it into Dudunsparce ex. Attach Psychic. Five Pokémon ex in play: Tenacious Tail does 300 to their Active Mega Rayquaza ex (280 HP). Knocked Out, 3 prizes.
opp 3: Kangaskhan's Rapid-Fire Combo does 200 to Dudunsparce ex (70 left).
you 3: They still have four Pokémon ex in play: Tenacious Tail does 240 to Kangaskhan (60 left). Next turn a Vengeful Anchor or a second Tenacious Tail finishes it for 3 more prizes.
lesson: Against decks built only from Pokémon ex, Dudunsparce ex is your biggest attacker, and a 3-prize Mega is worth trading it for. Count their Pokémon ex in play before you attack.
```

### Matcha Spin into Alakazam

```play
setup: Powerful Hand's damage counters can't touch a Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon, so Banette in front blanks Alakazam. Their Battle Cage stops counters on Benched Pokémon from your attacks too, which would stop Matcha Spin from reaching their bench.
opp 2: Battle Cage. Abra, Abra and Dunsparce on the bench; Alakazam Active.
you 2: Banette Active: Puppet Pull does 80 to Alakazam and searches for Sinistcha.
opp 3: Powerful Hand does nothing to Banette, because its damage counters are an effect, so their Alakazam can't trade with it.
you 3: Six Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon in your discard. Play Prism Tower: Battle Cage is discarded. Retreat Banette and send up Sinistcha (evolved from a Poltchageist benched last turn). Matcha Spin puts 4 counters on each of their Pokémon: 40 on Alakazam, both Abra and Dunsparce.
you 4: If they replayed Battle Cage, play another Prism Tower first. Matcha Spin again: both Abra (50 HP) are Knocked Out, and Dunsparce (70) has 80 on it: Knocked Out. 3 prizes from one attack, 4 if the Alakazam that took Puppet Pull's 80 is still in play.
lesson: Matcha Spin is a board wipe for small evolution decks, but only after their Battle Cage is gone. Play Prism Tower on the turn you spin.
```
