Festival Lead is the format's single-prize Grass deck. Dipplin and Seaking have the Festival Lead Ability: while Festival Grounds is in play, they can use their attack twice in one turn. Dipplin's Do the Wave does 20 for each of your benched Pokémon, so a full bench is 100, twice, for one Energy. If the first attack Knocks Out their Active, the second hits whatever they bring up. Thwackey searches your deck for any card each turn, which is how the deck finds Festival Grounds again and again, and how it empties its hand for Gladion's Final Battle.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** single-prize aggro with a full bench. Two attacks a turn from turn two.
- **Wins by:** six Knock Outs, often two in a turn, on small Pokémon. Against Megas, two big turns with Gladion's Final Battle (80 more on each attack).
- **Prize profile:** everything gives up 1. Nothing in the deck has a Rule Box.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two.
- **What beats it:** removing Festival Grounds (one attack a turn), Fire Weakness (Dipplin, Thwackey and Rabsca), damage counters on a bench of 70 to 100 HP Pokémon, and Pokémon too big for 200 a turn.

## Card by card

### The attackers

- **Dipplin (80 HP, Weak to Fire).** Evolves from Applin. Festival Lead: if Festival Grounds is in play, it may use its attack twice; if the first one Knocks Out their Active, the second comes after they choose a new one. Do the Wave (one Energy): 20 for each of your benched Pokémon.
- **Seaking (110 HP, Weak to Lightning, Retreat 1).** Evolves from Goldeen. Festival Lead too. Rapid Draw (one Energy) does 60 and draws 2: twice is 120 and four cards.

### The engine

- **Thwackey (100 HP, Weak to Fire, Retreat 2).** Evolves from Grookey. Boom Boom Groove: once during your turn, if your Active Pokémon has the Festival Lead Ability, search your deck for any card. Dipplin keeps the Ability when Festival Grounds is gone, so Thwackey can always search for the next Festival Grounds.
- **Rabsca (70 HP, Weak to Fire).** Evolves from Rellor. Spherical Shield: prevent all damage and effects of their attacks done to your benched Pokémon. Phantom Dive's bench counters, a copied Trifrost and Mirage Barrage all stop at your bench. Abilities such as Cursed Blast don't.
- **Shaymin.** Flower Curtain: your benched Pokémon without a Rule Box take no damage from their attacks. A backup for Rabsca; it doesn't stop damage counters.

### Damage

- **Gladion's Final Battle** (two): you can play it only if it is the last card in your hand. This turn, attacks by your Pokémon without a Rule Box do 80 more to their Active Pokémon. With Festival Lead, that is 80 on each attack.
- **Brave Bangle** (two, Tool): 30 more to their Active Pokémon ex, on each attack.
- **Kieran**: a switch, or 30 more to their Active Pokémon ex this turn.

### Stadiums

- **Festival Grounds** (four): turns on Festival Lead. Also, Pokémon with any Energy attached, on both sides, can't be affected by Special Conditions.
- **Forest of Vitality**: Grass Pokémon can evolve into Grass Pokémon on the turn they are put into play, except on a player's first turn. Applin, Grookey and Rellor can evolve the turn you bench them.

### Everything else

**Buddy-Buddy Poffin** and **Poké Pad** (four each) fill the bench. **Ultra Ball**, **Secret Box** (discard 3 cards to search an Item, a Tool, a Supporter and a Stadium), **Night Stretcher** and **Sacred Ash** (Pokémon from your discard pile back into your deck) keep it full. **Air Balloon** (three) and **Switch** move attackers. Five Energy is enough when every attack costs one.

## The first two turns

### Turn one

1. Bench as many Basics as you can: Applin, Grookey, Rellor, Goldeen. Poffin finds two, Poké Pad one.
2. Attach Grass to the Applin you want Active next turn.
3. Going second, Lillie's Determination draws 8 while you have all 6 prizes.

### Turn two

1. Evolve: Applin into Dipplin in the Active Spot, Grookey into Thwackey, Rellor into Rabsca.
2. Boom Boom Groove finds Festival Grounds if you don't have it.
3. Count your bench before you attack. Five benched is 100 per Do the Wave.

> Festival Grounds first, every turn. Without it, Dipplin attacks once, and 100 Knocks Out little besides a Basic.

## The middle game

### Count the bench

| Benched Pokémon | One Do the Wave | Two |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 60 | 120 |
| 4 | 80 | 160 |
| 5 | 100 | 200 |
| 5, with Gladion's Final Battle | 180 | 360 |
| 5, with Gladion's and Brave Bangle, into a Pokémon ex | 210 | 420 |

### Two Knock Outs a turn

The second attack only moves to a new target if the first Knocks Out their Active. Hit something small first: Dreepy (70), Drakloak (90), Abra, Kadabra, Team Rocket's Murkrow. They choose what comes up next and will pick something big, so the second attack is often damage for next turn.

### Gladion's Final Battle

Play everything else first: attach, evolve, bench, Boom Boom Groove for what you need, use your Items. Gladion's has to be the last card in your hand. Save those turns for Megas: 360 Knocks Out a Mega Rayquaza ex or a Mega Kangaskhan ex.

### Protect the bench

Rabsca is the reason a bench of 70 HP Pokémon survives Dragapult and Slowking. It is also your opponent's first Boss's Orders target. Keep a Rellor in reserve, and Night Stretcher for the Rabsca they take.

## Closing the game

- **Six prizes is six Knock Outs.** Count which of their Pokémon you can still Knock Out two at a time.
- **Use Weakness.** N's Zoroark ex and Mega Sharpedo ex are Weak to Grass: Do the Wave does double.
- **Keep the bench full to the end.** Every Pokémon you lose is 20 less on every attack.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Dipplin or Seaking Active.** Dipplin for damage; Seaking when you need cards more than damage (120 and four cards).
2. **Gladion's now or later.** Only two in the deck. Use them on the turns that take a Mega, not a single-prize Pokémon.
3. **How full a bench against Basic Box.** Lillie's Clefairy ex's Full Moon Rondo counts benched Pokémon on both sides; yours always adds five.
4. **Festival Grounds or Forest of Vitality.** Festival Grounds almost always. Forest only when an evolution this turn wins more than a second attack.
5. **Boss's Orders targets.** The Pokémon their damage depends on: Lillie's Clefairy ex, whose Full Moon Rondo grows with your bench, and Munkidori and Dusknoir, whose counters come from Abilities that Rabsca doesn't stop.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Grass Weakness: N's Zoroark

```play
setup: You go second. N's Zoroark ex has 280 HP and is Weak to Grass. Do the Wave does 20 for each of your benched Pokémon, doubled by Weakness, and Festival Lead lets Dipplin use it twice while Festival Grounds is in play.
opp 1: N's Zorua Active; N's Zekrom and Pecharunt ex benched.
you 1: Applin A Active with a Grass Energy. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Grookey and Applin B; Poké Pad finds Rellor; bench it. Lillie's Determination draws 8.
opp 2: N's Zorua becomes N's Zoroark ex; Night Joker copies Rampaging Thunder and Knocks Out Applin A. 1 prize. You promote Applin B.
you 2: Applin B becomes Dipplin, Grookey becomes Thwackey and Rellor becomes Rabsca. Boom Boom Groove finds Festival Grounds; play it. Bench Applin C and Goldeen: four benched. Attach Grass to Dipplin. Do the Wave: 80, doubled to 160. Again: 160. N's Zoroark ex is Knocked Out, 2 prizes. They promote Pecharunt ex.
opp 3: Subjugating Chains switches in their second Zoroark, but Festival Grounds keeps any Pokémon with Energy attached from being Poisoned, so Binding Mochi never turns on. Rampaging Thunder Knocks Out Dipplin: 1 prize. You promote Applin C.
you 3: Applin C becomes Dipplin. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches two more: five benched. Do the Wave: 100, doubled to 200, twice. The second Zoroark ex is Knocked Out: 4 prizes.
lesson: Against a Grass-Weak Pokémon ex, Festival Lead turns one Energy into 320 to 400 damage. Keep Festival Grounds in play: it is your second attack, and it also stops Pecharunt ex's Poison, so Binding Mochi never adds its 40.
```

### Two Gladion's turns into Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: Mega Rayquaza ex (280 HP) and Mega Kangaskhan ex (300) give up 3 prizes each, so two Knock Outs win. With five benched, Do the Wave does 100. Gladion's Final Battle, played as the last card in your hand, adds 80 to each attack, and Brave Bangle adds 30 against a Pokémon ex: 210 per Do the Wave.
you 3: Dipplin B Active with Brave Bangle and four benched; Festival Grounds is in play. Boom Boom Groove finds Gladion's Final Battle. Attach Grass and play Buddy-Buddy Poffin for a fifth benched Pokémon; Gladion's is now the only card in your hand, so play it. Do the Wave: 210 to Mega Rayquaza ex (70 left), then 210 again. Knocked Out, 3 prizes.
opp 3: They promote Mega Kangaskhan ex, and it Knocks Out Dipplin B. 1 prize. You promote Dipplin C from your bench.
you 4: Poké Pad refills the bench to five. Boom Boom Groove finds the second Gladion's. Attach Grass and Brave Bangle to Dipplin C, then play Gladion's as your last card. Do the Wave: 210 and 210 to Kangaskhan's 300. Knocked Out, 3 more prizes. You win.
lesson: Against a deck of Megas, Festival Lead needs two big turns, not six small ones. Empty your hand with Thwackey's search and finish with Gladion's Final Battle: two Do the Waves of 210 Knock Out a Mega Rayquaza ex or a Mega Kangaskhan ex.
```

### Racing Dragapult before Dragapult ex

```play
setup: You go first against Dragapult. Two Drakloak are their whole engine, and their Dragapult ex is a turn away when you start attacking. Do the Wave with five benched is 100: enough for any Dreepy (70) or Drakloak (90).
you 1: Applin A Active with a Grass. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Grookey and Applin B; Poké Pad finds Rellor; bench it. No Supporter going first.
opp 1: Budew Active, three Dreepy benched. Itchy Pollen: you can't play Items next turn.
you 2: No Items, so Lillie's Determination does the work: 8 cards. Applin A becomes Dipplin, Grookey Thwackey and Rellor Rabsca. Boom Boom Groove finds Festival Grounds, a Stadium, so Itchy Pollen doesn't stop it. Bench Goldeen and a second Grookey from your hand: five benched. Do the Wave Knocks Out Budew; they promote a Dreepy, and the second Do the Wave Knocks it Out too. 2 prizes.
opp 2: Their other two Dreepy become Drakloak, and they bench Munkidori. Risky Ruins replaces Festival Grounds.
you 3: Dipplin keeps the Festival Lead Ability without Festival Grounds, so Boom Boom Groove still works: it finds Festival Grounds, which replaces Risky Ruins. Do the Wave Knocks Out the Active Drakloak; they promote Munkidori (110 HP), and the second Do the Wave leaves it on 10. 3 prizes.
opp 3: Their last Drakloak becomes Dragapult ex, and Phantom Dive Knocks Out Dipplin. Its 6 counters can't land on your bench while Rabsca's Spherical Shield is up. 1 prize.
lesson: Dragapult needs two Drakloak and two turns; Festival Lead takes two Knock Outs a turn from your second turn on. Spend those turns on their Basics and Stage 1s before Dragapult ex arrives, keep Rabsca up against Phantom Dive's counters, and replace Risky Ruins the turn it lands.
```
