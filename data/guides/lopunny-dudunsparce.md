Lopunny Dudunsparce is built around one condition. Mega Lopunny ex's Gale Thrust does 230 for a single Energy, but only if Lopunny moved from your bench to the Active Spot this turn. Everything else in the deck exists to get it there: Dudunsparce draws 3 from the Active Spot and then shuffles itself away, Air Balloon makes retreating free, and Fan Rotom finds the pieces on turn one. Mega Froslass ex is the second Mega, and it hits harder the more cards your opponent holds.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a one-Energy Mega that has to come off the bench every turn.
- **Wins by:** Gale Thrust. 230 Knocks Out every single-prize Pokémon and every Pokémon ex up to 230 HP in one hit. Megas take two hits, or one Resentful Refrain against a big hand.
- **Prize profile:** Mega Lopunny ex and Mega Froslass ex give up 3, Dudunsparce ex 2, everything else 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two.
- **What beats it:** Fighting Weakness (Mega Lucario), decks that can Knock Out a 330 HP Mega in one attack, and single-prize decks that trade small Pokémon into your 3-prize Lopunny.

## Card by card

### The Megas

- **Mega Lopunny ex (330 HP, Weak to Fighting, Retreat 1).** Evolves from Buneary. Gale Thrust (one Energy) does 60, plus 170 if it moved from your bench to the Active Spot this turn: 230. Spiky Hopper (two Energy) does 160 and isn't affected by effects on their Active Pokémon. It is Colorless, so any Energy pays for both.
- **Mega Froslass ex (310 HP, Weak to Metal, Retreat 1).** Evolves from Snorunt. Resentful Refrain (one Water) does 50 for each card in their hand. Absolute Snow (Water and two more) does 150 and puts their Active Pokémon to sleep.
- **Buneary (70 HP, Retreat 1).** Run Around (one Energy) switches it with one of your benched Pokémon. It is an attack, so the Pokémon it brings in can't attack that turn.
- **Snorunt (70 HP).** Froslass's Basic.

### The movement engine

- **Dudunsparce.** Evolves from Dunsparce. Run Away Draw: once during your turn, draw 3, then shuffle Dudunsparce and everything attached to it into your deck. Used from the Active Spot, it leaves the Spot empty, and the Pokémon you promote has moved from your bench this turn. That is the deck's switch.
- **Air Balloon** (two): Retreat Cost two less. On Lopunny or Dudunsparce, retreating is free and the Energy stays.
- **Fan Rotom (70 HP).** Fan Call: during your first turn only, search your deck for up to 3 Colorless Pokémon with 100 HP or less. Buneary and Dunsparce qualify. Assault Landing (one Energy) does 70, but only while a Stadium is in play.
- **Dudunsparce ex (270 HP).** Tenacious Tail (one Energy): 60 for each of their Pokémon ex in play. Destructive Drill (three Energy) does 150 and isn't affected by effects on their Active.

### Support

- **Wally's Compassion** (three): heal all damage from one Mega Evolution Pokémon ex; its Energy goes back to your hand. With one-Energy attacks, that costs you almost nothing.
- **Hilda** (four): an Evolution Pokémon and an Energy card.
- **Battle Cage** (three): no damage counters can be put on Benched Pokémon by the opponent's attacks or Abilities, on either side. Phantom Dive's bench counters, Cursed Blast on your bench and Munkidori all stop at your bench. It is also the Stadium that turns on Assault Landing.
- **Psyduck (70 HP).** Damp: Pokémon in play lose any Ability that needs them to Knock themselves Out. While Psyduck is in play, nobody can use Cursed Blast.
- **Hero's Cape**: 100 more HP. A Mega Lopunny ex with it has 430.
- **Mist Energy** (four, Colorless: their attacks' effects don't apply to the Pokémon it's on) and **Bubbly Water Energy** (three, Water: the Water Pokémon it's on can't be affected by Special Conditions). Seven Energy is enough for one-Energy attacks and free retreats. Don't pay retreat costs with it.
- **Pokégear 3.0** (three) looks at the top 7 for a Supporter. **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** (four) fits every Basic in the deck.

## The first two turns

### Turn one

1. Fan Rotom Active if you have it. Fan Call finds Buneary, Dunsparce and one more of either; it only works on your first turn.
2. Bench at least one Buneary and one Dunsparce. Next turn you need a Lopunny and a Dudunsparce.
3. Going second, Lillie's Determination draws 8. Battle Cage plus an Energy on Fan Rotom lets Assault Landing hit for 70.

### Turn two

1. Evolve Buneary into Mega Lopunny ex and Dunsparce into Dudunsparce. Attach an Energy to Lopunny, and Air Balloon if you have it.
2. Get Dudunsparce into the Active Spot (it may already be there; if not, retreat into it), use Run Away Draw, and promote Lopunny. Gale Thrust: 230.

> Gale Thrust only asks whether Lopunny moved from your bench to the Active Spot this turn. Promoting it after a Knock Out on their turn doesn't count. Promoting it after Run Away Draw on your turn does.

## The middle game

### The loop

Every turn: retreat Lopunny (free with Air Balloon) into a benched Dudunsparce, use Run Away Draw from the Active Spot, and promote the same Lopunny. It has moved from the bench this turn, so Gale Thrust does 230 again. Each loop uses one Dudunsparce and sends it back into your deck; four Dunsparce and three Dudunsparce keep it going. Always have a Dunsparce benched a turn ahead.

### What 230 does

| Target | HP | Gale Thrust |
|---|---|---|
| Slowking, Dhelmise, Alakazam, N's Zekrom | 120–140 | One hit |
| Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, Lillie's Clefairy ex, Latias ex, Fezandipiti ex | 190–210 | One hit |
| Mega Rayquaza ex, N's Zoroark ex, Talonflame ex | 280 | Two hits, or Resentful Refrain with 6 cards in their hand |
| Mega Kangaskhan ex | 300 | Two hits, or Resentful Refrain with 6 |
| Dragapult ex, Mega Excadrill ex, Mega Lucario ex | 320–340 | Two hits, or Resentful Refrain with 7 |

### Heal instead of trading

Wally's Compassion returns Lopunny's Energy to your hand, and you attach it again the same turn: a one-Energy Mega loses nothing. Most of the format can't do 330 in one attack, so a healed Lopunny makes them start their damage over.

### Froslass when their hand is big

Count their hand before you choose an attacker. Decks that draw a lot (Alakazam on purpose, Mega Rayquaza with Run Errand) often end their turn on 6 cards or more. Resentful Refrain doesn't need a trip from the bench.

## Closing the game

- **Two Mega Knock Outs** win against the Mega decks. Resentful Refrain is the finisher that needs no setup.
- **Boss's Orders** on damaged Pokémon. Two Gale Thrusts on one Mega is the usual plan; make sure the second hits the same one.
- **Hero's Cape** on the Lopunny that has to survive the last turns: 430 HP.
- **Spiky Hopper** when an effect is in the way. Crustle's Mysterious Rock Inn is an effect on their Active, and Spiky Hopper ignores it.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Lopunny or Froslass first.** Lopunny when you have the Dudunsparce for the loop; Froslass when their hand is 5 or more and you don't.
2. **Battle Cage or their Stadium.** Battle Cage against bench counters (Dragapult, Dusknoir, Munkidori). It also replaces their Stadium, so hold one for the turn they play theirs.
3. **Psyduck against Dusknoir decks.** Bench it, and expect it to be their first Boss's Orders target. That is still a good trade.
4. **Wally's Compassion or Boss's Orders.** One Supporter a turn. Take the prizes when a damaged Mega is on their bench; heal when nothing is.
5. **Energy.** Seven in the deck. Never pay a retreat cost with one if an Air Balloon or Run Away Draw can do the job.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### The Dudunsparce loop into Dhelmise

```play
setup: You go second against Dhelmise. Dhelmise (140 HP) and Banette (80) are single-prize Pokémon, and one Gale Thrust (230) Knocks Out either. Vengeful Anchor does 170 once 4 Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon are in their discard pile: a full Mega Lopunny ex survives it.
opp 1: Shuppet (50 HP) Active, Dhelmise benched, four Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon already in their discard pile.
you 1: Fan Rotom Active. Fan Call finds Buneary A, Buneary B and Dunsparce; bench all three. Lillie's Determination draws 8. Play Battle Cage and attach Mist Energy to Fan Rotom: Assault Landing does 70 to Shuppet, Knocked Out. 1 prize.
opp 2: Dhelmise comes up and Vengeful Anchor Knocks Out Fan Rotom. 1 prize. You promote Dunsparce.
you 2: Buneary A becomes Mega Lopunny ex; give it Mist Energy and Air Balloon. The Active Dunsparce becomes Dudunsparce. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches two more Dunsparce. Run Away Draw: draw 3, Dudunsparce shuffles into your deck, and you promote Lopunny, which has moved from the bench this turn. Gale Thrust does 230 to Dhelmise: Knocked Out, 2 prizes.
opp 3: Their second Dhelmise: Vengeful Anchor does 170 to Lopunny (160 left).
you 3: A benched Dunsparce becomes Dudunsparce. Wally's Compassion heals Lopunny completely and returns its Mist Energy to your hand; attach it again. Retreat Lopunny for free into Dudunsparce, Run Away Draw, promote Lopunny: Gale Thrust Knocks Out the second Dhelmise. 3 prizes.
lesson: Mega Lopunny ex needs a fresh trip from the bench every turn, and Dudunsparce gives you one: Run Away Draw from the Active Spot draws 3 and leaves the Spot empty for Lopunny. With Air Balloon on Lopunny the retreat that starts the loop is free, and Wally's Compassion costs nothing on a one-Energy attacker.
```

### Froslass closes a Mega race

```play
setup: Against Mega Rayquaza both decks are built on Megas, so each side needs two Knock Outs. Gale Thrust takes two hits to Knock Out a Mega Rayquaza ex (280 HP). Mega Froslass ex can do it in one against a big hand: Resentful Refrain does 50 for each card in it.
you 2: Run Away Draw from your Active Dudunsparce A, then promote Lopunny A (Mist Energy, Air Balloon). Gale Thrust does 230 to Mega Rayquaza ex A (50 left).
opp 2: Latias ex lets their Basics retreat for free. Rayquaza A goes to the bench and Rayquaza B does 250 to Lopunny A (80 left). Their 5 Fire and Lightning are on Rayquaza B and the rest of their bench, none on Rayquaza A.
you 3: Attach Bubbly Water Energy to your benched Mega Froslass ex. Boss's Orders brings up Rayquaza A. Retreat Lopunny A for free into Dudunsparce B, Run Away Draw, and promote Lopunny A again: Gale Thrust Knocks Out Rayquaza A. 3 prizes.
opp 3: One more attachment makes 6: Storm Emerald does 300 and Knocks Out Lopunny A. 3 prizes for them. They end their turn holding 7 cards, and you promote Mega Froslass ex.
you 4: Resentful Refrain: 7 cards is 350 to Rayquaza B. Knocked Out, 3 more prizes: you win.
lesson: Gale Thrust and Resentful Refrain cover for each other. Gale Thrust needs a trip from the bench; Resentful Refrain needs only their hand. Against a deck that draws a lot, the second Mega Knock Out can come from Froslass with no setup at all.
```

### Psyduck against Talonflame Dusknoir

```play
setup: Talonflame Dusknoir adds Dusknoir's Cursed Blast (13 damage counters, then Dusknoir is Knocked Out) to Talon Hunt (150). Psyduck's Damp turns Cursed Blast off for both players while Psyduck is in play, and Battle Cage keeps their counters off your bench.
you 2: Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Psyduck and Dunsparce C; play Battle Cage. Run Away Draw from your Active Dudunsparce, promote Lopunny A: Gale Thrust does 230 to Talonflame ex A (50 left).
opp 2: With Damp in play there is no Cursed Blast, so Psyduck is their target: Boss's Orders brings it up and Talon Hunt Knocks it Out. 1 prize. You promote Dunsparce C.
you 3: Dunsparce C becomes Dudunsparce. Run Away Draw from the Active Spot and promote Lopunny A, which has moved from the bench this turn. Gale Thrust Knocks Out Talonflame ex A: 2 prizes.
opp 3: Cursed Blast is back. Talonflame ex B's Talon Hunt (150) and 13 counters from Cursed Blast do 280 to Lopunny A (50 left); Dusknoir's self-Knock Out gives you a prize.
you 4: Wally's Compassion heals Lopunny A completely; its Energy comes back to your hand and you attach it again, ready for the next trip from the bench.
lesson: Damp makes Psyduck their first target, and every Boss's Orders and Talon Hunt spent on a 70 HP Psyduck is one not spent on a Lopunny. Even with Cursed Blast, Talon Hunt plus 13 counters is 280: a full Mega Lopunny ex survives it, and Wally's Compassion erases it.
```
