N's Zoroark won LAIC 2026–27. Its damage lives on the bench: N's Zoroark ex borrows the attacks of a benched N's Zekrom, so a Stage 1 ex with two Darkness Energy swings for 250 on turn two. Everything else in the list exists to make that attack bigger, safer or repeatable.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a fast Stage 1 ex deck. N's Zorua on turn one, N's Zoroark ex attacking on turn two.
- **Wins by:** two-prize Knock Outs with Rampaging Thunder (250), pushed to 290 by Binding Mochi on a Poisoned Zoroark and rotated between Zoroarks so the "can't attack next turn" clause never costs a turn.
- **Prize profile:** N's Zoroark ex, Pecharunt ex, Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2. N's Zorua, N's Zekrom, Munkidori and Budew give up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** your second turn, with a Zekrom on the bench and two Darkness Energy on the Zoroark.
- **What beats it:** Grass Pokémon. N's Zoroark ex is Weak to Grass, so Teal Mask Ogerpon ex and Hydrapple ex hit it for double.

## Card by card

### The attackers

- **N's Zoroark ex (280 HP, Weak to Grass, Retreat 2).** Trade: discard a card from your hand to draw 2, once a turn for each Zoroark. Night Joker costs two Darkness: pick an attack from one of your benched N's Pokémon and use it. The copied attack's own Energy cost doesn't matter; you only pay the two Darkness.
- **N's Zekrom (130 HP).** It almost never attacks itself. It is the library Night Joker reads from:
  - **Shred** does 70, and its damage isn't affected by effects on the opponent's Active Pokémon.
  - **Rampaging Thunder** does 250, and the Pokémon that used it can't attack during your next turn. Through Night Joker, that Pokémon is your Zoroark.
- **Pecharunt ex (190 HP).** Subjugating Chains switches one of your benched Darkness Pokémon (not Pecharunt ex) into the Active Spot and Poisons it, once a turn. That is your switch, and the Poison is a feature: it turns on Binding Mochi. Its attack, Irritated Outburst, does 60 for each prize your opponent has taken, a late-game backup.

### Support Pokémon

- **Munkidori.** With a Darkness Energy attached, Adrena-Brain moves up to 3 damage counters from one of your Pokémon to one of theirs. Poison puts a counter on your Zoroark between turns; Munkidori sends those, and any damage they did, back across.
- **Budew.** Itchy Pollen costs nothing and stops their Items for a turn.
- **Meowth ex** (Supporter search when benched) and **Fezandipiti ex** (draw 3 after a Knock Out; Cruel Arrow for 100 anywhere). Meowth ex is a Colorless Pokémon, so your own Team Rocket's Watchtower turns its Ability off: bench it before the Watchtower goes down.

### Trainers that make the damage

- **Binding Mochi** (Tool): if the Pokémon it's attached to is Poisoned, its attacks do 40 more to the opponent's Active Pokémon, before Weakness. Mochi plus Pecharunt's Poison turns Rampaging Thunder into 290.
- **Black Belt's Training**: 40 more damage to their Active Pokémon ex this turn, also before Weakness. With Mochi, Rampaging Thunder does 330.
- **Gravity Mountain**: every Stage 2 Pokémon in play gets 30 less HP. Dragapult ex drops to 290, exactly Rampaging Thunder plus Mochi.
- **Team Rocket's Watchtower**: Colorless Pokémon in play have no Abilities. That turns off Mega Kangaskhan ex's Run Errand, Mega Rayquaza ex's Ruler's Roar, Meowth ex, Dudunsparce and Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex's Seasoned Skill. Talonflame ex's Excited Dive works from the hand, so the Watchtower doesn't stop it.
- **N's Castle**: N's Pokémon have no Retreat Cost, both sides. Your Zoroarks and Zekroms retreat for free.

### Trainers that find things

- **N's PP Up** attaches a Basic Energy from your discard pile to a benched N's Pokémon. Trade and Ultra Ball discard cards; discarded Darkness Energy becomes N's PP Up fuel. That is how a deck with 8 Energy powers two Zoroarks by turn two.
- **Lillie's Determination** (draw 8 at 6 prizes), **Judge**, **Cyrano** (three Pokémon ex), **Secret Box** (discard 3 to find an Item, a Tool, a Supporter and a Stadium), **Special Red Card** (only when they have 3 or fewer prizes left: their hand goes to the bottom and they draw 3).

## The first two turns

The turn-two attack needs four things: a Zoroark ex in the Active Spot, two Darkness on it, a benched N's Zekrom, and a second Zorua on the bench for later.

1. **Turn one:** Buddy-Buddy Poffin for two N's Zorua. Attach a Darkness to the Active Zorua. If you have Ultra Ball, discard a Darkness with it and N's PP Up that Darkness onto the benched Zorua. Bench Pecharunt ex if you have it. Going second, Lillie's Determination at 6 prizes draws 8.
2. **Turn two:** evolve both Zorua. Use Trade on each Zoroark. Attach Darkness to the Active one (two total). Bench N's Zekrom now, not earlier: a Zekrom that sits on the bench for a turn is a target for Phantom Dive counters, Cursed Blast and snipes.
3. **Choose the attack.** Rampaging Thunder for a two-prize Knock Out or to stop a key attacker; Shred when 70 is enough, because Shred has no drawback.

> Going first, you can't attack or play a Supporter on turn one, so spend it on Zorua, Energy and N's PP Up. Your Zoroark still attacks on turn two.

## The middle game: the rotation

### Two Zoroarks, one Pecharunt

Rampaging Thunder stops that Zoroark from attacking next turn. The answer is a second powered Zoroark on the bench:

1. Zoroark A uses Rampaging Thunder.
2. Next turn, Subjugating Chains switches in Zoroark B. B is Poisoned, so its Binding Mochi adds 40. A goes to the bench, where its Poison is removed.
3. The turn after, Chains brings A back. A's restriction only covered the turn right after it attacked.

Keep Mochi on whichever Zoroark Chains will bring in next.

### Damage table

| Setup | Damage to the Active |
|---|---|
| Shred | 70 (140 against Darkness Weakness) |
| Rampaging Thunder | 250 |
| Rampaging Thunder, Poisoned with Binding Mochi | 290 |
| ...plus Black Belt's Training (Pokémon ex only) | 330 |
| ...plus Munkidori's 3 counters | +30 |

### Shred against Psychic decks

Slowking, Alakazam, Dhelmise, Banette, Munkidori and Dusknoir are Psychic Pokémon Weak to Darkness. Shred's 70 becomes 140: a Knock Out on Slowking (120), Alakazam (140), Dhelmise (140) and Banette (80), with no turn off afterwards. With Mochi, Shred does 220 to them, enough for Dusknoir (160) and Mew ex (160).

## Closing the game

- **Megas are 3 prizes.** Against Mega Rayquaza ex or Mega Kangaskhan ex decks, two Knock Outs on Megas win. Rampaging Thunder with Mochi does 290: enough for Mega Rayquaza ex (280); add Munkidori's 30 for Mega Kangaskhan ex (300).
- **Irritated Outburst** does 60 for each prize they have taken. After they take 4, Pecharunt ex hits for 240 without needing a Zekrom.
- **Special Red Card** when they are on 3 or fewer prizes and holding the cards they need.
- **Night Stretcher** brings back a Zekrom. Count your Zekroms: if both are gone, Night Joker has nothing big to copy.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **When to bench Zekrom.** The turn you need it. A Zekrom on the bench is the first thing smart opponents attack.
2. **Rampaging Thunder or Shred.** Shred if it takes the Knock Out; Rampaging Thunder when you need the 250 and have the next Zoroark ready.
3. **Which Stadium.** Watchtower against Colorless decks (Mega Rayquaza, Talonflame Dusknoir, Basic Box), Gravity Mountain against Stage 2 decks (Dragapult, Hydrapple, Alakazam, Dusknoir), N's Castle when you need free retreats.
4. **How many ex to bench.** Each Pokémon ex is 2 prizes for their Boss's Orders and snipes, and Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail does 60 for each Pokémon ex you have in play.
5. **Where Trade discards go.** Discard Darkness Energy when you have N's PP Up to bring it back; never discard your last Zekrom or Mochi.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Gravity Mountain into Dragapult

```play
setup: You go second. They open Dreepy and bench two more with Buddy-Buddy Poffin.
you 1: Buddy-Buddy Poffin for two N's Zorua (A Active, B benched). Attach Darkness to A. Ultra Ball (discarding a Darkness and a spare card) finds Pecharunt ex for the bench; N's PP Up attaches that Darkness to Zorua B. Lillie's Determination draws 8.
opp 2: Two Drakloak, Recon Directive twice. One Drakloak now holds Fire and Psychic for next turn's Dragapult ex.
you 2: Evolve both Zorua. Trade twice. Attach Darkness to Zoroark A (two Darkness) and N's PP Up a second Darkness onto Zoroark B. Bench N's Zekrom. Boss's Orders the Drakloak with Fire and Psychic, and Night Joker copies Rampaging Thunder: 250, Knocked Out. One prize, and their fastest Dragapult ex is gone. Zoroark A can't attack next turn.
opp 3: They promote their other Drakloak and evolve it into Dragapult ex, but it has only one Energy: Jet Headbutt does 70 to Zoroark A.
you 3: Play Gravity Mountain: Dragapult ex now has 290 HP. Attach Binding Mochi to Zoroark B; Subjugating Chains switches B in and Poisons it. Night Joker, Rampaging Thunder: 250 plus 40 is 290. Dragapult ex is Knocked Out: 3 prizes taken in total.
opp 4: A third Drakloak becomes Dragapult ex with Fire and Psychic. Phantom Dive does 200 to Zoroark B and 6 counters to Zekrom (70 left).
you 4: Chains brings Zoroark A back in, Poisoned. Tools don't move with the Pokémon, so attach the second Binding Mochi to A (Secret Box can find it). Rampaging Thunder plus Mochi does 290 to Dragapult ex: Knocked Out, 2 more prizes.
lesson: Gravity Mountain plus Binding Mochi makes Rampaging Thunder an exact one-hit Knock Out on Dragapult ex. Two powered Zoroarks and Pecharunt ex mean the lockout never costs you a turn, and an early Boss's Orders on the Drakloak holding Energy buys a whole turn.
```

### Shred is enough: into Slowking

```play
setup: Slowking's Seek Inspiration discards its top card and uses an attack from it if it is a Pokémon without a Rule Box. Their copy targets are Kyurem (110 to three of your Pokémon), Metagross (150) and Zeraora (210 to a benched Pokémon ex).
you 2: Zoroark ex attacks with Night Joker copying Shred: 70, doubled by Darkness Weakness to 140. Their Active Slowpoke (80) is Knocked Out. No lockout, so the same Zoroark attacks next turn.
opp 2: A copied Trifrost does 110 to your Active Zoroark and two benched Pokémon: Munkidori (110) is Knocked Out, Zekrom drops to 20.
you 3: Shred the Active Slowking (120 HP): 140, Knocked Out. Keep Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex in your hand; on the bench they are 2-prize targets for Zeraora's Thunder Raid (210).
opp 3: Trifrost again: your Zoroark ex is on 60 and Zekrom is Knocked Out.
you 4: Bench the second Zekrom from your hand. Pecharunt ex's Chains brings in a fresh Zoroark (the damaged one goes to the bench), and Shred Knocks Out the next Slowking.
lesson: Against Psychic decks Weak to Darkness, Shred is a Knock Out with no drawback. Save Rampaging Thunder for Pokémon ex, hold your second Zekrom in hand until you need it, and keep your low-HP Pokémon ex off the bench when Zeraora is in their deck.
```

### Into Hydrapple: racing the Weakness

```play
setup: This is the worst matchup (26% online). Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's Myriad Leaf Shower does 30 plus 30 for each Energy on both Active Pokémon, doubled against your Grass Weakness; with your two Darkness and their three Grass it does 360. You can't trade evenly, so you race.
you 1: Going second, Budew Active. Itchy Pollen: no Bug Catching Set, Ultra Ball or Poké Pad for them next turn, so Chikorita has a harder time becoming Meganium.
opp 2: Ogerpon Knocks Out Budew. Bayleef comes down, but no Meganium yet.
you 2: Zoroark ex with two Darkness. Gravity Mountain: Meganium will have 130 HP. Rampaging Thunder Knocks Out the Active Ogerpon ex (210): 2 prizes.
opp 3: A second Ogerpon Knocks Out your Zoroark ex (2 prizes for them).
you 3: Boss's Orders the benched Bayleef or Meganium before Wild Growth starts doubling their Energy, and Knock it Out with the next Zoroark. Every turn without Meganium is a smaller Ogerpon attack.
lesson: You will not win a long game against Grass. Your outs are a turn-one Budew, Gravity Mountain plus Boss's Orders on the Meganium line, and spending your attacks on two-prize Ogerpon ex rather than single-prize support.
```
