Greninja is a Stage 2 deck that plays like a toolbox. Greninja ex attacks for one Water: Shinobi Blade does 170 and searches your deck for any card, so every attack also finds the next piece. Around it, Robson Fortes's LAIC list carries Dusknoir for damage counters, Mega Starmie ex for 210 that ignores effects, and Mega Froslass ex for big hands. Grand Tree evolves Froakie into Greninja ex in one turn.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a Stage 2 that comes down on turn two, then attacks and searches every turn.
- **Wins by:** Shinobi Blade (170) plus Cursed Blast (13 counters) for 300-point Knock Outs, and Mirage Barrage for two Knock Outs on small Pokémon.
- **Prize profile:** Mega Starmie ex and Mega Froslass ex give up 3. Greninja ex, Meowth ex, Latias ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2. Everything else gives up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two, with Grand Tree or Rare Candy.
- **What beats it:** Psychic attackers (Greninja ex is a Fighting Pokémon, Weak to Psychic), Psyduck's Damp and Hide 'n' Sneak against Cursed Blast, and decks that use your Grand Tree better than you do.

## Card by card

### The Greninja line

- **Greninja ex (310 HP, Fighting, Weak to Psychic, Retreat 1).** Evolves from Frogadier. Shinobi Blade (one Water): 170, then search your deck for any card. Mirage Barrage (Water and two more): discard 2 Energy from it and do 120 to two of their Pokémon. It is a Tera Pokémon: on your bench it takes no damage from attacks, though damage counters still land. As a Fighting Pokémon it hits Mega Kangaskhan ex for double, and Mega Rayquaza ex, Talonflame ex, Slowking, Dhelmise and Alakazam resist it.
- **Frogadier (100 HP, Weak to Lightning, Retreat 1).** Summoning Jutsu (one Water): search your deck for up to 3 Pokémon. Aqua Edge (two Water) does 50.
- **Froakie (70 HP, Weak to Lightning, Retreat 1).** Collect (one Energy): draw a card.

### The toolbox

- **Dusknoir (160 HP) and Dusclops (90 HP).** Cursed Blast puts 13 (Dusknoir) or 5 (Dusclops) damage counters on any of their Pokémon; then your Pokémon is Knocked Out and they take a prize. **Duskull (60 HP):** Come and Get You returns up to 3 Duskull from your discard pile to your bench.
- **Mega Starmie ex (330 HP, Weak to Lightning, Retreat 2).** Evolves from Staryu. Jetting Blow (one Water): 120, plus 50 to one of their benched Pokémon. Nebula Beam (three Energy): 210, ignoring Weakness, Resistance and effects on their Active. Ignition Energy pays for it in one card.
- **Mega Froslass ex (310 HP, Weak to Metal).** Resentful Refrain (one Water): 50 for each card in their hand. Absolute Snow (Water and two more): 150, and their Active falls asleep.
- **Latias ex** (Skyliner: your Basic Pokémon have no Retreat Cost), **Meowth ex** (two; a Supporter when benched), **Fezandipiti ex** and **Budew**.

### Trainers

- **Grand Tree**: once a turn, a player may evolve one of their Basic Pokémon into a Stage 1 from their deck, then that Stage 1 into a Stage 2. Not on a player's first turn, and not on a Basic put into play this turn. It works for your opponent too.
- **Rare Candy** (two), **Hilda** (two), **Colress's Tenacity** (two: a Stadium and an Energy, so it finds Grand Tree with a Water), **Ultra Ball** (four), **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** (four) and **Poké Pad** (three).
- **Jamming Tower**: Pokémon Tools have no effect, on both sides. Binding Mochi, Brave Bangle, Hero's Cape, Air Balloon and Gravity Gemstone all stop working.
- **Special Red Card**: when they have 3 or fewer prizes left, their hand goes to the bottom of their deck and they draw 3.

### Energy

Five **Water**, three **Ignition** (three Energy on an Evolution Pokémon, discarded at the end of your turn) and one **Bubbly Water** (Water, and the Water Pokémon it's on can't be affected by Special Conditions; Greninja ex is a Fighting Pokémon, so it doesn't protect it). Ignition pays for Nebula Beam or Absolute Snow in one card.

## The first two turns

### Turn one

1. Froakie with a Water. Two Froakie if you can: Buddy-Buddy Poffin finds a Froakie and a Duskull.
2. Decide whether to play Grand Tree now (it can't be used on your first turn) or keep it. See the note below.
3. Going second, Lillie's Determination draws 8 while you have all 6 prizes.

### Turn two

1. Grand Tree or Rare Candy on a Froakie that has been in play since last turn: Greninja ex.
2. Attach the second Water. Shinobi Blade: 170, and search for what next turn needs.

> Grand Tree is a Stadium, and your opponent can use it on their turn. Against Dragapult, Alakazam or Hydrapple it builds their Stage 2 as fast as yours. Against those decks, evolve with Rare Candy and keep Grand Tree in your hand.

## The middle game

### Shinobi Blade every turn

One Water, 170, and a search. Search for next turn, not this one: Rare Candy for a Dusknoir, a Water, a Boss's Orders, Ignition for Mega Starmie ex.

### Add Cursed Blast

| Target | HP | Knocked Out by |
|---|---|---|
| Slowking (resists Fighting) | 120 | Shinobi Blade (140 after Resistance) |
| Dhelmise, Alakazam (resist Fighting) | 140 | Shinobi Blade, exactly |
| Lillie's Clefairy ex, Teal Mask Ogerpon ex | 190, 210 | Shinobi Blade plus Cursed Blast |
| N's Zoroark ex | 280 | Shinobi Blade plus Cursed Blast (300) |
| Mega Kangaskhan ex (Weak to Fighting) | 300 | Shinobi Blade alone (340) |
| Mega Rayquaza ex, Talonflame ex (resist Fighting) | 280 | Nebula Beam plus Cursed Blast (340) |
| Dragapult ex, Mega Excadrill ex | 320, 340 | Shinobi Blade, Cursed Blast and Dusclops (350) |

### Mirage Barrage for two Knock Outs

120 to two Pokémon anywhere on their side: two Drakloak, Kadabra and Abra, two Team Rocket's Murkrow, Dipplin and Thwackey. It discards two Energy from Greninja ex, so plan the Water for next turn's Shinobi Blade.

### Every Cursed Blast is a prize for them

Dusknoir Knocks itself Out. Use it when it completes a Knock Out worth 2 or 3 prizes, never to finish a single-prize Pokémon that Shinobi Blade would take anyway.

## Closing the game

- **Special Red Card** when they are on 3 or fewer prizes.
- **Mega Starmie ex** for the Pokémon that effects protect: Nebula Beam goes through Crustle's Mysterious Rock Inn and Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex's Cornerstone Stance.
- **Jetting Blow's 50 to the bench** finishes a damaged benched Pokémon while 120 hits the Active.
- **Boss's Orders** (two) for the damaged Pokémon ex on their bench.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Grand Tree or Rare Candy.** Grand Tree against Basic and Stage 1 decks; Rare Candy against Stage 2 decks, which can use Grand Tree too.
2. **What Shinobi Blade searches.** The card next turn can't do without.
3. **Mirage Barrage or Shinobi Blade.** Mirage Barrage when it takes two Knock Outs or removes their engine; Shinobi Blade otherwise.
4. **When to spend Dusknoir.** Only to complete a Knock Out worth 2 or more.
5. **Which Mega.** Mega Starmie ex against walls and Resistance; Mega Froslass ex against big hands.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### The Mega that turns on Talonflame

```play
setup: You go second against Talonflame Dusknoir. Talonflame ex comes onto their bench through Excited Dive, which needs a Mega Evolution Pokémon ex in play, and in this deck that Mega is Mega Kangaskhan ex (300 HP, Weak to Fighting). Greninja ex is a Fighting Pokémon: Shinobi Blade does 340 to Kangaskhan.
opp 1: Mega Kangaskhan ex Active and two Duskull benched. Run Errand draws 2.
you 1: Froakie A Active with a Water. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Froakie B and a Duskull. Lillie's Determination draws 8. Play Grand Tree; it can't be used on your first turn. Collect draws a card.
opp 2: Excited Dive puts Talonflame ex on their bench, and they attach to it. Grand Tree works for them too: a Duskull becomes Dusclops, then Dusknoir, and Cursed Blast Knocks Out Froakie B. 1 prize for them; Dusknoir's self-Knock Out gives you 1.
you 2: Grand Tree: Froakie A, in play since last turn, becomes Frogadier and then Greninja ex. Attach a second Water. Shinobi Blade: 170, doubled to 340, Knocks Out Kangaskhan. 3 prizes, 4 in all. Search: Rare Candy.
opp 3: With no Mega in play, Excited Dive is off: any Talonflame ex still in their hand stays there. They promote the benched Talonflame ex, and Talon Hunt does 150 to Greninja ex (160 left).
lesson: Look for the Pokémon their engine depends on. Here it is the only Mega that turns on Excited Dive, and it is Weak to Fighting: one Shinobi Blade takes 3 prizes and locks their other Talonflame ex in their hand. Notice also what your Grand Tree gave them.
```

### Shinobi Blade plus Cursed Blast into N's Zoroark

```play
setup: N's Zoroark ex has 280 HP. Shinobi Blade (170) and one Cursed Blast (13 counters) add up to 300. Every Cursed Blast gives them a prize when Dusknoir Knocks itself Out, so use it when the Knock Out it completes is worth 2.
you 3: Greninja ex A is Active with two Water. Rare Candy turns Duskull A into Dusknoir: Cursed Blast puts 13 counters on N's Zoroark ex A (150 left), and Dusknoir is Knocked Out (1 prize for them). Shinobi Blade does 170: Zoroark A is Knocked Out, 2 prizes. Search: the second Rare Candy.
opp 3: Subjugating Chains brings in Zoroark B, Poisoned; with Binding Mochi, Night Joker's copied Rampaging Thunder does 290 to Greninja ex A (20 left).
you 4: Poison has taken Zoroark B to 270. Rare Candy turns Duskull B into Dusknoir: Cursed Blast leaves it on 140 (1 prize for them). Shinobi Blade Knocks it Out: 2 more prizes.
lesson: Spend Dusknoir only on Knock Outs worth 2 or more. Shinobi Blade plus Cursed Blast is 300, enough for an N's Zoroark ex, and Shinobi Blade's own search finds the Rare Candy for the next Dusknoir. With Jamming Tower in play, Binding Mochi would have done nothing.
```

### Mirage Barrage on two Drakloak

```play
setup: You go first against Dragapult. Mirage Barrage (Water and two more) discards 2 Energy from Greninja ex and does 120 to two of their Pokémon, benched or Active. Drakloak has 90 HP, and their two Drakloak are their whole engine.
you 1: Froakie A Active with a Water; Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Froakie B and Duskull. Keep Grand Tree in your hand: it would evolve their Dreepy too. No Supporter going first.
opp 1: Dreepy A Active; Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Dreepy B and Dreepy C, and Crispin gives Dreepy B a Fire and a Psychic.
you 2: Rare Candy: Froakie A becomes Greninja ex. Attach a second Water. Shinobi Blade Knocks Out the Active Dreepy (70 HP): 1 prize. Search: a Water Energy.
opp 2: They promote Dreepy B and evolve both remaining Dreepy into Drakloak. Recon Directive twice.
you 3: Attach the third Water. Mirage Barrage: discard 2 Water, 120 to Drakloak B and Drakloak C. Both are Knocked Out: 3 prizes in all.
opp 3: No Drakloak left. They bench new Dreepy, and without Rare Candy their next Dragapult ex is two turns away.
lesson: Two Drakloak are Dragapult's whole engine and share one number: 90 HP. Mirage Barrage takes both in one attack. Against Stage 2 decks, evolve with Rare Candy, not Grand Tree, which would have turned their Dreepy into Dragapult ex too.
```
