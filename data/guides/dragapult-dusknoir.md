Dragapult Dusknoir adds a second source of damage counters to Phantom Dive. Dusknoir's Cursed Blast puts 13 damage counters on any of their Pokémon, then Dusknoir Knocks itself Out and gives them a prize. Phantom Dive's 200 plus one Cursed Blast is 330: a Dragapult ex, a Mega Rayquaza ex or a Mega Kangaskhan ex in one turn. The list builds both lines with Dawn and Hilda, and pays for Phantom Dive with one card: Neo Upper Energy on a Stage 2 provides every type of Energy, two at a time. The Phantom Dive basics are in the [Dragapult guide](guide.html?id=dragapult).

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** Dragapult with a counter-placing engine on the bench. Two Stage 2 lines, both found by Dawn.
- **Wins by:** Phantom Dive plus Cursed Blast for one-turn Knock Outs on 280 to 330 HP Pokémon, and counters that kill Basics on the bench before they evolve.
- **Prize profile:** Dragapult ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2, everything else 1. Each Cursed Blast also gives them 1.
- **Fastest attack:** Phantom Dive on your third turn, or your second with Rare Candy.
- **What beats it:** Psyduck's Damp, Battle Cage, Hide 'n' Sneak, Legendary Pyroduct (Drakloak, Dusclops and Dusknoir are all Evolution Pokémon), and Lillie's Clefairy ex.

## Card by card

### The Dusknoir line

- **Dusknoir (160 HP, Weak to Darkness).** Evolves from Dusclops. Cursed Blast: once during your turn, put 13 damage counters on one of their Pokémon, then Dusknoir is Knocked Out and they take a prize. Shadow Bind (two Psychic and one more): 150, and their Active can't retreat during their next turn.
- **Dusclops (90 HP).** The same Ability for 5 counters.
- **Duskull (60 HP, Weak to Darkness, resists Fighting).** Come and Get You (one Psychic): put up to 3 Duskull from your discard pile onto your bench. Every Duskull that Cursed Blast sends to the discard pile can come back.

### The Dragapult line

- **Dragapult ex (320 HP, no Weakness).** Phantom Dive (Fire and Psychic): 200 to their Active and 6 damage counters on their bench. Tera: on your bench it takes no damage from attacks.
- **Drakloak (90 HP)** and **Dreepy (70 HP)**: three of each, one fewer than the straight Dragapult list, because Dawn and Hilda find the line.

### Getting both lines

- **Dawn** (three): a Basic, a Stage 1 and a Stage 2 Pokémon from your deck. A whole Duskull line or a whole Dreepy line in one card.
- **Hilda** (four): an Evolution Pokémon and an Energy card. Dusknoir plus Neo Upper Energy is the common pick.
- **Rare Candy** (two), **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** and **Poké Pad** (four each), **Ultra Ball** and **Night Stretcher** (two).

### Energy

- **Telepathic Psychic Energy** (four): Psychic Energy. When you attach it from your hand to a Psychic Pokémon, you may put up to 2 Basic Psychic Pokémon from your deck onto your bench. On a Duskull, that is two more Duskull.
- **Neo Upper Energy**: on a Stage 2 Pokémon it provides every type of Energy, two at a time. On Dragapult ex it is the whole Phantom Dive cost.
- **Fire Energy** (two).

### Trainers

- **Team Rocket's Watchtower** (two): Colorless Pokémon have no Abilities. It also replaces Legendary Pyroduct and Battle Cage, the two Stadiums that shut your deck off.
- **Special Red Card** (two): when they have 3 or fewer prizes left, their hand goes to the bottom of their deck and they draw 3.
- **Budew** (two), **Fezandipiti ex**, **Boss's Orders** (three), **Lillie's Determination** (four).

## The first two turns

### Turn one

1. A Duskull with Telepathic Psychic Energy: two more Duskull onto your bench. Buddy-Buddy Poffin for Dreepy.
2. Going second, Dawn for the rest of a line, or Lillie's Determination for 8.
3. Going second with Budew Active, Itchy Pollen stops their Items on their next turn.

### Turn two

1. Evolve: Duskull into Dusclops, Dreepy into Drakloak. Recon Directive.
2. Come and Get You returns any Knocked Out Duskull.
3. Hilda for Dusknoir and Neo Upper Energy, ready for next turn.

> Don't Cursed Blast on turn two just because you can. Each one gives them a prize. Wait for the turn it completes a Knock Out worth 2 or 3.

## The middle game

### Phantom Dive plus Cursed Blast on their Active

| Their Active | HP | Phantom Dive plus |
|---|---|---|
| Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, Latias ex | 210 | Dusclops (250) |
| Mega Rayquaza ex, N's Zoroark ex, Talonflame ex | 280 | Cursed Blast (330) |
| Mega Kangaskhan ex, Dragapult ex | 300, 320 | Cursed Blast (330) |
| Hydrapple ex, Mega Excadrill ex | 330, 340 | Cursed Blast and Dusclops (380) |

### Counters on their bench

| On their bench | HP | Counters needed |
|---|---|---|
| Duskull, Abra | 60, 50 | Phantom Dive's 6 |
| Drakloak, Dusclops | 90 | Phantom Dive and Dusclops (11) |
| N's Zekrom | 130 | Cursed Blast (13) |
| Meowth ex, Lillie's Clefairy ex | 170, 190 | Phantom Dive and Cursed Blast (19) |

### Count prizes for both sides

Phantom Dive plus Cursed Blast on a Mega is 3 prizes for you and 1 for them. On a Pokémon that gives up 1, it is an even trade that cost you a Stage 2. Dusclops's 5 counters cost the same prize; use it when 50 finishes something.

### Bring the Duskull back

Come and Get You returns up to three Duskull from your discard pile, so the Dusknoir you spent are new Dusknoir two turns later.

## Closing the game

- **Cursed Blast works from the bench** on any of their Pokémon. A damaged Pokémon anywhere is a Knock Out without Boss's Orders.
- **Special Red Card** when they are on 3 or fewer prizes.
- **Watch your own prize count.** Each Cursed Blast is one of theirs. Late in the game, don't give them the last one.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **When to Cursed Blast.** When it completes a Knock Out worth 2 or more, or removes the Pokémon their deck needs.
2. **Dusknoir or Dusclops.** Dusclops when 5 counters are enough; it costs the same prize.
3. **Neo Upper Energy.** On a Dragapult ex that will attack for several turns.
4. **Watchtower timing.** Hold one for the turn after their Pyroduct or Battle Cage.
5. **What Dawn finds.** The line you're short of. Usually the Dreepy line on turn one, the Duskull line later.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Phantom Dive plus Cursed Blast into Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: You go second against Mega Rayquaza. Phantom Dive (200) and one Cursed Blast (13 counters) add up to 330: a Knock Out on Mega Rayquaza ex (280) or Mega Kangaskhan ex (300) in one turn, for 3 prizes. Each Cursed Blast gives them 1.
opp 1: Mega Kangaskhan ex Active; Mega Rayquaza ex benched with Ruler's Roar's Lightning and a Fire.
you 1: Duskull A Active. Attach Telepathic Psychic Energy to it: Duskull B and Duskull C come onto your bench. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Dreepy A and Dreepy B. Lillie's Determination draws 8.
opp 2: Storm Emerald with 3 Fire and Lightning (150) Knocks Out Duskull A. 1 prize. You promote Duskull B.
you 2: Dreepy A and B become Drakloak (Recon Directive twice) and Duskull C becomes Dusclops. Attach Telepathic Psychic Energy to Duskull B: Duskull D comes onto your bench. Come and Get You puts Duskull A back on your bench. Hilda finds Dusknoir and Neo Upper Energy for next turn.
opp 3: Storm Emerald with 4 (200) Knocks Out Duskull B. 2 prizes. You promote Drakloak A.
you 3: Drakloak A becomes Dragapult ex, and Neo Upper Energy pays for Phantom Dive. Dusclops becomes Dusknoir: Cursed Blast puts 13 counters on Mega Rayquaza ex (150 left), and Dusknoir is Knocked Out (3 prizes for them). Phantom Dive Knocks Out Rayquaza: 3 prizes. Its 6 counters go on the benched Kangaskhan (240 left).
opp 4: Kangaskhan comes up and Run Errand draws 2, but nothing they have in play has the Energy to attack yet.
you 4: Duskull D becomes Dusclops: Cursed Blast's 5 counters leave Kangaskhan on 190, and Dusclops gives them a fourth prize. Phantom Dive Knocks it Out: 3 more prizes. You win.
lesson: Count prizes for both sides. Each Cursed Blast is a prize for them, so spend it where it finishes a 3-prize Knock Out: Phantom Dive plus Cursed Blast is 330, and the 6 counters set up the next one.
```

### Running their plan back at Talonflame Dusknoir

```play
setup: Talonflame Dusknoir's damage also comes from a Duskull line, and Duskull has 60 HP: exactly Phantom Dive's 6 counters. Talonflame ex has 280 HP.
you 3: Phantom Dive does 200 to Talonflame ex (80 left), and its 6 counters Knock Out their benched Duskull A. 1 prize.
opp 3: Rare Candy turns their Duskull B into Dusknoir. Cursed Blast puts 13 counters on Dragapult ex (190 left), and Talon Hunt does 150 (40 left). Their Dusknoir's self-Knock Out gives you a prize, 2 in all.
you 4: Hilda finds Dusknoir, and your Dusclops evolves. Cursed Blast: 13 counters on Talonflame ex, which had 80 left. Knocked Out, 2 prizes, 4 in all; your Dusknoir gives them 1. Phantom Dive does 200 to the Mega Kangaskhan ex they promote (100 left), and 6 counters on Latias ex (150 left).
lesson: Run their plan back at them. Phantom Dive's counters kill a 60 HP Duskull before it becomes Dusknoir, and your own Cursed Blast finishes the Pokémon ex that Phantom Dive damaged, from the bench, with no Boss's Orders.
```

### Watchtower against Basic Box's Pyroduct

```play
setup: Basic Box runs Legendary Pyroduct: while it is in play, Evolution Pokémon have no Abilities, and Drakloak, Dusclops and Dusknoir are all Evolution Pokémon. Your Team Rocket's Watchtower replaces it and turns off Mega Kangaskhan ex's Run Errand as well. Their Lillie's Clefairy ex (190 HP) gives your Dragons Psychic Weakness with Fairy Zone.
opp 2: Legendary Pyroduct. They bench Lillie's Clefairy ex.
you 3: Team Rocket's Watchtower replaces Pyroduct: Recon Directive and Cursed Blast work again. Drakloak A becomes Dragapult ex with Neo Upper Energy. Hilda finds Dusknoir, and Dusclops evolves. Cursed Blast puts 13 counters on Lillie's Clefairy ex (60 left); Dusknoir is Knocked Out, 1 prize for them. Phantom Dive does 200 to their Active Mega Kangaskhan ex (100 left), and its 6 counters Knock Out Clefairy: 2 prizes.
opp 3: Their second Pyroduct replaces Watchtower. With no Lillie's Clefairy ex in play, your Dragapult ex has no Weakness for them to use.
lesson: Against Basic Box the Stadium decides the game: with Pyroduct down you have no Recon Directive and no Cursed Blast. Answer each Pyroduct with Watchtower, and spend the first Cursed Blast you get on Lillie's Clefairy ex.
```
