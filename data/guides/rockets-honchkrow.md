Rocket's Honchkrow turns Supporters into damage. Team Rocket's Honchkrow's Rocket Feathers discards Team Rocket Supporters from your hand for 60 each, and the deck plays twenty of them plus a dozen cards that find more. Team Rocket's Ariana draws until you hold 8 cards while every Pokémon you have is a Team Rocket's Pokémon. Five Supporters is 300, and everything you play gives up a single prize.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a single-prize deck whose damage is its hand. One Supporter played and four or five discarded, every turn.
- **Wins by:** Rocket Feathers. Four Team Rocket Supporters is 240, five is 300, six is 360.
- **Prize profile:** everything gives up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two. Team Rocket's Proton can be played on your first turn, even going first, so the Murkrow are down from the start.
- **What beats it:** hand disruption, decks that Knock Out a 130 HP Honchkrow and an 80 HP Murkrow every turn, and Cursed Blast (13 counters, exactly Honchkrow's HP).

## Card by card

### The attackers

- **Team Rocket's Honchkrow (130 HP, Darkness, Weak to Lightning, resists Fighting, Retreat 1).** Evolves from Team Rocket's Murkrow. Rocket Feathers (two Energy): discard any number of Supporter cards with "Team Rocket" in their name from your hand, 60 for each. Hammer In (Darkness and two more) does 100.
- **Team Rocket's Porygon2 (90 HP, Weak to Fighting).** Evolves from Team Rocket's Porygon. R Command (three Energy): 20 for each Team Rocket Supporter in your discard pile. Every Supporter you play or discard counts.
- **Team Rocket's Articuno (120 HP, Weak to Lightning, resists Fighting).** Repelling Veil: the effects of their attacks don't apply to your Basic Team Rocket's Pokémon. Damage still does. Dark Frost (Water and two more) does 60, plus 60 with Team Rocket's Energy attached.

### The Basics

- **Team Rocket's Murkrow (80 HP, Weak to Lightning, resists Fighting).** Deceit (one Energy) searches your deck for a Supporter. Torment (Darkness and one more): 30, and one of their Active's attacks can't be used next turn.
- **Team Rocket's Porygon (60 HP, Weak to Fighting).** Hacking (one Energy): discard a card from your hand, and they discard one from theirs.

### The Supporters

- **Team Rocket's Ariana** (four): draw until you have 5 cards, or 8 if all your Pokémon in play are Team Rocket's Pokémon.
- **Team Rocket's Archer** (four): only if one of your Team Rocket's Pokémon was Knocked Out during their last turn. Both players shuffle their hands into their decks; you draw 5, they draw 3.
- **Team Rocket's Petrel** (four): any Trainer from your deck.
- **Team Rocket's Giovanni** (four): switch your Active Team Rocket's Pokémon with a benched one; if you do, switch one of their benched Pokémon into their Active Spot.
- **Team Rocket's Proton** (four): usable on your first turn even going first. Up to 3 Basic Team Rocket's Pokémon from your deck.
- **Aarune**: up to 3 Supporter or Stadium cards from your deck. It is not a Team Rocket Supporter, so it doesn't count for Rocket Feathers.

### Finding more Supporters

- **Team Rocket's Transceiver** (four): a Team Rocket Supporter from your deck.
- **Roto-Stick** (four): look at the top 4 cards and take any Supporters there.
- **Miracle Headset**: up to 2 Supporters from your discard pile back to your hand.
- **Team Rocket's Factory** (three): once during each player's turn, if they played a Team Rocket Supporter from their hand, they may draw 2.
- **Poké Pad** (four), **Night Stretcher** (two) and **Lucky Helmet** (draw 2 when the Active Pokémon it's on is damaged by an attack).

### Energy

- **Team Rocket's Energy** (four): only on Team Rocket's Pokémon. It provides 2 Energy in any combination of Psychic and Darkness, so one attachment pays for Rocket Feathers.
- **Ignition Energy** (four): one Colorless Energy, or three on an Evolution Pokémon, and it is discarded at the end of your turn. One attachment for Rocket Feathers or R Command.

## The first two turns

### Turn one

1. Team Rocket's Proton, even going first: two Murkrow and an Articuno or Porygon.
2. Team Rocket's Factory: you played a Team Rocket Supporter, so draw 2.
3. Keep every Pokémon in play a Team Rocket's Pokémon. A single other Pokémon cuts Ariana from 8 cards to 5.

### Turn two

1. Evolve a Murkrow into Honchkrow and attach Team Rocket's Energy or Ignition.
2. Ariana draws to 8; Factory draws 2. Transceiver and Roto-Stick find more Supporters.
3. Count the Team Rocket Supporters in your hand, then attack.

> You play one Supporter a turn and discard the rest. A Supporter in your hand is 60 damage; the one you play is a card you didn't discard. Play the one that draws you the most.

### Discard only what the Knock Out needs

Supporters you keep are next turn's damage. The ones you discard aren't wasted: they count for Porygon2's R Command, and Miracle Headset brings two back.

### Archer after a Knock Out

When they Knock Out one of your Team Rocket's Pokémon, Archer is usually the right Supporter: you draw 5, they draw 3, and their deck is shuffled. Against Slowking, that shuffle also destroys the cards they stacked on top.

### Giovanni is a gust and a switch

Switch a damaged Honchkrow out and pull up their benched Pokémon with one card. It is also a Team Rocket Supporter, so it turns on Factory.

## Closing the game

- **Two Megas are six prizes.** Five Supporters per Mega.
- **Porygon2's R Command** for the last Knock Out: ten Team Rocket Supporters in your discard pile is 200, and you discard nothing.
- **Miracle Headset** returns two Supporters for one more big Rocket Feathers.
- **Giovanni** for the damaged Pokémon on their bench.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Which Supporter to play.** Ariana when you need cards, Archer after a Knock Out, Giovanni when the Knock Out you want is on their bench.
2. **How many to discard.** The table above; never more than the Knock Out needs.
3. **Never bench a Pokémon that isn't a Team Rocket's Pokémon.** Ariana's 8 depends on it.
4. **Team Rocket's Energy or Ignition.** Team Rocket's Energy stays, so put it on the Honchkrow that will attack for several turns. Ignition is for one turn: Porygon2, or a fresh Honchkrow.
5. **Lucky Helmet.** On the Active Honchkrow, so the turn they damage it you draw 2.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Five Supporters, twice: Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: You go first against Mega Rayquaza. Mega Kangaskhan ex has 300 HP and Mega Rayquaza ex 280: five Team Rocket Supporters (300) Knock Out either, and each is 3 prizes.
you 1: Team Rocket's Murkrow A Active. Team Rocket's Proton, playable on your first turn even going first, finds Murkrow B, Murkrow C and Team Rocket's Articuno; bench them. Team Rocket's Factory: you played a Team Rocket Supporter, so draw 2.
opp 1: Mega Kangaskhan ex Active; Mega Rayquaza ex benched with Ruler's Roar's Lightning, plus a Fire.
you 2: Murkrow A becomes Team Rocket's Honchkrow; attach Team Rocket's Energy. Team Rocket's Ariana: every Pokémon you have is a Team Rocket's Pokémon, so draw until you hold 8. Factory draws 2, and two Transceivers find two more Team Rocket Supporters. Five in hand: Rocket Feathers does 300 to Mega Kangaskhan ex. Knocked Out, 3 prizes.
opp 2: They promote Rayquaza and attach a Lightning. With 3 Fire and Lightning in play, Storm Emerald does 150 and Knocks Out Honchkrow A. 1 prize.
you 3: Team Rocket's Archer: one of your Team Rocket's Pokémon was Knocked Out last turn. Both hands go back into the decks; you draw 5, they draw 3. Factory draws 2. Murkrow B becomes Honchkrow B; attach Ignition Energy, three Energy on an Evolution. Roto-Stick and a Transceiver bring you to five Team Rocket Supporters: Rocket Feathers does 300 to Rayquaza. Knocked Out, 3 more prizes. You win.
lesson: Count Team Rocket Supporters, not cards: five in your hand is 300, enough for Mega Kangaskhan ex or Mega Rayquaza ex. Ariana with an all-Team Rocket board draws to 8 and Factory adds 2, and after they take a Knock Out, Archer refills your hand while it cuts theirs to 3.
```

### Archer against Slowking

```play
setup: Slowking (120 HP) is Weak to Darkness, so a single Team Rocket Supporter is enough: Rocket Feathers does 60, doubled to 120. Slowking stacks its own deck so it knows which attack it will copy, and a copied Trifrost does 110 to three of your Pokémon, enough for any Murkrow (80 HP).
opp 2: Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking stacks Kyurem on top and another card under it. Seek Inspiration copies Trifrost: 110 to Honchkrow A (20 left) and to Murkrow B and Murkrow C, both Knocked Out. 2 prizes.
you 3: Team Rocket's Archer: you draw 5, they draw 3, and their deck is shuffled, so the second card they stacked is gone. Factory draws 2. Rocket Feathers with one Team Rocket Supporter: 120 to Slowking, Knocked Out. 1 prize. Keep the other Supporters for next turn.
opp 3: Their top card is random now, so Seek Inspiration flips whatever is there, and their hand is the 3 cards Archer gave them.
lesson: Against Slowking, Archer is the best card in your deck: a draw Supporter that also shuffles away the cards they stacked and cuts their hand to 3. And one Supporter is enough for a 120 HP Slowking, so spend no more.
```

### Porygon2 finishes a Mega Excadrill

```play
setup: Mega Excadrill ex has 340 HP, so Rocket Feathers needs six Team Rocket Supporters to Knock it Out from full. Every Team Rocket Supporter in your discard pile also counts for Team Rocket's Porygon2's R Command, 20 each.
you 3: Rocket Feathers with four Supporters: 240 to Mega Excadrill ex (100 left). With the ones you've played, your discard pile now holds 8 Team Rocket Supporters.
opp 3: Maximum Drilling Knocks Out Honchkrow A. 1 prize. You promote Team Rocket's Porygon2.
you 4: Team Rocket's Archer: you draw 5, they draw 3, and Archer itself makes 9 Team Rocket Supporters in your discard pile. Attach Ignition Energy to Porygon2 (three Energy on an Evolution). R Command does 180: Excadrill is Knocked Out, 3 prizes, and every Supporter you drew is still in your hand.
lesson: Rocket Feathers and R Command use the same Supporters twice, once from your hand and again from your discard pile. Late in the game, Porygon2 Knocks Out a damaged Mega without discarding anything.
```
