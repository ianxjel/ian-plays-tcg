Basic Box has no evolutions and no single plan. Twenty Basic Pokémon, most of them Pokémon ex, each built to beat a different kind of opponent. Playing it well is mostly choosing: which attacker for this matchup, which Stadium this turn, and how many Pokémon ex you can afford to put in play.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a toolbox. Every Pokémon is a Basic, so it can attack on your first turn going second and never loses a turn to an evolution.
- **Wins by:** picking the attacker that one-hits their key Pokémon: Lillie's Clefairy ex against Dragons, Koraidon ex and Passimian against Mega Kangaskhan ex, Teal Mask Ogerpon ex against N's Zoroark ex, Raging Bolt ex against anything big.
- **Prize profile:** Mega Kangaskhan ex gives up 3; the other Pokémon ex give up 2; Chien-Pao and Passimian give up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn one going second.
- **What beats it:** decks that punish a bench full of Pokémon ex (Dhelmise's Dudunsparce ex, Slowking's copied Thunder Raid) and Grass decks that grow faster than it can answer.

## Card by card

### The engine

- **Mega Kangaskhan ex (300 HP, Weak to Fighting).** Run Errand draws 2 while it is Active. Rapid-Fire Combo costs three Energy of any type: 200, plus 50 for each heads until tails. It is your draw engine on turn one and a 200-damage attacker that fits any Energy.
- **Latias ex.** Skyliner: your Basic Pokémon have no Retreat Cost. Every Pokémon in this deck is a Basic, so with Latias in play you can draw with Kangaskhan, then retreat it for free and attack with whatever fits.
- **Meowth ex** (three copies). When played onto your bench, Last-Ditch Catch searches for a Supporter. Three copies make Crispin, Boss's Orders or Cyrano reachable most turns, at the cost of a 2-prize Pokémon on the bench each time.
- **Teal Mask Ogerpon ex** (Tera). Teal Dance attaches a Grass from your hand to itself and draws a card, once a turn per Ogerpon. It is your Energy battery as well as an attacker.

### The utility

- **Chien-Pao**: Snow Sink discards a Stadium in play when you bench it from your hand. That is your answer to Team Rocket's Watchtower, Battle Cage, Jamming Tower, Forest of Vitality and Academy at Night.
- **Area Zero Underdepths**: while you have a Tera Pokémon in play (either Ogerpon, Koraidon), you may bench up to 8. More bench means bigger Full Moon Rondo and Passimian attacks. When it leaves play, both players discard down to 5 benched.
- **Legendary Pyroduct**: Evolution Pokémon in play have no Abilities. You have no Evolution Pokémon, so it only hurts them: Drakloak, Dudunsparce, Meganium, Hydrapple ex, Kadabra, Alakazam, Dusclops, Dusknoir, N's Zoroark ex and Metang all lose their Abilities.
- **Crispin** (two Basic Energy of different types: one attached, one in hand), **Energy Switch** (move a Basic Energy between your Pokémon) and **Glass Trumpet** (with a Tera Pokémon in play, attach a Basic Energy from your discard to up to two benched Colorless Pokémon) power whichever attacker you chose.
- **Hero's Cape** gives 100 more HP. On Mega Kangaskhan ex that is 400.

## The first two turns

1. **Start Kangaskhan or Ogerpon.** Kangaskhan draws 2 a turn from the Active Spot; Ogerpon draws 1 and builds Energy.
2. **Get Latias ex down early.** Free retreat is what makes the toolbox work.
3. **Choose your Stadium on turn one or two.** Area Zero if your plan needs a big bench (Clefairy, Passimian), Pyroduct if their plan needs an Evolution's Ability. You can't have both in play.
4. **Energy for the plan, not the deck.** Crispin can find two different types: Psychic for Clefairy, Lightning and Fighting for Raging Bolt, Fighting for Koraidon. Grass goes on Ogerpon through Teal Dance from your hand.

> Each Meowth ex finds a Supporter, but each one is a 2-prize Pokémon on your bench. Count how many Pokémon ex you have in play before benching another.

## The middle game

### Choosing the attacker

Ask, in order:

1. **Is there a one-hit Knock Out on a Pokémon ex?** Take it with the attacker that gives up the fewest prizes in return.
2. **Is their key Pokémon on the bench?** Boss's Orders it, or Wellspring Mask Ogerpon ex's Torrential Pump (120 to a benched Pokémon) if the numbers work. Tera Pokémon on their bench take no damage from attacks.
3. **Can a single-prize attacker do the job?** Passimian and Chien-Pao give up 1 prize instead of 2 or 3.

### Raging Bolt's big turn

Bellowing Thunder discards Basic Energy from any of your Pokémon, 70 damage each. Teal Dance lets each Ogerpon add a Grass a turn on top of your normal attachment, so two turns of setup on two Ogerpon can bank five or six Energy. Five is 350, enough for every Pokémon in the format. It costs you the Energy, so plan the next attacker before you spend it.

### Bench size cuts both ways

Full Moon Rondo and Passimian want a big bench. Dragapult's Phantom Dive counters, Slowking's Trifrost and Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail (60 for each of your Pokémon ex) punish one. Fill the bench when your attacker needs it, not by habit.

## Closing the game

- **Three prizes from one Kangaskhan.** If they are on 3 or fewer, your Megas are their whole game. Keep only one Kangaskhan in play late.
- **Special Red Card** when they are on 3 or fewer prizes: their hand goes to the bottom of the deck and they draw 3.
- **Fezandipiti ex** draws 3 after a Knock Out and its Cruel Arrow (100 to any Pokémon) takes a last prize from a damaged benched Pokémon.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Area Zero or Pyroduct.** Bench size for your damage, or their Evolutions' Abilities turned off.
2. **Which Ogerpon gets the Energy.** Teal Mask for Grass attacks and Bellowing Thunder fuel; Wellspring only when its bench hit takes 2 prizes.
3. **Meowth ex or not.** Every Meowth ex benched is a Supporter now and 2 prizes later.
4. **When to spend Raging Bolt's Energy.** On a 3-prize Mega or a Pokémon that would otherwise win them the game.
5. **Chien-Pao timing.** Bench it the turn their Stadium matters, not as a free Basic.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Clefairy math into Dragapult

```play
setup: You go second. Fairy Zone gives their Dragon Pokémon Psychic Weakness, so Full Moon Rondo does double to them: (20 + 20 for each benched Pokémon on both sides) × 2. Against a 320 HP Dragapult ex that is a Knock Out once 7 Pokémon are benched in total.
opp 1: Dreepy Active. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches a Dreepy and Budew, and Poké Pad finds a third Dreepy.
you 1: Run Errand draws 2. Bench Latias ex, Lillie's Clefairy ex and Teal Mask Ogerpon ex (Teal Dance: a Grass and a card). Play Legendary Pyroduct: their Drakloak will have no Recon Directive. Crispin: Psychic onto Clefairy, Fighting to hand, then attach the Fighting to Clefairy as well. Retreat Kangaskhan for free. With 3 benched on each side, Full Moon Rondo does (20 + 120) × 2 = 280 to the Dragon Dreepy: Knocked Out, 1 prize.
opp 2: Budew is promoted. Two Drakloak, but no Recon Directive under Pyroduct. They bench Munkidori. Itchy Pollen: no Items for you next turn.
you 2: Boss's Orders a Drakloak; it is a Supporter, so Itchy Pollen doesn't stop it. Benched: their 3 (Drakloak, Budew, Munkidori) and your 3. Rondo does 280 to the Drakloak: Knocked Out, 2 prizes. Teal Dance again on Ogerpon.
opp 3: Their last Drakloak becomes Dragapult ex. Phantom Dive Knocks Out Clefairy (190 HP) and puts 6 counters on your bench.
you 3: Promote Ogerpon. Ultra Ball for the second Clefairy; Crispin puts a Psychic on it and a Grass in your hand for Teal Dance. Your attachment gives Ogerpon its third Grass. Myriad Leaf Shower counts the Energy on both Active Pokémon (3 and 2): 180 to Dragapult ex, 140 left.
opp 4: Phantom Dive does 200 to Ogerpon, and Munkidori moves 3 counters from Dragapult ex onto it: Knocked Out. They have taken 4 prizes, and Dragapult ex is back to 170 HP left.
you 4: Promote the second Clefairy and attach. Benched: 5 in total, so Rondo does (20 + 100) × 2 = 240 to Dragapult ex. Knocked Out: 4 prizes taken.
lesson: Clefairy is the plan, and the first one draws Dragapult's first Phantom Dive. Have the second ready to attack the turn after, keep Pyroduct in play to slow their setup, and count total benched Pokémon every turn.
```

### Weakness for the Megas: into Talonflame Dusknoir

```play
setup: Their Mega Kangaskhan ex sits Active for Run Errand and lets Talonflame ex drop in. Kangaskhan is Weak to Fighting and worth 3 prizes. Their damage is Talon Hunt (150) plus Dusknoir's Cursed Blast (13 counters).
you 1: Play Legendary Pyroduct: Dusclops and Dusknoir are Evolution Pokémon, so Cursed Blast is off while it stays in play. Bench Koraidon ex and Latias ex. Crispin: Fighting onto Koraidon, another Fighting to hand.
opp 2: Talon Hunt does 150 to your Active Kangaskhan and searches for Jamming Tower and an Ignition Energy.
you 2: Attach the Fighting to Koraidon. Energy Switch a Grass from Ogerpon onto it: Fighting, Fighting and one more pay for Impact Blow. Latias lets the damaged Kangaskhan retreat for free. Impact Blow does 200, doubled by Weakness to 400, on their Kangaskhan: Knocked Out, 3 prizes.
opp 3: Jamming Tower replaces Pyroduct. Talon Hunt does 150 to Koraidon and Cursed Blast's 13 counters finish it. They take 2 prizes and give you 1 for their Dusknoir.
you 3: Your second Pyroduct discards Jamming Tower and Cursed Blast is off again. You have taken 4 prizes; two more Knock Outs win.
lesson: Kangaskhan is their engine and 3 prizes, and Impact Blow doubles to 400 against it. Pyroduct turns off Cursed Blast, so they have to spend cards on Jamming Tower, and every Dusknoir they use is a prize for you. Orichalcum Fang's bonus needs a Knock Out by attack damage; a Cursed Blast Knock Out doesn't count.
```

### Raging Bolt into Mega Excadrill

```play
setup: Mega Excadrill ex has 340 HP and Maximum Drilling does 330 once it has 5 Energy, enough to Knock Out your Kangaskhan. You need one hit worth 340.
you 1: Two Teal Mask Ogerpon ex on the bench, each using Teal Dance (two Grass, two cards). Play Pyroduct: Metang's Metal Maker is off, so their Energy comes one attachment at a time.
you 2: Teal Dance twice more (four Grass on the bench) plus your attachment. Crispin: Lightning onto Raging Bolt ex, Fighting to hand.
you 3: Attach the Fighting to Raging Bolt: it can pay Bellowing Thunder. Their Mega Excadrill ex is Active. Discard five Basic Energy from your Pokémon (four Grass from the Ogerpons and the Lightning): 350 damage, Knocked Out, 3 prizes.
lesson: Ogerpon's Teal Dance is how Basic Box banks Energy for Raging Bolt. Pyroduct keeps their acceleration off while you build, and one Bellowing Thunder takes a 3-prize Mega.
```
