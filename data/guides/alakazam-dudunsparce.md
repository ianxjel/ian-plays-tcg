Alakazam turns your hand into damage. Powerful Hand puts 2 damage counters on their Active Pokémon for every card you hold, so the whole deck is built around drawing more than you play. The skill is restraint: every card you put on the table is 20 damage you don't do.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a single-prize Stage 2 deck that draws on every evolution and attacks for one Energy.
- **Wins by:** Powerful Hand. Eight cards in hand is 160, enough for Meganium or Dusknoir; fourteen is 280, enough for a Mega Rayquaza ex. Damage counters ignore Weakness, Resistance and effects that reduce damage.
- **Prize profile:** almost everything gives up 1. Lillie's Clefairy ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2, but Lillie's Pearl makes a Clefairy ex give up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two with Rare Candy; turn three the normal way.
- **What beats it:** hand disruption (Judge, Unfair Stamp, Special Red Card), Legendary Pyroduct (Kadabra, Alakazam and Dudunsparce are Evolution Pokémon, so their draw Abilities turn off), Darkness attackers, and Hide 'n' Sneak.

## Card by card

### The Alakazam line

- **Abra (50 HP).** Teleportation Attack (one Psychic): 10 and switch with a benched Pokémon. A free pivot for your first turns.
- **Kadabra (80 HP).** Psychic Draw: when you evolve into it from your hand, draw 2.
- **Alakazam (140 HP, Weak to Darkness, Retreat 1).** Psychic Draw: draw 3 when you evolve into it from your hand, Rare Candy included. Powerful Hand costs one Psychic: 2 damage counters on their Active for each card in your hand.

### The draw

- **Dunsparce and Dudunsparce.** Run Away Draw draws 3, then shuffles Dudunsparce and everything attached back into your deck. You can play it again later. Dunsparce's Trading Places switches it with a benched Pokémon for one Energy.
- **Dawn**: a Basic, a Stage 1 and a Stage 2 from your deck. Abra, Kadabra and Alakazam is three cards for one: plus two to your hand.
- **Hilda**: an Evolution Pokémon and an Energy card. Plus one.
- **Enriching Energy**: when you attach it from your hand, draw 4.

### Protection

- **Battle Cage** (four): no damage counters can be put on Benched Pokémon by the opponent's attacks or Abilities, on either side. Phantom Dive's 6, Cursed Blast, Munkidori and Matcha Spin all stop at your bench.
- **Shaymin.** Flower Curtain: your benched Pokémon without a Rule Box take no damage from their attacks. Battle Cage stops counters; Shaymin stops damage (Kyurem's Trifrost, Fezandipiti ex's Cruel Arrow).
- **Lillie's Pearl** (on a Lillie's Pokémon): if it is Knocked Out by an attack, they take 1 fewer prize. Your Clefairy ex gives up 1.
- **Genesect.** ACE Nullifier: while it has a Tool attached, your opponent can't play ACE SPEC cards from their hand. Unfair Stamp, Secret Box, Hero's Cape and Legacy Energy are ACE SPECs in this format's lists.
- **Mist Energy**: the Pokémon it is attached to ignores the effects of their attacks (not the damage).

### Lillie's Clefairy ex

Fairy Zone gives their Dragon Pokémon Psychic Weakness, and Full Moon Rondo (Psychic and one more) does 20 plus 20 for each benched Pokémon on both sides. It is your answer to Dragapult ex, whose 320 HP would need 16 cards in hand.

### Energy

Telepathic Psychic Energy benches two Basic Psychic Pokémon (Abra, Clefairy ex) when you attach it to a Psychic Pokémon. Wondrous Patch puts a Basic Psychic from your discard pile onto a benched Psychic Pokémon. You only need one Energy per Alakazam.

## The first two turns

1. **Turn one:** Telepathic Psychic Energy on an Abra benches two more Basic Psychic Pokémon. Buddy-Buddy Poffin for Abra or Dunsparce. Battle Cage if you have it. Dawn for the Alakazam line.
2. **Turn two:** evolve. Rare Candy into Alakazam (draw 3) on one Abra and Kadabra (draw 2) on another. Attack with Powerful Hand if the numbers work; otherwise set up Dudunsparce for next turn.
3. **Turn three onward:** one Alakazam attacks, one evolves behind it, Dudunsparce refills.

> Don't empty your hand early to build a perfect board. Two Abra, one Dunsparce and one Clefairy ex is enough bench for most games; every extra Pokémon you bench is 20 less damage.

## The middle game

- **Play cards that draw more than they cost.** Dawn (+2), evolving into Alakazam (+3 minus the Alakazam card, so +2), Kadabra (+1), Run Away Draw (+3), Enriching Energy (+3). Poffin and Poké Pad cost you a card in hand; play them only when the Pokémon they find is worth 20 damage this turn.
- **Choose targets by the table.** Boss's Orders onto a benched Pokémon your hand can Knock Out is often better than chipping at a big Active.
- **Keep Battle Cage up.** Their Stadium replaces yours, so hold a spare. Against decks with Legendary Pyroduct, replacing it with Battle Cage switches your draw Abilities back on.

## Closing the game

- **Single-prize math favors you.** They need six Knock Outs (five if a Pearl-ed Clefairy ex is one of them). You need six too, but any two Mega Knock Outs are six prizes.
- **Special Red Card** when they are on 3 or fewer prizes: their hand goes to the bottom and they draw 3. That also slows their draw for their last turns.
- **Sacred Ash** shuffles five Pokémon from your discard pile back into your deck for the long game.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Rare Candy now or Kadabra first.** Candy gets Alakazam a turn earlier; Kadabra draws 2 on the way.
2. **What to bench.** The minimum: an Alakazam line in waiting, a Dunsparce, and Clefairy ex if you face Dragons.
3. **When to Run Away Draw.** On the turn you attack, so the 3 cards count for damage.
4. **Battle Cage or keep theirs.** Theirs is gone the moment you play yours; do it the turn their Stadium hurts you.
5. **Attack or Boss.** Use Boss's Orders when a benched target dies to your hand and the Active doesn't.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games. Hand counts are at the moment of attack.

### Counting cards into Hydrapple

```play
setup: You go second. Meganium (160 HP) is their engine: it makes every Basic Grass they have provide two Energy. Powerful Hand Knocks it Out with 8 cards in hand.
opp 1: Teal Mask Ogerpon ex Active, Chikorita and Applin benched.
you 1: Abra Active. Telepathic Psychic Energy on it benches another Abra and Lillie's Clefairy ex. Battle Cage. Dawn: Abra, Kadabra and Alakazam. Teleportation Attack does 10 and switches in the second Abra.
opp 2: Chikorita becomes Bayleef. Ogerpon Knocks Out your Active Abra.
you 2: Promote the first Abra, which still has its Telepathic Psychic Energy, and Rare Candy it into Alakazam: draw 3. Bench the Abra from Dawn. Hold Boss's Orders: the Bayleef will be Meganium next turn, and that is the better target. Your hand is 9 cards: Powerful Hand does 180 to the Active Ogerpon ex, 30 short of its 210.
opp 3: Bayleef becomes Meganium. Ogerpon, now with Wild Growth, Knocks Out Alakazam.
you 3: Promote the Abra you benched last turn, Rare Candy it into Alakazam (draw 3) and attach a Psychic. Dudunsparce's Run Away Draw adds 3 more. Boss's Orders Meganium. Hand: 8 cards, 160 damage. Meganium is Knocked Out, and their Grass Energy is back to providing one each.
lesson: Damage you do to a 210 HP Ogerpon ex is wasted if it doesn't Knock it Out; damage to Meganium cuts their whole deck in half. Save Boss's Orders for the turn your hand reaches the number.
```

### Battle Cage and Clefairy into Dragapult

```play
setup: Dragapult ex has 320 HP, which would need 16 cards in hand. Phantom Dive puts 6 counters on your bench every turn, which would kill Abra (50 HP) and Kadabra (80 HP) for free.
you 1: Battle Cage. Their counters can't go on your bench while it stays in play.
opp 3: Dragapult ex. Phantom Dive does 200 to your Active Alakazam (Knocked Out), and the 6 counters do nothing.
you 3: Promote Lillie's Clefairy ex with Lillie's Pearl attached; attach Psychic (Telepathic Psychic Energy was already on it). Fairy Zone gives Dragapult ex Psychic Weakness. Benched: your 4 and their 5, so Full Moon Rondo does (20 + 180) × 2 = 400. Dragapult ex is Knocked Out: 2 prizes.
opp 4: Phantom Dive Knocks Out Clefairy ex. Lillie's Pearl: they take 1 prize instead of 2.
you 4: Your next Alakazam attacks whatever they promote, and the second Clefairy waits for the next Dragapult ex.
lesson: Battle Cage turns Phantom Dive into a 200-damage attack with no bonus. Clefairy ex with Lillie's Pearl trades 1 prize for their 2, and Fairy Zone makes it a one-hit Knock Out.
```

### Hide 'n' Sneak: when Powerful Hand does nothing

```play
setup: Dhelmise decks attack with Banette and lead with Pokémon that have Hide 'n' Sneak: they ignore all effects of your attacks, and Powerful Hand's damage counters are an effect. Only Dhelmise itself (140 HP) and their Pokémon ex have no Hide 'n' Sneak.
opp 2: Banette Active. Puppet Pull Knocks Out your Active Abra and finds a card.
you 2: Powerful Hand on Banette would do nothing. Boss's Orders their benched Dhelmise instead: 7 cards in hand is 140, Knocked Out.
opp 3: Banette again, and they have 3 Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon in their discard. At 4, Vengeful Anchor does 170.
you 3: No Boss's Orders in hand. Lillie's Clefairy ex's Full Moon Rondo does 20 plus 20 for each benched Pokémon: with 6 benched in total that is 140, and damage isn't an effect, so Banette (80 HP) is Knocked Out. That Banette goes to their discard and feeds Vengeful Anchor, which is why Knocking Out Dhelmise first was the better trade.
lesson: Against Hide 'n' Sneak, your real targets are Dhelmise and their Pokémon ex. Use Boss's Orders on them, and use damage (Full Moon Rondo, or Dudunsparce's Land Crush if you have three Energy for it) only when the Active Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon has to go.
```
