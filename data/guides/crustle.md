Crustle is a wall. Mysterious Rock Inn means attacks by Pokémon ex do nothing to it, and most of the format attacks with Pokémon ex. The rest of the deck stretches the game out: Eri and Xerosic's Machinations take their cards, Gravity Gemstone keeps their Active stuck, and Superb Scissors or Mega Kangaskhan ex take prizes slowly. It loses to everything that gets around the wall, and this guide is mostly about knowing which decks those are.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a control deck. Crustle Active, a short bench, and a Supporter every turn that takes something away from them.
- **Wins by:** prizes the slow way. Superb Scissors (120) two or three times per Pokémon ex, Mega Kangaskhan ex when a bigger hit is safe, and Spiky Energy against the attackers that can hurt Crustle.
- **Prize profile:** Crustle, Dwebble and Azurill give up 1. Mega Kangaskhan ex gives up 3, Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex 2.
- **Fastest attack:** going second, Dwebble's Ascension puts a Crustle in play on your first turn. Superb Scissors needs three Energy, usually by turn three.
- **What beats it:** Pokémon that aren't ex (Rocket's Honchkrow, Festival Lead, Slowking, Dhelmise), damage counters (Cursed Blast, Phantom Dive's bench counters, Powerful Hand), attacks that ignore effects (Mega Lopunny ex's Spiky Hopper, Mega Starmie ex's Nebula Beam), and Legendary Pyroduct.

## Card by card

### The wall

- **Crustle (150 HP, Weak to Fire, Retreat 3).** Evolves from Dwebble. Mysterious Rock Inn: prevent all damage done to it by attacks from their Pokémon ex. Superb Scissors (Grass and two more) does 120 and isn't affected by effects on their Active Pokémon. Mysterious Rock Inn is an Ability on an Evolution Pokémon, so Legendary Pyroduct turns it off.
- **Dwebble (70 HP, Weak to Fire).** Ascension (one Energy): search your deck for a card that evolves from it and evolve right away. Going second, that is a Crustle on turn one.
- **Growing Grass Energy** (four): Grass Energy, and the Grass Pokémon it's on gets 20 more HP. A Crustle with one has 170; add Hero's Cape and it has 270.

### The other attackers

- **Mega Kangaskhan ex (300 HP, Weak to Fighting, Retreat 3).** Run Errand draws 2 while it is Active. Rapid-Fire Combo (three Energy of any type) does 200 plus 50 for each heads until tails. It is a Mega, and their Pokémon ex hit it normally: it attacks only when they can't answer it.
- **Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex (210 HP, Weak to Grass).** Cornerstone Stance: no damage from attacks by Pokémon that have an Ability. Demolish (Fighting and two more) does 140, ignoring Weakness, Resistance and effects on their Active. The list's one Fighting Energy is for it.
- **Azurill (30 HP, Retreat 0).** Bouncy-Bounce Charge costs nothing: search your deck for an Energy and attach it to a benched Pokémon. A free retreat and an Energy, but also a 30 HP prize.

### Taking their cards

- **Eri** (three): they reveal their hand and you discard up to two Items from it. Pokémon Tools are Items too.
- **Xerosic's Machinations**: they discard down to 3 cards. They choose what to keep.
- **Lisia's Appeal**: switch one of their benched Basic Pokémon into the Active Spot; it is now Confused.
- **Boss's Orders** (four) and **Team Rocket's Petrel** (four, any Trainer). **Pokégear 3.0** looks at the top 7 for a Supporter.
- **Gravity Gemstone** (two): while the Pokémon it's on is Active, both Active Pokémon's Retreat Cost is one higher.

### Staying alive

- **Jumbo Ice Cream** (four): heal 80 from your Active Pokémon if it has 3 or more Energy. An attacking Crustle always does.
- **Spiky Energy** (three): when the Active Pokémon it's on is damaged by their attack, even if it is Knocked Out, put 2 damage counters on the attacker.
- **Mist Energy** (four): the effects of their attacks don't apply to the Pokémon it's on. Damage still does.
- **Hero's Cape**: 100 more HP.

### Stadiums

**Festival Grounds, Prism Tower and Lumiose City**, plus **Colress's Tenacity** to find one with an Energy. Their main job is to replace Legendary Pyroduct. Lumiose City also benches a Basic from your deck but ends your turn; Prism Tower lets each player discard 2 cards to draw 1. Festival Grounds stops Special Conditions on Pokémon with Energy, including the Confusion from your own Lisia's Appeal.

## The first two turns

### Going second

1. Dwebble Active with an Energy, Growing Grass if you have one. Bench a second Dwebble with Buddy-Buddy Poffin.
2. Lillie's Determination draws 8 while you have all 6 prizes.
3. Ascension: Crustle in play on your first turn. From now on their Pokémon ex can't damage your Active.

### Going first

No attack on turn one, so no Ascension yet. Bench a second Dwebble and attach. On turn two, either Dwebble attacks with Ascension or Hilda finds Crustle and an Energy so you can evolve by hand.

> Don't fill the bench. Against a deck of Pokémon ex, everything you bench besides Crustle is a Boss's Orders target, and often the only place they can take prizes.

## The middle game

### Ask who can hurt Crustle

Do it every turn, for every Pokémon they have.

| Their damage | Stopped by Mysterious Rock Inn? |
|---|---|
| Attacks by Pokémon ex: Phantom Dive's 200, Storm Emerald, Night Joker, Talon Hunt | Yes |
| Attacks by Pokémon that aren't ex: Rocket's Honchkrow, Dipplin, Slowking, Dhelmise, N's Zekrom | No |
| Damage counters: Phantom Dive's 6, Cursed Blast, Powerful Hand, Munkidori | No |
| Attacks that ignore effects: Spiky Hopper, Nebula Beam, Demolish, Destructive Drill | No |
| Anything while Legendary Pyroduct is in play | No |

### Trap their Active

Gravity Gemstone on Crustle makes their Active's retreat one Energy dearer, Eri takes their Switch, and Lisia's Appeal drags a Basic Pokémon ex up Confused. A Pokémon ex stuck in front of Crustle does nothing, and every turn it does nothing is a turn of Superb Scissors.

### Two plans, depending on the deck

Against Pokémon ex, play control: Eri, Xerosic's Machinations and Gravity Gemstone. Against Pokémon that aren't ex, Crustle is just a 170 or 270 HP attacker: Jumbo Ice Cream, Hero's Cape and Spiky Energy matter more than taking their cards.

### Pick Superb Scissors targets by HP

120 a turn is two hits for a 210 or 240 HP Pokémon ex, and one for a 120 HP Slowking. Damage on their bench doesn't move, so finish what you start with Boss's Orders.

## Closing the game

- **Kangaskhan for the big hit.** Rapid-Fire Combo (200 or more) ends a damaged Mega, but Kangaskhan is 3 prizes: bench it the turn it attacks, not before.
- **Boss's Orders** on damaged benched Pokémon; you run four.
- **Count their outs.** When their last Pokémon that can hurt Crustle is gone, the game is decided. Spend Boss's Orders and Lisia's Appeal on those Pokémon first.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Ascension or Hilda.** Ascension when Dwebble is Active and you have nothing better to attack with; Hilda when you need Crustle on a benched Dwebble.
2. **When to bench Mega Kangaskhan ex.** Only when its Run Errand draws or Rapid-Fire Combo matter more than giving them a 3-prize target.
3. **Eri or Xerosic's Machinations.** Eri when their Items are the problem (Switch, Ultra Ball, Binding Mochi); Xerosic's when their hand is their damage, as with Alakazam's Powerful Hand.
4. **Which Stadium to keep.** Against Pyroduct decks, hold one in hand at all times. Against everything else, Lumiose City or Prism Tower can be spent freely.
5. **Azurill.** A free Energy and a free retreat, but against Pokémon ex decks it is the easiest prize on your board.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### N's Zoroark can't touch it

```play
setup: N's Zoroark ex (280 HP, Weak to Grass) is a Pokémon ex, so Night Joker does nothing to an Active Crustle. Their outs are Boss's Orders on your other Pokémon and N's Zekrom attacking with its own Energy. Crustle A (Growing Grass, Mist and Spiky Energy, 170 HP) is Active; Crustle B and Dwebble C are benched.
you 3: Superb Scissors does 120, doubled by Weakness: 240 to N's Zoroark ex A (40 left).
opp 3: Night Joker can't hurt Crustle A, so they Boss's Orders Dwebble C and Knock it Out with Zoroark A. 1 prize. You promote Crustle A again.
you 4: Eri: you discard the Binding Mochi and Night Stretcher in their hand. Superb Scissors Knocks Out Zoroark A: 2 prizes.
opp 4: Zoroark B comes up. Night Joker does nothing to Crustle A, and N's Zekrom has no Energy of its own to attack with.
you 5: Superb Scissors: 240 to Zoroark B (40 left).
lesson: Give them nothing to Boss's Orders and N's Zoroark ex has no way to take prizes. Grass Weakness makes it a two-hit Knock Out every time, and Eri keeps Night Stretcher from bringing back the Pokémon you've removed.
```

### Pyroduct and the Stadium count

```play
setup: Mega Rayquaza runs four Legendary Pyroduct. While one is in play, Evolution Pokémon have no Abilities, so Crustle loses Mysterious Rock Inn and Storm Emerald (50 for each Fire and Lightning Energy on their Pokémon) hits it normally. Crustle A (Growing Grass and Mist, 170 HP) is Active; Crustle B (with Gravity Gemstone) and Azurill are benched.
opp 2: Pyroduct goes down. With 4 Fire and Lightning in play, Storm Emerald does 200 to Crustle A: Knocked Out, 1 prize. You promote Crustle B.
you 3: Colress's Tenacity finds Prism Tower and a Growing Grass. Prism Tower replaces Pyroduct, so Mysterious Rock Inn works again. Attach the Growing Grass to Crustle B (170 HP).
opp 3: No Pyroduct in their hand this turn: Storm Emerald does nothing to Crustle B. They Boss's Orders Azurill instead and Knock it Out. 2 prizes.
you 4: Crustle B is Active again. Lisia's Appeal brings up their benched Mega Kangaskhan ex, Confused. It can't damage Crustle B either, and Gravity Gemstone makes its retreat one Energy dearer.
lesson: Mysterious Rock Inn is only as good as your Stadium count. They have four Pyroduct; you have three Stadiums and Colress's Tenacity. Keep one in hand for the turn after each Pyroduct, and keep your bench short, so the turns they can't hurt Crustle are turns they take no prizes at all.
```

### Spiky Energy against Dhelmise

```play
setup: Dhelmise isn't a Pokémon ex, so Mysterious Rock Inn doesn't help. Vengeful Anchor does 170 once 4 Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon are in their discard pile: exactly a Crustle with Growing Grass. Crustle A has Growing Grass, Spiky and Mist Energy and Hero's Cape, so 270 HP. Dhelmise has 140.
you 3: Superb Scissors does 120 to Dhelmise A (20 left).
opp 3: Vengeful Anchor does 170 to Crustle A (100 left). Spiky Energy puts 2 damage counters on Dhelmise A: Knocked Out, 1 prize for you.
you 4: Jumbo Ice Cream heals 80 (180 left). Superb Scissors does 120 to Dhelmise B (20 left).
opp 4: Vengeful Anchor leaves Crustle A on 10, and Spiky Energy Knocks Out Dhelmise B. 2 prizes for you.
you 5: Jumbo Ice Cream again (90 left) and Superb Scissors on whatever they promote.
lesson: Against Pokémon that aren't ex, Crustle is an attacker with a lot of HP, not a wall. Hero's Cape and Growing Grass keep it above their damage, Jumbo Ice Cream undoes a hit every turn, and Spiky Energy turns each of their attacks into 20 back. Superb Scissors plus Spiky is exactly one Dhelmise.
```
