Mega Lucario is the format's Fighting deck. Its Mega is a Stage 1 that attacks on turn two, and its one-Energy attack refuels the next attacker from your discard pile. Lunatone and Solrock draw cards and chip in; four Premium Power Pro turn 270 into 300, 330 or 360. Its problem is Weakness: Dhelmise, Slowking and Alakazam are Psychic, they all resist Fighting, and they hit Lucario for double.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a fast Stage 1 Mega. Riolu on turn one, Mega Lucario ex attacking on turn two, a second Lucario charged from the discard pile behind it.
- **Wins by:** two Knock Outs on Megas, or three on two-prize Pokémon ex. Mega Brave (270) plus Premium Power Pro takes most big Pokémon in one hit.
- **Prize profile:** Mega Lucario ex gives up 3. Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex, Dudunsparce ex and Meowth ex give up 2. Riolu, Lunatone, Solrock, Makuhita, Hariyama and Dunsparce give up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two, going first or second.
- **What beats it:** Psychic attackers (Lucario is Weak to Psychic), Pokémon that resist Fighting, and single-prize decks: you need six Knock Outs, they need two on Megas.

## Card by card

### The attackers

- **Mega Lucario ex (340 HP, Weak to Psychic, Retreat 2).** Evolves from Riolu. Aura Jab (one Fighting) does 130 and attaches up to 3 Basic Fighting Energy from your discard pile to your benched Pokémon, split any way. Mega Brave (two Fighting) does 270, and that Lucario can't use Mega Brave during your next turn. It is a Mega: 3 prizes.
- **Hariyama (150 HP, Weak to Psychic, Retreat 3).** Evolves from Makuhita. Heave-Ho Catcher: when you play it from your hand to evolve, you may switch one of their benched Pokémon into the Active Spot. Wild Press (three Fighting) does 210 and 70 to Hariyama itself.
- **Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex (210 HP, Weak to Grass).** Cornerstone Stance: it takes no damage from attacks by Pokémon that have an Ability. Demolish (Fighting and two more) does 140 and ignores Weakness, Resistance and effects on their Active Pokémon.
- **Dudunsparce ex (270 HP).** Evolves from Dunsparce. Tenacious Tail (one Energy): 60 for each of their Pokémon ex in play.
- **Riolu (80 HP, Weak to Psychic, Retreat 2).** Accelerating Stab (one Fighting) does 30 and can't be used two turns running. At 80 HP it is too big for Buddy-Buddy Poffin, which is why the list has none.

### The engine

- **Lunatone (110 HP, Weak to Grass).** Lunar Cycle: once a turn, if Solrock is in play, discard a Basic Fighting Energy from your hand to draw 3. Only one Lunar Cycle a turn, however many Lunatone you have. Every Energy it discards is fuel for the next Aura Jab.
- **Solrock (110 HP, Weak to Grass).** Cosmic Beam (one Fighting) does 70 and ignores Weakness and Resistance, but only with Lunatone on your bench. With Premium Power Pro it is a single-prize attacker for 100 or 130.
- **Fighting Gong** finds a Basic Fighting Energy or a Basic Fighting Pokémon: Riolu, Makuhita, Lunatone or Solrock. **Poké Pad** finds any Pokémon without a Rule Box.
- **Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking** puts two cards of your choice on top of your deck. Play it, then Lunar Cycle: you draw both, plus one.
- **Meowth ex** finds a Supporter when you bench it.

### Support

- **Premium Power Pro** (four): your Fighting Pokémon's attacks do 30 more to their Active this turn, before Weakness and Resistance. Copies stack.
- **Wally's Compassion** heals all damage from one Mega Evolution Pokémon ex; its Energy goes back to your hand.
- **Air Balloon** (two): Retreat Cost two less, so Lucario retreats for free. **Switch** sends Lucario to the bench and a retreat brings it back in the same turn (see Mega Brave's lock, below).
- **Gravity Mountain** (two): every Stage 2 has 30 less HP. Dragapult ex, Hydrapple ex, Greninja ex and Talonflame ex all drop into Mega Brave range.
- **Team Rocket's Watchtower**: Colorless Pokémon have no Abilities. That turns off Mega Kangaskhan ex, Mega Rayquaza ex, Dudunsparce and Fan Rotom, and your own Meowth ex.
- **Rocky Fighting Energy** (two): the effects of their attacks don't apply to the Fighting Pokémon it is attached to. Damage still does. It stops Absolute Snow's Asleep and Yveltal's Clutch.

## The first two turns

### Going first

1. Riolu Active. Bench Lunatone, Solrock and a second Riolu with Fighting Gong, Poké Pad and Ultra Ball.
2. Attach Fighting to the Riolu that will evolve first. If you can only spare one Energy, put it on a benched Riolu: the Active one is the likeliest to be Knocked Out.
3. Use Lunar Cycle on turn one. The Fighting you discard is the first Aura Jab's fuel.
4. Turn two: evolve and attach. With two Fighting, Mega Brave is ready, but Aura Jab is often better: it powers the second Lucario.

### Going second

Same build, plus a Supporter: Lillie's Determination draws 8 while you still have all 6 prizes. Riolu can attack on turn one (Accelerating Stab, 30), which matters most against Mega Kangaskhan ex (Weak to Fighting, so 60).

> If Lunatone and Solrock aren't both in play, Lunar Cycle is off. Fighting Gong finds either; bench them as a pair.

## The middle game

### The rhythm: Aura Jab, then Mega Brave

Aura Jab is your turn-two attack and your Energy engine. Every Fighting that Lunar Cycle, an Ultra Ball or a Crushing Hammer puts in your discard pile comes back onto your bench. Use it whenever 130 (or 160 with one Premium Power Pro) takes the Knock Out. Save Mega Brave for the Pokémon ex that only 270 or more can reach.

### Count Premium Power Pro before you attack

|---|---|---|
| Drakloak, Metang | 90, 100 | Aura Jab |

### Mega Brave's lock is on the Pokémon

"Can't use Mega Brave during your next turn" is an effect on that Lucario, and moving it to the bench removes it. Two Lucario alternate naturally. One Lucario can also Mega Brave two turns running: Switch it to the bench, then retreat the new Active back into it; an Air Balloon on that Pokémon makes the retreat free.

### Where Aura Jab's Energy goes

The next Lucario first (two Fighting is a Mega Brave). Then Hariyama, which needs three for Wild Press, or Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex, which needs three for Demolish. Solrock needs one.

## Closing the game

- **Two Knock Outs on Megas are six prizes.** Against Mega Rayquaza and Basic Box, that is the whole game.
- **Hariyama is your third gust.** Two Boss's Orders are not many; Heave-Ho Catcher pulls up a benched Pokémon the turn Hariyama evolves.
- **Wally's Compassion** resets a damaged Lucario, but its Energy comes back to your hand, so it attacks next turn with one attachment: Aura Jab, not Mega Brave.
- **Unfair Stamp** after they Knock Out a Lucario: you draw 5, they draw 2.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Aura Jab or Mega Brave.** Aura Jab when 130 or 160 is enough; Mega Brave only when nothing smaller takes the Knock Out.
2. **Gravity Mountain or Watchtower.** One Stadium at a time. Gravity Mountain against Stage 2 decks (Dragapult, Hydrapple, Greninja, Talonflame); Watchtower against Colorless engines (Mega Rayquaza's Ruler's Roar, Dudunsparce).
3. **Watchtower or Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex.** Ogerpon's wall only works against Pokémon that have an Ability. Your own Watchtower takes the Abilities away from Mega Kangaskhan ex and Mega Rayquaza ex, and then they hit Ogerpon normally. Pick one plan.
4. **Lunar Cycle after Lillie's Determination or Judge.** Both shuffle your hand away; Lunar Cycle doesn't. Draw with the Supporter first, then use Lunar Cycle.
5. **How many Pokémon to bench against Psychic decks.** Their attacks double against your Lucario and Hariyama. Make them spend attacks on Riolu, Lunatone and Solrock first.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Aura Jab refuels into Dragapult

```play
setup: You go first against Dragapult. Riolu A is Active; Solrock and Fighting Gong are in your hand.
you 1: Fighting Gong finds Lunatone. Bench Lunatone, Solrock and Riolu B (Poké Pad). Attach Fighting to Riolu A. Lunar Cycle: discard a Fighting from your hand, draw 3. No Supporter going first.
opp 1: Dreepy Active; Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches two more Dreepy, and Crispin puts Psychic on Dreepy C.
you 2: Lillie's Determination draws 8 while you have all 6 prizes. Riolu A becomes Mega Lucario ex; attach a second Fighting. Lunar Cycle again: two Fighting in your discard pile. Aura Jab Knocks Out the Active Dreepy (70 HP) and puts both Fighting on Riolu B. 1 prize.
opp 2: They promote Dreepy B and evolve it and Dreepy C into Drakloak, then attach Fire to Drakloak C. Crushing Hammer: heads, one Fighting comes off Lucario A.
you 3: Riolu B (two Fighting) becomes Mega Lucario ex B. Boss's Orders the benched Drakloak C, the one holding Fire and Psychic. Lucario A has one Fighting left: Aura Jab does 130 to a 90 HP Drakloak, Knocked Out. 2 prizes. The Hammered Fighting and this turn's Lunar Cycle discard go onto Lucario B and Solrock.
opp 3: Drakloak B becomes Dragapult ex; Crispin pays for Phantom Dive: 200 to Lucario A (140 left) and 6 counters on Solrock (50 left).
you 4: Play Gravity Mountain: Dragapult ex, a Stage 2, now has 290 HP. Attach Fighting to Lucario A (two again). Premium Power Pro: Mega Brave does 300. Knocked Out, 4 prizes, and they have no Drakloak left.
lesson: Aura Jab is both your opener and your Energy engine: every Fighting that Lunar Cycle or a Hammer puts in the discard pile comes back on the next Lucario. Save Mega Brave for the Pokémon ex, and let Gravity Mountain make 300 enough for Dragapult ex.
```

### Two Megas, two attacks: Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: You go second. They open Mega Kangaskhan ex Active (300 HP, Weak to Fighting) with Mega Rayquaza ex (280 HP) benched. Ruler's Roar attached a Lightning and they attached a Fire: 2 Fire and Lightning in play.
you 1: Riolu A Active. Fighting Gong finds Riolu B and Poké Pad finds Solrock; bench them with Lunatone. Attach Fighting to the benched Riolu B, not the Active one. Lunar Cycle draws 3. Lillie's Determination draws 8.
opp 2: Run Errand draws 2. They bench Latias ex, attach Lightning to Rayquaza (3), retreat Kangaskhan for free and Storm Emerald (150) Knocks Out Riolu A. 1 prize.
you 2: Promote Riolu B and evolve it into Mega Lucario ex; attach a second Fighting. Fighting Gong benches Riolu C. Lunar Cycle draws 3. Boss's Orders brings up Kangaskhan. Premium Power Pro, then Aura Jab: 160, doubled by Weakness to 320. Knocked Out, 3 prizes. The two Fighting in your discard pile go onto Riolu C.
opp 3: They promote Rayquaza, attach a fourth Fire or Lightning and bench a second Rayquaza (Ruler's Roar: 5). Storm Emerald does 250 to Lucario B (90 left).
you 3: Lucario B used Aura Jab last turn, so Mega Brave is open. Premium Power Pro: 300 to Rayquaza. Knocked Out, 3 more prizes. You win.
lesson: Against a deck of Megas you need two attacks. Fighting Weakness makes Kangaskhan the cheap one: Aura Jab plus one Premium Power Pro is 320. Mega Brave plus one is 300, enough for Rayquaza. Their Storm Emerald needs 7 Fire and Lightning to Knock Out a full Lucario in one hit; make them get there.
```

### Heave-Ho Catcher on N's Zekrom

```play
setup: You go second. N's Zoroark ex (280 HP) uses Night Joker (two Darkness) to copy an attack from a benched N's Pokémon. N's Zekrom (130 HP) on their bench is the source of Rampaging Thunder, 250 damage, after which that Zoroark can't attack next turn.
opp 1: N's Zorua Active; N's Zekrom and Pecharunt ex benched.
you 1: Riolu A Active with a Fighting. Bench Riolu B, Makuhita, Lunatone and Solrock. Lunar Cycle draws 3. Lillie's Determination draws 8.
opp 2: N's Zorua becomes N's Zoroark ex; Night Joker copies Rampaging Thunder and Knocks Out Riolu A. 1 prize.
you 2: Lillie's Determination again: you still have all 6 prizes, so 8 cards. Promote Riolu B, evolve it and attach a Fighting. Evolve Makuhita into Hariyama from your hand: Heave-Ho Catcher switches N's Zekrom into their Active Spot. Premium Power Pro, then Aura Jab: 160, Zekrom Knocked Out. 1 prize. The two Fighting in your discard pile go onto Hariyama.
opp 3: Heave-Ho Catcher sent their Zoroark to the bench, and going to the bench cleared its "can't attack next turn". They promote it, bench their second Zekrom, and Rampaging Thunder does 250 to Lucario B (90 left).
you 3: Attach Fighting to Lucario B. Premium Power Pro: Mega Brave does 300 to Zoroark ex. Knocked Out, 3 prizes in total, and both your Boss's Orders are still in the deck.
lesson: Hariyama is a Boss's Orders that leaves your Supporter free. Pull up the Pokémon their damage depends on and keep the real Boss's Orders for the last prizes. But any gust moves their Active to the bench, and that clears its "can't attack next turn".
```
