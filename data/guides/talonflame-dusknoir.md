Talonflame Dusknoir finished second at LAIC with five Energy in the deck. It works because Ignition Energy provides three Energy on an Evolution Pokémon, so Talonflame ex attacks every turn off a single card, and its attack searches for the next one. Dusknoir adds 13 damage counters to whatever needs them.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a combo deck. Talonflame ex hits for 150 and fetches two cards; Dusknoir's Cursed Blast adds 130 anywhere.
- **Wins by:** adding the two together. 150 plus 130 is 280; add Black Belt's Training (40 more against a Pokémon ex) and it is 320.
- **Prize profile:** Talonflame ex, Latias ex, Meowth ex, Fezandipiti ex and Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex give up 2; Mega Kangaskhan ex gives up 3; the Dusk line gives up 1. Every Cursed Blast also gives up 1, because Dusknoir is Knocked Out using it.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two, Talonflame ex from your hand with an Ignition Energy.
- **What beats it:** Legendary Pyroduct (Dusclops and Dusknoir are Evolution Pokémon, so Cursed Blast turns off), Team Rocket's Watchtower (Colorless Pokémon lose their Abilities), Battle Cage (no counters on the bench), and N's Zoroark ex.

## Card by card

### The attacker

- **Talonflame ex (280 HP, Weak to Lightning, resists Fighting, Retreat 0).** You never evolve it. Excited Dive moves it from your hand to your bench while you have a Mega Evolution Pokémon ex in play. Talon Hunt costs two Energy of any type: 150 damage, then search your deck for any 2 cards.
- **Ignition Energy.** Provides one Colorless Energy, or three if it is attached to an Evolution Pokémon, and is discarded at the end of your turn. On Talonflame ex (a Stage 2) one Ignition pays for Talon Hunt.
- **Neo Upper Energy.** On a Stage 2 Pokémon it provides every type of Energy, two at a time, and it stays. Talonflame ex with Neo Upper attacks every turn without needing an Ignition.

### The combo

- **Dusknoir (160 HP, Weak to Darkness).** Cursed Blast: once a turn, put 13 damage counters on any of your opponent's Pokémon, then Dusknoir is Knocked Out (your opponent takes a prize for it). Shadow Bind (Psychic, Psychic and one more) does 150 and stops the Defending Pokémon from retreating; Neo Upper plus an Ignition pays for it.
- **Dusclops (90 HP).** The same Ability for 5 counters. Use it when 50 finishes something.
- **Duskull.** The Basic. Rare Candy takes it straight to Dusknoir.

### The engine

- **Mega Kangaskhan ex (300 HP).** Run Errand draws 2 a turn while it is Active, and it is the Mega that turns on Excited Dive. With Ignition providing only one Energy on a Basic Pokémon, it rarely attacks in this deck.
- **Latias ex.** Skyliner: your Basic Pokémon have no Retreat Cost. Kangaskhan draws from the Active Spot, then retreats for free so Talonflame can attack.
- **Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex.** Blood Moon does 240 and costs one fewer Energy for each prize your opponent has taken. Once they have taken 4, it costs one Energy, which an Ignition pays (on a Basic, Ignition provides one).

### Trainers

- **Hilda** (an Evolution Pokémon and an Energy card: Dusknoir or Talonflame ex, plus Ignition), **Dawn** (Duskull, Dusclops and Dusknoir, or a Stage 2 Talonflame ex), **Dusk Ball** (look at the bottom 7 cards of your deck for a Pokémon), **Rare Candy**.
- **Black Belt's Training**: 40 more damage to their Active Pokémon ex this turn.
- **Jamming Tower**: Pokémon Tools have no effect. Binding Mochi, Lillie's Pearl, Hero's Cape and Lucky Helmet stop working; it also replaces their Stadium.
- **Special Red Card** (two): only when they have 3 or fewer prizes left. Their hand goes to the bottom of their deck and they draw 3.

## The damage menu

| Combination | Total | Knocks Out |
|---|---|---|
| Cursed Blast alone | 130 | Slowking, N's Zekrom, Drakloak, Metang, Munkidori, Kadabra |
| Talon Hunt alone | 150 | Alakazam, Dhelmise, Banette |
| Talon Hunt + Dusclops | 200 | Meowth ex, Lillie's Clefairy ex, Pecharunt ex |
| Talon Hunt + Cursed Blast | 280 | Talonflame ex, Mega Rayquaza ex, N's Zoroark ex |
| Talon Hunt + Cursed Blast + Black Belt's Training | 320 | Dragapult ex, Mega Kangaskhan ex |
| Talon Hunt + Cursed Blast + Dusclops + Black Belt's Training | 370 | Mega Excadrill ex |

Counters from Cursed Blast land on benched Pokémon too, including Tera Pokémon (Tera stops damage from attacks, not counters).

## The first two turns

1. **Turn one:** Kangaskhan Active, Run Errand. Duskull on the bench (Poffin or Dusk Ball). Latias ex if you have it. Talonflame ex from your hand onto the bench with Excited Dive.
2. **Turn two:** Hilda for Ignition and Dusknoir, Rare Candy on Duskull. Ignition on Talonflame, retreat Kangaskhan for free, Talon Hunt. Search for next turn's Ignition and whatever the next combo needs.
3. **Every turn after:** Talon Hunt finds the next Ignition. Neo Upper on a Talonflame frees up both searches.

> Ignition is discarded at the end of your turn whether or not you attacked. Attach it only on the turn you are attacking with the Pokémon it's on.

## The middle game

- **Cursed Blast costs you a prize.** Use it when it takes at least as much as it gives: a 2-prize Knock Out, a Knock Out on an engine piece (Drakloak, Meganium after damage, N's Zekrom, Metang), or a Dusknoir that would be Knocked Out next turn anyway.
- **Blast before you attack.** Put the counters on first, then let Talon Hunt finish. If the target survives the counters for some reason, you still choose the attack.
- **Rebuild with Talon Hunt.** Its two searches are your whole engine: next turn's Ignition plus Rare Candy, Boss's Orders, Black Belt's Training or a Dusknoir.
- **Protect Kangaskhan.** It is 3 prizes and doesn't attack. Keep it Active only while Run Errand matters; with Latias out, retreat it as soon as it has drawn.

## Closing the game

- **Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex** once they have taken 4 prizes: 240 for one Ignition.
- **Special Red Card** before the turn they need a specific card to win.
- **Boss's Orders plus Cursed Blast** for the last 2 prizes on a benched Pokémon ex.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Dusclops or Dusknoir.** Rare Candy straight to Dusknoir for 13 counters; keep a Dusclops when 5 counters will finish something.
2. **Which Stadium is in play.** Jamming Tower whenever their Stadium (Pyroduct, Watchtower, Battle Cage) hurts you or their Tool helps them.
3. **Black Belt's Training or Lillie's Determination.** Black Belt's when it turns a 280 into a 320 Knock Out; otherwise draw.
4. **Where Neo Upper goes.** On a Talonflame ex that will attack for several turns, or on a Dusknoir that needs to use Shadow Bind.
5. **How many Pokémon ex to bench.** Against Dhelmise, Dudunsparce ex does 60 for each one.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### One Ignition a turn into Slowking

```play
setup: You go first. Mega Kangaskhan ex Active with Duskull and Talonflame ex in hand.
you 1: Run Errand draws 2. Bench Duskull and Latias ex. Excited Dive moves Talonflame ex from your hand to your bench. No attack or Supporter on the first turn.
opp 1: Slowpoke Active; Telepathic Psychic Energy benches two more Slowpoke.
you 2: Hilda: Dusknoir and an Ignition. Rare Candy turns Duskull into Dusknoir. Ignition on Talonflame (three Energy on a Stage 2). Retreat Kangaskhan for free and Talon Hunt the Active Slowpoke for 150: Knocked Out, 1 prize. Search: another Ignition and Boss's Orders.
opp 2: A Slowking flips Kyurem: Trifrost does 110 each to Talonflame ex (170 left), Dusknoir (50 left) and Latias ex (100 left).
you 3: Ignition on Talonflame. Dusknoir would be Knocked Out by the next Trifrost anyway, so Cursed Blast now: 13 counters Knock Out their benched Slowking (they take 1 prize for Dusknoir). Talon Hunt Knocks Out the Active Slowking. 3 prizes taken, and Talon Hunt finds the next Ignition and a Duskull.
lesson: Every Talon Hunt pays for the next one. Spend Cursed Blast when your Dusknoir is about to fall anyway, or when it takes something that matters.
```

### 320 into Dragapult

```play
setup: Dragapult ex has 320 HP. Talon Hunt (150), Cursed Blast (130) and Black Belt's Training (40 against an Active Pokémon ex) add up to exactly 320.
opp 3: Dragapult ex Phantom Dives your Active Kangaskhan for 200 and puts 6 counters on your benched Duskull.
you 3: Retreat Kangaskhan for free (Latias), send up Talonflame ex and attach Ignition. Black Belt's Training is your Supporter. Cursed Blast first: 13 counters on Dragapult ex. Then Talon Hunt: 150 plus 40 is 190, and 130 was already on it. Dragapult ex is Knocked Out: 2 prizes for you, 1 for them (Dusknoir).
opp 4: Their next Drakloak evolves; Phantom Dive does 200 to Talonflame ex (80 left).
you 4: Talon Hunt found a Dusknoir and Rare Candy last turn. Same line if you have Black Belt's Training again; if not, Boss's Orders a benched Drakloak and Talon Hunt it, and keep the Dusknoir for the next Dragapult ex.
lesson: Against a 320 HP Pokémon, order matters: Cursed Blast, then Black Belt's Training, then Talon Hunt. And protect your Duskull line on the bench, because Phantom Dive's counters are aimed at it.
```

### Jamming Tower into N's Zoroark

```play
setup: N's Zoroark ex's copied Rampaging Thunder does 250; Binding Mochi on a Poisoned Zoroark makes it 290, enough for Talonflame ex. Their Team Rocket's Watchtower turns off Kangaskhan's Run Errand and Meowth ex (both Colorless). Excited Dive still works, because it is used from your hand.
opp 2: Watchtower. Rampaging Thunder with Mochi (290) plus Munkidori's 3 counters Knocks Out your Active Kangaskhan: 3 prizes.
you 2: Play Jamming Tower: Watchtower is discarded, and Binding Mochi has no effect while Jamming Tower is in play. Talonflame ex with Ignition. Cursed Blast puts 13 counters on the Active Zoroark (they take 1 prize for Dusknoir), then Talon Hunt does 150: 280, Knocked Out. 2 prizes.
opp 3: Pecharunt ex brings in their next Zoroark. Rampaging Thunder does 250 to Talonflame ex, which survives with 30 because Mochi does nothing (unless Munkidori has counters to move onto it).
you 3: Ignition again; Talon Hunt plus your next Cursed Blast take the second Zoroark.
lesson: Jamming Tower is your best card in this matchup: it removes Watchtower and switches off the 40 from Binding Mochi, which is the difference between Talonflame ex surviving and giving up 2 prizes.
```
