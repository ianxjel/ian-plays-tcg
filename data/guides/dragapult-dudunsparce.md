Dragapult Dudunsparce is Dragapult with a second attacker. Phantom Dive is still the plan, but Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail does 60 for each Pokémon ex your opponent has in play, for one Energy. Against the Mega decks, whose benches are full of Pokémon ex, that is often more than Phantom Dive does. It has the best record of ranks 11 to 20: 56% of its games won online. The Phantom Dive basics are in the [Dragapult guide](guide.html?id=dragapult); this one covers what the Dudunsparce build changes.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** Dragapult. Two turns of Dreepy and Drakloak, then Phantom Dive every turn, with Dudunsparce ex as a one-Energy attacker beside it.
- **Wins by:** Phantom Dive's bench counters, plus Tenacious Tail Knock Outs on boards full of Pokémon ex.
- **Prize profile:** Dragapult ex, Dudunsparce ex, Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2. Everything else gives up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** Tenacious Tail on turn two, from a Dunsparce benched on turn one. Phantom Dive on your third turn.
- **What beats it:** straight Dragapult (it wins 55% of these games), Mega Lucario (Dudunsparce ex is Weak to Fighting), and the usual Dragapult answers: Lillie's Clefairy ex, Hide 'n' Sneak and Battle Cage.

## Card by card

### What stays the same

- **Dragapult ex (320 HP, no Weakness).** Phantom Dive (Fire and Psychic) does 200 to their Active and puts 6 damage counters on their bench in any split. It is a Tera Pokémon: on your bench it takes no damage from attacks.
- **Drakloak (90 HP).** Recon Directive: look at the top 2 cards, keep 1. Two Drakloak is two looks a turn.
- **Munkidori.** With a Darkness attached, moves up to 3 damage counters from one of your Pokémon to one of theirs, once a turn.
- **Crushing Hammer** (four), **Risky Ruins** (two), **Budew**, **Meowth ex**, **Fezandipiti ex**, **Crispin** and **Unfair Stamp** work exactly as in the Dragapult guide.

### What's new

- **Dudunsparce ex (270 HP, Weak to Fighting).** Evolves from Dunsparce. Tenacious Tail (one Energy): 60 for each of their Pokémon ex in play. Destructive Drill (three Energy) does 150 and isn't affected by effects on their Active.
- **Dunsparce (two) and Dudunsparce (one).** Run Away Draw: draw 3, then Dudunsparce and its cards shuffle back into your deck. With two Dunsparce, one can become the attacker and the other the draw.
- **Rosa's Encouragement.** Only while you have more prizes left than your opponent: attach up to 2 Basic Energy from your discard pile to one of your Stage 2 Pokémon. The Fire and Psychic of a Knocked Out Dragapult ex go straight onto the next one.
- **Night Stretcher** (three): a Pokémon or a Basic Energy from your discard pile back to your hand.

### What Tenacious Tail does

| Their Pokémon ex in play | Tenacious Tail | Enough for |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 180 | Meowth ex (170) |
| 4 | 240 | Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, Latias ex, Fezandipiti ex (210), Raging Bolt ex (240) |
| 5 | 300 | Mega Rayquaza ex, N's Zoroark ex, Talonflame ex (280), Mega Kangaskhan ex (300) |
| 6 | 360 | Dragapult ex (320), Hydrapple ex (330), Mega Excadrill ex (340) |

The decks that bench five or more are Mega Rayquaza, Basic Box, Talonflame Dusknoir and N's Zoroark. Dhelmise and Alakazam bench few, so there Dudunsparce ex is just a 270 HP wall.

## The first two turns

The goal is Dragapult's: two Drakloak by the end of turn two, one of them holding Fire and Psychic. Add one thing: a Dunsparce on the bench on turn one.

### Going second

1. Buddy-Buddy Poffin for a Dreepy and a Dunsparce (both fit). Crispin for Fire and Psychic on a benched Dreepy.
2. On turn two, decide what the Dunsparce becomes. Against a bench of three or more Pokémon ex, Dudunsparce ex, which attacks right away. Otherwise Dudunsparce, for Run Away Draw.

### Going first

Bench Dreepy and Dunsparce, attach Energy to a benched Dreepy, and keep Budew in your hand for turn two.

> Dudunsparce ex gives up 2 prizes. If their bench is small and Phantom Dive is a turn away, leave the Dunsparce as a future Dudunsparce instead.

## The middle game

### Phantom Dive or Tenacious Tail

Count their Pokémon ex before you choose. Phantom Dive spreads damage that sets up next turn; Tenacious Tail takes the big Knock Out now. The best turns use both: Phantom Dive softens a Pokémon ex, and Dudunsparce ex finishes it while their board is still full.

### Take the big Pokémon ex first

Every Knock Out you take lowers Tenacious Tail by 60. Use it on the biggest Pokémon ex while their bench is at its fullest, and leave Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex for Phantom Dive's counters.

### Rosa's Encouragement after a Knock Out

When they Knock Out a Dragapult ex and you're behind on prizes, the next Dragapult ex evolves and gets its Fire and Psychic back from the discard pile in the same turn. Plan the turn they take a Dragapult: keep Drakloak on the bench and Rosa's in hand.

### Hammer what pays for the attack

As in the Dragapult guide: Crushing Hammer is best on the Energy that pays their attack cost. Four Hammers mean, on average, two heads.

## Closing the game

- **Two Mega Knock Outs are six prizes.** Against Mega Rayquaza, Tenacious Tail often takes one and Phantom Dive the other.
- **Boss's Orders** pulls up the damaged Pokémon ex, and Tenacious Tail Knocks it Out for one Energy.
- **Watch your own Pokémon ex late.** Dudunsparce ex, Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex are 2 prizes each; keep only one of them in play when you're close to losing.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Dudunsparce ex or Dudunsparce.** Count their Pokémon ex: three or more, the attacker; fewer, the draw.
2. **Which attacker leads.** Dudunsparce ex is ready on turn two; Dragapult ex isn't until turn three. Against Mega decks, leading with Tenacious Tail is a whole turn faster.
3. **Rosa's Encouragement or Boss's Orders.** Both are Supporters. Rosa's when the Dragapult ex needs both Energy back; Boss's Orders when Crispin or your hand covers the attack.
4. **Where Phantom Dive's counters go.** On Pokémon that Tenacious Tail can't reach yet, and on Duskull, Abra and Drakloak that die to them alone.
5. **Bench Dudunsparce ex against Mega Lucario?** Only the turn you attack with it. It is Weak to Fighting and gives up 2.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Tenacious Tail into Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: You go second against Mega Rayquaza. Everything they bench is a Pokémon ex, and Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail does 60 for each of their Pokémon ex in play, for one Energy.
opp 1: Mega Kangaskhan ex Active. They bench Mega Rayquaza ex (Ruler's Roar attaches a Lightning) and Latias ex, and attach a Fire to Rayquaza. Three Pokémon ex in play.
you 1: Dreepy A Active. Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Dreepy B and Dunsparce. Crispin: Psychic onto Dreepy B and Fire to your hand; attach the Fire to Dreepy B too.
opp 2: Run Errand draws 2. They bench Meowth ex and, with Excited Dive, Talonflame ex: five Pokémon ex. Latias ex lets Kangaskhan retreat for free, and Storm Emerald with 3 Fire and Lightning (150) Knocks Out Dreepy A. 1 prize. You promote Dunsparce.
you 2: Dunsparce becomes Dudunsparce ex; attach Darkness. Dreepy B becomes Drakloak (Recon Directive). Tenacious Tail: five Pokémon ex is 300, and Mega Rayquaza ex has 280. Knocked Out, 3 prizes.
opp 3: They promote Mega Kangaskhan ex. Nothing on their side has the Energy for a big attack yet: they attach to Talonflame ex, and Run Errand draws 2.
you 3: Four Pokémon ex left: Tenacious Tail does 240 to Kangaskhan (60 left). Drakloak becomes Dragapult ex on your bench, where it takes no damage from attacks.
opp 4: Latias ex lets Kangaskhan retreat for free; Talonflame ex's Talon Hunt does 150 to Dudunsparce ex (120 left).
you 4: Boss's Orders brings Kangaskhan back up. Tenacious Tail, still 240, Knocks it Out: 3 more prizes. You win.
lesson: Against a deck of Pokémon ex, Dudunsparce ex is a one-Energy attacker that hits harder than Dragapult ex, and it is ready a turn earlier. Count their Pokémon ex before you attack: five is 300, enough for Mega Rayquaza ex (280) and Mega Kangaskhan ex (300).
```

### Rosa's Encouragement against Talonflame Dusknoir

```play
setup: Rosa's Encouragement can only be played while you have more prizes left than your opponent. Then it attaches up to two Basic Energy from your discard pile to one of your Stage 2 Pokémon. Talonflame Dusknoir's Knock Outs come from Talon Hunt (150) plus Dusknoir's Cursed Blast (13 counters). They have already taken 1 prize, for a Dreepy.
you 3: Dragapult ex A: Phantom Dive does 200 to Talonflame ex (80 left), and its 6 counters Knock Out a benched Duskull (60 HP). 1 prize.
opp 3: Talon Hunt (150), Cursed Blast (13 counters) and Black Belt's Training (40) do exactly 320: Dragapult ex A is Knocked Out. 2 prizes for them, 3 in all. Dusknoir Knocks itself Out: 1 for you, 2 in all. You promote Drakloak B.
you 4: You have 4 prizes left and they have 3, so Rosa's Encouragement is live. Drakloak B becomes Dragapult ex, and Rosa's puts the Fire and Psychic from your discard pile on it. Phantom Dive Knocks Out Talonflame ex: 2 prizes. The 6 counters go on their Dusclops (90 HP, 30 left).
lesson: Losing a Dragapult ex usually costs you a turn while the next one is powered. Rosa's Encouragement turns that around: once you're behind, the Energy that went to the discard pile with the old Dragapult ex is back on the new one the same turn.
```

### Dudunsparce ex finishes N's Zoroark

```play
setup: N's Zoroark fields up to five Pokémon ex at once: two N's Zoroark ex (280 HP), Pecharunt ex, Fezandipiti ex and Meowth ex. Their Gravity Mountain gives your Dragapult ex 290 HP, exactly Rampaging Thunder (250) plus Binding Mochi (40).
you 3: Dragapult ex A: Phantom Dive does 200 to N's Zoroark ex A (80 left) and 6 counters on N's Zekrom (70 left). Their Pokémon ex in play: Zoroark A, Zoroark B, Pecharunt ex and Meowth ex.
opp 3: Gravity Mountain. Subjugating Chains switches in Zoroark B, Poisoned; Night Joker copies Rampaging Thunder and Binding Mochi adds 40: 290 Knocks Out Dragapult ex A. 2 prizes for them. They bench Fezandipiti ex: five Pokémon ex in play.
you 4: Promote Dudunsparce ex, benched since turn two with a Darkness attached. Poison has taken Zoroark B to 270. Tenacious Tail: five Pokémon ex is 300. Knocked Out, 2 prizes. Zoroark A (80 left) and the damaged Zekrom are next.
lesson: Against a bench of Pokémon ex, your one-Energy attacker grows with their board. Let Phantom Dive put damage everywhere, and let Dudunsparce ex take the Knock Out that Dragapult ex can't reach.
```
