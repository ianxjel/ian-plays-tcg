On Limitless this deck has the highest average win rate across its top-10 matchups, and the reason is one Ability. Meganium's Wild Growth makes every Basic Grass Energy you have in play provide two Grass Energy, and both of the deck's attackers count Energy. Everything else in the list is there to get Meganium into play, keep it there, and stack Grass Energy as fast as Teal Mask Ogerpon ex can attach it.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a Grass ramp deck with two Stage 2 lines. Teal Mask Ogerpon ex attacks early; Hydrapple ex closes.
- **Wins by:** damage that scales with Energy. With Meganium in play, each Basic Grass on the Active Ogerpon adds 60 to Myriad Leaf Shower, and each Basic Grass anywhere adds 60 to Hydrapple ex's Syrup Storm.
- **Prize profile:** Ogerpon ex, Hydrapple ex, Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2; Meganium and the rest give up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two with Ogerpon. Meganium usually lands on turn three.
- **What beats it:** Legendary Pyroduct (Meganium and Hydrapple ex are Evolution Pokémon, so it turns off Wild Growth and Ripening Charge), Fire attackers (every Pokémon in the deck is Weak to Fire), and anything that Knocks Out Meganium before it matters.

## Card by card

### The attackers

- **Teal Mask Ogerpon ex (210 HP, Weak to Fire, Retreat 1).** Teal Dance: once a turn, attach a Basic Grass from your hand to this Ogerpon and draw a card. Each Ogerpon has its own Teal Dance, so two Ogerpon in play is two extra attachments a turn. Myriad Leaf Shower (three Grass) does 30 plus 30 for each Energy attached to both Active Pokémon, yours and theirs. It is a Tera Pokémon: on your bench it takes no damage from attacks.
- **Hydrapple ex (330 HP, Weak to Fire, Retreat 3).** Ripening Charge: once a turn, attach a Basic Grass from your hand to any of your Pokémon and heal 30 from it. Syrup Storm costs only two Energy of any type and does 30 plus 30 for each Grass Energy attached to all of your Pokémon.

### The engine

- **Meganium (160 HP).** Wild Growth: each Basic Grass Energy attached to your Pokémon provides two Grass Energy. It doesn't stack, so a second Meganium is a backup, not a bonus. Its own attack, Solar Beam (140), is a last resort.
- **Celebi (80 HP).** Traverse Time costs one Grass: search for up to three cards in any mix of Grass Pokémon and Stadiums. Going second, it is a turn-one attack that finds Bayleef, Meganium and Forest of Vitality.
- **Forest of Vitality.** Grass Pokémon can evolve into Grass Pokémon on the turn they are put into play, except on a player's first turn. A Chikorita you bench on turn two can become Bayleef right away, so a Knocked Out Meganium line can be rebuilt fast. It also replaces their Stadium, which matters most when that Stadium is Legendary Pyroduct.
- **Chikorita, Bayleef, Applin, Dipplin.** The evolution steps. You don't attack with them.

### Trainers

- **Bug Catching Set** (four): look at the top 7 cards, take up to two Grass Pokémon or Basic Grass Energy. It finds both halves of the deck.
- **Dawn**: a Basic, a Stage 1 and a Stage 2 in one card (Chikorita, Bayleef, Meganium).
- **Lana's Aid**: up to three Pokémon without a Rule Box or Basic Energy from your discard pile. Rebuild Meganium after it falls.
- **Briar**: only when your opponent has exactly 2 prizes left. If your Tera Pokémon (Ogerpon) Knocks Out their Active with an attack this turn, take 1 more prize.
- **Team Rocket's Petrel** finds any Trainer: usually Forest of Vitality or Boss's Orders.

### What Wild Growth does to the numbers

| Board | Myriad Leaf Shower | Syrup Storm |
|---|---|---|
| Ogerpon with 3 Grass, their Active with 2 Energy, no Meganium | 30 + 30 × 5 = 180 | |
| Same, with Meganium | 30 + 30 × 8 = 270 | |
| Ogerpon with 4 Grass and Meganium, their Active with 2 | 30 + 30 × 10 = 330 | |
| 5 Basic Grass in play, no Meganium | | 30 + 30 × 5 = 180 |
| 5 Basic Grass in play, with Meganium | | 30 + 30 × 10 = 330 |
| 6 Basic Grass in play, with Meganium | | 390 |

Myriad Leaf Shower also counts your opponent's Energy, so it punishes the decks that load their Active (Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Excadrill ex).

## The first two turns

1. **Turn one:** Ogerpon on the bench (Teal Dance right away), Chikorita and Applin on the bench, Celebi Active if you are going second. Celebi's Traverse Time finds Bayleef, Meganium and Forest of Vitality.
2. **Turn two:** Forest of Vitality, Bayleef, and a second Ogerpon with Teal Dance. Ogerpon can attack this turn with three Grass from Teal Dance and attachments.
3. **Turn three:** Meganium. From here every Grass on the Active Ogerpon is worth 60.

> Wild Growth doesn't stack, but you still want both Meganium lines started. The first Meganium is the first thing a good opponent will Boss's Orders.

## The middle game

- **Feed the Active Ogerpon.** Teal Dance only attaches to the Ogerpon using it, so the attacking Ogerpon grows itself. Your normal attachment and Ripening Charge can go anywhere.
- **Hydrapple ex is the wall.** At 330 HP, with Ripening Charge healing 30 a turn, it survives most single hits. Syrup Storm counts every Basic Grass in play, so the Energy you put on benched Ogerpon still counts.
- **Protect Meganium.** Bench it behind threats they must answer, and keep Lana's Aid, Night Stretcher or a second line ready. Without Wild Growth, the numbers above are halved.
- **Count their Hammers.** Against Crushing Hammer, each Basic Grass removed is 60 damage with Meganium in play. Spread Energy across Pokémon so one heads doesn't strand your attacker.

## Closing the game

- **Briar** when they have exactly 2 prizes left: an Ogerpon Knock Out takes 3 instead of 2.
- **Boss's Orders** for the last two-prize Pokémon ex on their bench; Ogerpon's damage depends on the Active, so a benched target with lots of Energy is often the bigger hit.
- **Syrup Storm finishes.** Late in the game you usually have 5 or more Basic Grass in play, which is 330 with Meganium. That is a Knock Out on almost anything.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Celebi or Ogerpon first.** Celebi Active going second sets up the Meganium line; Ogerpon Active going first starts building Energy for turn two.
2. **Which Ogerpon gets the Grass.** The one that will be Active next turn.
3. **When to play Forest of Vitality.** When you need to evolve something benched this turn, or the turn they play Pyroduct.
4. **Hydrapple or Ogerpon to attack.** Ogerpon when their Active carries Energy; Hydrapple ex when they don't, or when you need 330 HP in front.
5. **How many Pokémon ex to bench.** Against Dhelmise, Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail does 60 for each of your Pokémon ex in play.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### The full build into Dragapult

```play
setup: You go second. Celebi Active; Teal Mask Ogerpon ex and Chikorita on the bench.
opp 1: Dreepy Active, two more Dreepy benched. Psychic attached.
you 1: Teal Dance: a Grass onto Ogerpon and a card. Lillie's Determination at 6 prizes draws 8. Attach Grass to Celebi and use Traverse Time: Bayleef, Meganium and Forest of Vitality.
opp 2: Two Drakloak, Recon Directive twice. Crushing Hammer: heads, the Grass on Ogerpon is discarded.
you 2: Play Forest of Vitality. Chikorita evolves into Bayleef. Bench a second Chikorita and, under Forest, evolve it into Bayleef the same turn. Teal Dance and your attachment put two Grass on Ogerpon.
opp 3: Dragapult ex. Phantom Dive Knocks Out Celebi (1 prize) and spreads 6 counters across your two Bayleef.
you 3: One Bayleef becomes Meganium. Promote Ogerpon, Teal Dance for a third Grass and attach a fourth. With Wild Growth that is 8 Grass Energy on Ogerpon, plus 2 Energy on Dragapult ex: Myriad Leaf Shower does 30 + 30 × 10 = 330. Dragapult ex is Knocked Out: 2 prizes.
opp 4: They Boss's Orders Meganium and Knock it Out with their next Dragapult ex. Wild Growth is gone.
you 4: The second Bayleef becomes Meganium and Wild Growth is back. Teal Dance a fifth Grass onto Ogerpon: 10 Grass Energy plus their 2 is 390. Another Dragapult ex Knocked Out.
lesson: Start both Meganium lines, because Dragapult's best answer to you is Boss's Orders on Meganium. Forest of Vitality lets the second line catch up the turn you bench it.
```

### Into N's Zoroark: Weakness does the work

```play
setup: N's Zoroark ex (280 HP) is Weak to Grass. Its usual attack is a copied Rampaging Thunder for 250, plus 40 with Binding Mochi.
you 1: Ogerpon A Active: Teal Dance and an attachment, two Grass. Bench Ogerpon B (Teal Dance, one Grass) and Applin.
opp 2: N's Zoroark ex. Rampaging Thunder with Mochi does 290 to Ogerpon A: Knocked Out, 2 prizes.
you 2: Promote Ogerpon B. Teal Dance and an attachment: three Grass. Myriad Leaf Shower counts its 3 and the Zoroark's 2 Darkness: 180, doubled by Weakness to 360. Knocked Out, 2 prizes. Bench Ogerpon C (Teal Dance, one Grass), and Applin becomes Dipplin.
opp 3: Pecharunt ex brings in their second Zoroark, Poisoned with Mochi. Rampaging Thunder Knocks Out Ogerpon B.
you 3: Promote Ogerpon C: Teal Dance and an attachment make three Grass, and Myriad Leaf Shower does 360 again. Knocked Out: 4 prizes taken. Dipplin becomes Hydrapple ex on the bench; at 330 HP it survives Rampaging Thunder plus Mochi (290), so it is the Pokémon you want in front next.
lesson: Against Zoroark you don't need Meganium to win trades; Weakness doubles everything. Your job is to keep an attacker coming every turn and to put Hydrapple ex, which survives their best hit, in front as soon as you can.
```

### The Stadium war into Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: Mega Rayquaza decks play four Legendary Pyroduct: Evolution Pokémon have no Abilities, which turns off Wild Growth and Ripening Charge. Their Ethan's Ho-Oh ex is a Fire Pokémon, and you are Weak to Fire.
opp 3: Pyroduct. Storm Emerald Knocks Out your Active Ogerpon. Rayquaza now has 3 Energy on it.
you 3: Meganium is in play but does nothing under Pyroduct. Your next Ogerpon has 4 Grass: without Wild Growth, Myriad Leaf Shower does 30 + 30 × 7 = 240 to Rayquaza (280 HP). Not enough. Play Forest of Vitality instead: Pyroduct is discarded and Wild Growth is back. Now it is 30 + 30 × 11 = 360. Mega Rayquaza ex is Knocked Out: 3 prizes.
opp 4: They play their next Pyroduct and Knock Out Ogerpon.
you 4: Team Rocket's Petrel or Celebi's Traverse Time finds your next Forest. Same turn, same answer.
lesson: You play three Forest of Vitality and two ways to search for them, and they play four Pyroduct. The turn you put Forest down is the turn Wild Growth works, so play it right before you attack, and make the attack count.
```
