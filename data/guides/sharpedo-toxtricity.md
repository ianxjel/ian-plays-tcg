Sharpedo Toxtricity is a Darkness deck that damages its own attacker on purpose. Mega Sharpedo ex's Hungry Jaws does 120, plus 150 if Sharpedo has any damage counters on it. Toxtricity's Sinister Surge attaches a Darkness Energy from your deck to a benched Darkness Pokémon and puts 2 damage counters on it; Pecharunt ex's Subjugating Chains switches it in, Poisoned; and Binding Mochi adds 40 to a Poisoned Pokémon's attacks. That is 310 on the first turn Sharpedo attacks.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a Mega with a built-in condition. Sinister Surge on the bench, Subjugating Chains into the Active Spot, Hungry Jaws.
- **Wins by:** Hungry Jaws for 270 to 310, which takes Megas and big Pokémon ex in one hit, and Greedy Fang (one Energy, 70 and draw 2) against Darkness-Weak single-prize decks.
- **Prize profile:** Mega Sharpedo ex gives up 3, Pecharunt ex and Fezandipiti ex 2, everything else 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two.
- **What beats it:** Grass attackers (Mega Sharpedo ex and Carvanha are Weak to Grass), Fighting attackers against Toxel and Toxtricity, and Festival Grounds, which stops the Poison that turns on Binding Mochi.

## Card by card

### The attacker

- **Mega Sharpedo ex (330 HP, Darkness, Weak to Grass, Retreat 0).** Evolves from Carvanha. Greedy Fang (one Darkness): 70, then draw 2. Hungry Jaws (two Darkness): 120, plus 150 if it has any damage counters on it: 270.
- **Carvanha (70 HP, Weak to Grass, Retreat 1).** Reckless Charge (one Darkness) does 30 and 10 to itself, and that damage stays when it evolves.

### The engine

- **Toxtricity (140 HP, Weak to Fighting, Retreat 2).** Evolves from Toxel. Sinister Surge: once during your turn, search your deck for a Basic Darkness Energy and attach it to one of your benched Darkness Pokémon, then put 2 damage counters on that Pokémon. On a benched Sharpedo, that is an Energy and exactly the counters Hungry Jaws wants. Gentle Slap (two Darkness and one more) does 100.
- **Toxel (70 HP, Weak to Fighting, Retreat 1).** Call for Family (one Darkness): up to 2 Basic Pokémon from your deck onto your bench.
- **Pecharunt ex (190 HP).** Subjugating Chains: once during your turn, switch one of your benched Darkness Pokémon (not a Pecharunt ex) with your Active Pokémon; the new Active is now Poisoned. A free switch, and the Poison that turns on Binding Mochi.
- **Binding Mochi** (three, Tool): if the Pokémon it's attached to is Poisoned, its attacks do 40 more to their Active. Hungry Jaws becomes 310.
- **Munkidori** (two): with a Darkness attached, moves up to 3 damage counters from one of your Pokémon to one of theirs, once a turn. Leave at least one counter on Sharpedo; the counters are your bonus.
- **Janine's Secret Art**: up to two of your Darkness Pokémon each get a Basic Darkness Energy from your deck; if one of them is your Active, it is now Poisoned.

### Support Pokémon

- **Tatsugiri (70 HP).** Attract Customers: once a turn while it is your Active Pokémon, look at the top 6 cards and take a Supporter.
- **Yveltal (110 HP, Retreat 0).** Clutch (one Darkness): 20, and their Active can't retreat during their next turn. Dark Feather (two Darkness and one more): 110. A single-prize attacker for when a 3-prize Sharpedo is the wrong trade.
- **Fezandipiti ex**: draws 3 after one of your Pokémon is Knocked Out.

### Trainers

- **Team Rocket's Watchtower** (two): Colorless Pokémon have no Abilities: Mega Kangaskhan ex, Mega Rayquaza ex, Dudunsparce, Fan Rotom and Meowth ex.
- **Gravity Mountain**: every Stage 2 has 30 less HP. Dragapult ex drops to 290 and Hydrapple ex to 300.
- **Team Rocket's Petrel** (two), **Judge** (two), **Unfair Stamp**, **Night Stretcher**, **Ultra Ball** and **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** (four each), **Poké Pad** (three).

### Energy

Nine Basic Darkness Energy. Sinister Surge and Janine's Secret Art both search for a Basic Darkness, so the count matters.

## The first two turns

### Turn one

1. Toxel and Carvanha in play. Going second, Toxel's Call for Family benches two more Basics.
2. Bench Pecharunt ex if you can: Ultra Ball finds it, since Buddy-Buddy Poffin can't (190 HP).
3. Going second, Lillie's Determination before you attack.

### Turn two

1. Evolve Toxel into Toxtricity and a benched Carvanha into Mega Sharpedo ex.
2. Sinister Surge on the benched Sharpedo. Attach a second Darkness by hand, and Binding Mochi.
3. Subjugating Chains: Sharpedo into the Active Spot, Poisoned. Hungry Jaws: 120 plus 150 plus 40 is 310.

> Sharpedo has no Retreat Cost, but Subjugating Chains is better than retreating: it is free, and it brings the Poison.

## The middle game

### What Hungry Jaws reaches

| Hungry Jaws with | Damage | Knocks Out |
|---|---|---|
| Damage counters on Sharpedo | 270 | Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, Lillie's Clefairy ex, every single-prize Pokémon |
| Counters and Binding Mochi (Poisoned) | 310 | Mega Rayquaza ex, N's Zoroark ex, Talonflame ex, Mega Kangaskhan ex |
| Counters, Mochi and a Munkidori move | 340 | Dragapult ex, Hydrapple ex, Mega Excadrill ex, Mega Lucario ex |
| Counters and Munkidori, under Gravity Mountain | 300 | Dragapult ex (290) |

### Poison keeps the bonus on

Poison puts a damage counter on Sharpedo between turns, so a Poisoned Sharpedo always has counters. Munkidori can move the rest onto their Pokémon, healing you as it deals damage, as long as one stays.

### Greedy Fang against small Pokémon

Dhelmise, Slowking and Alakazam are Weak to Darkness. One Darkness does 140 and draws 2. Don't spend Hungry Jaws on them.

### Watchtower or Gravity Mountain

Watchtower against Colorless engines (Mega Rayquaza, the Dudunsparce decks); Gravity Mountain against Stage 2 Pokémon ex (Dragapult, Hydrapple). You have two and one.

## Closing the game

- **Two Mega Knock Outs** win against the Mega decks. 310 is enough for Mega Rayquaza ex and Mega Kangaskhan ex; the 330 and 340 HP Megas need a Munkidori move as well.
- **Yveltal's Clutch** keeps their Active in place for a turn while you set up the next Sharpedo.
- **Unfair Stamp** after they Knock Out a Sharpedo: you draw 5, they draw 2.
- **Boss's Orders** for a damaged benched Pokémon ex.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Where Sinister Surge goes.** A benched Sharpedo almost always. A Munkidori or Yveltal only when the counters on them are moved or don't matter.
2. **How many counters Munkidori moves.** All but one from Sharpedo.
3. **Watchtower or Gravity Mountain.** See above.
4. **Greedy Fang or Hungry Jaws.** Greedy Fang whenever 70 (or 140 against Weakness) takes the Knock Out; you draw 2.
5. **How many Pecharunt ex to bench.** One. It gives up 2 prizes, and only one Subjugating Chains works each turn.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Surge, Chains, 310: Mega Rayquaza

```play
setup: You go second against Mega Rayquaza. Hungry Jaws (two Darkness) does 120, plus 150 if Mega Sharpedo ex has any damage counters on it. Sinister Surge gives a benched Darkness Pokémon an Energy and 2 counters; Subjugating Chains switches it in, Poisoned; Binding Mochi adds 40 while it is Poisoned.
opp 1: Mega Kangaskhan ex Active; Mega Rayquaza ex benched with Ruler's Roar's Lightning and a Fire.
you 1: Lillie's Determination draws 8. Toxel A Active with a Darkness: Call for Family benches Toxel B and Carvanha A. Ultra Ball finds Pecharunt ex and Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches Carvanha B.
opp 2: Run Errand draws 2. They bench Latias ex, attach a Lightning (3 Fire and Lightning), retreat Kangaskhan for free and Storm Emerald (150) Knocks Out Toxel A. 1 prize. You promote Carvanha B.
you 2: Toxel B becomes Toxtricity and Carvanha A becomes Mega Sharpedo ex on your bench. Sinister Surge: a Darkness from your deck onto Sharpedo, and 2 counters on it. Attach a second Darkness and Binding Mochi. Subjugating Chains: Sharpedo switches in, Poisoned. Hungry Jaws: 120, plus 150, plus 40. Mega Rayquaza ex is Knocked Out, 3 prizes.
opp 3: Their second Rayquaza comes off the bench with a fresh Ruler's Roar and a Fire: only 2 Fire and Lightning, since the first Rayquaza's Energy left with it. Storm Emerald does 100 to Sharpedo (200 left).
you 3: Sharpedo is still Poisoned, still damaged and still holding Binding Mochi: Hungry Jaws does 310 again. The second Rayquaza is Knocked Out, 3 more prizes. You win.
lesson: Sinister Surge's 2 counters are the switch that turns on Hungry Jaws, and Subjugating Chains' Poison turns on Binding Mochi: 310 on the first turn Sharpedo attacks. After that, Poison adds a counter every turn, so the bonus never turns itself off.
```

### Greedy Fang into Dhelmise

```play
setup: Dhelmise (140 HP), Shuppet (50) and Banette (80) are all Weak to Darkness. Greedy Fang costs one Darkness: 70, doubled to 140, then draw 2. Their Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon ignore Munkidori's counters, but Lillie's Clefairy ex doesn't have Hide 'n' Sneak.
you 2: Mega Sharpedo ex A with one Darkness: Greedy Fang Knocks Out Dhelmise A and draws 2. 1 prize.
opp 2: Vengeful Anchor does 170 to Sharpedo A (160 left).
you 3: Munkidori moves 3 counters from Sharpedo A to their benched Lillie's Clefairy ex (160 left); Sharpedo A is back to 190. Greedy Fang Knocks Out Dhelmise B: 2 prizes.
opp 3: Vengeful Anchor leaves Sharpedo A on 20.
you 4: Munkidori again: Sharpedo A on 50, Clefairy on 130. Greedy Fang Knocks Out the Banette they promoted: 3 prizes.
lesson: Against a Darkness-Weak single-prize deck the one-Energy attack is the right one: Greedy Fang takes a Knock Out and draws 2. Munkidori moves the damage they do onto the Pokémon that doesn't have Hide 'n' Sneak.
```

### Gravity Mountain and Munkidori into Dragapult

```play
setup: Dragapult ex has 320 HP. Gravity Mountain takes 30 off every Stage 2, so Hungry Jaws (270) plus one Munkidori move (3 counters) is enough: 300 against 290. Phantom Dive's 200 doesn't come close to Knocking Out a 330 HP Mega Sharpedo ex.
you 3: Play Gravity Mountain. Sharpedo A, Active, has 4 damage counters on it. Munkidori moves 3 of them to Dragapult ex A; Sharpedo keeps 1, so Hungry Jaws keeps its bonus. 270 to a Dragapult ex with 290 HP and 30 damage: Knocked Out, 2 prizes.
opp 3: Dragapult ex B: Phantom Dive does 200 to Sharpedo A (120 left) and 6 counters on Toxtricity (80 left).
you 4: Munkidori moves 3 counters from Sharpedo A to Dragapult ex B; Hungry Jaws does 270. Knocked Out under Gravity Mountain: 2 more prizes, 4 in all.
lesson: Keep at least one damage counter on Sharpedo when Munkidori moves the rest: the counters are your bonus. Gravity Mountain turns 270 plus Munkidori's 30 into a Dragapult ex Knock Out every turn.
```
