Mega Excadrill is a 340 HP wall that hits for 330. It is a Stage 1, so it comes down on turn two, but its big attack needs five Energy. The deck's answer is a second evolution line whose only job is Energy: Metang's Metal Maker digs Metal Energy off the top of your deck every turn.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a heavy attacker with an Energy engine behind it. Mega Excadrill ex in front, two or three Metang on the bench.
- **Wins by:** Maximum Drilling, 200 plus 130 once Excadrill has two Energy beyond its three-Energy cost. 330 Knocks Out every Pokémon in the format except Mega Excadrill ex itself.
- **Prize profile:** Mega Excadrill ex and Mega Skarmory ex give up 3; Genesect ex and Fezandipiti ex give up 2; the Metal lines, Drilbur and Shaymin give up 1.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two (Undermine for two Energy). Maximum Drilling for 330 usually on turn three.
- **What beats it:** Legendary Pyroduct (Metang is an Evolution Pokémon, so Metal Maker turns off), Crushing Hammer, Grass decks that count your Energy for their damage, and decks that need 6 single-prize Knock Outs from you.

## Card by card

### The attackers

- **Mega Excadrill ex (340 HP, Weak to Fire, resists Grass, Retreat 4).** Evolves from Drilbur. Undermine (two Metal) does 90 and discards the top 2 cards of your opponent's deck. Maximum Drilling (three Metal) does 200, or 330 if Excadrill has at least 2 more Energy than the cost.
- **Mega Skarmory ex (260 HP, Retreat 0).** Sonic Ripper (two Metal and one more): shuffle all of its Energy into your deck and do 220 to any one of their Pokémon. Weakness and Resistance don't apply to the bench. It is a Mega: 3 prizes if it falls.
- **Genesect ex (220 HP).** Metallic Signal searches your deck for up to two Metal Evolution Pokémon (Metang, Metagross, Mega Excadrill ex) once a turn. Protect Charge (150) also makes it take 30 less damage next turn.
- **Metagross (180 HP).** A one-of Stage 2. Bounce Back (one Metal) does 60 and sends their Active to the bench (they pick the replacement); Metallic Hammer does 150, or 300 if you discard 3 Metal from it.

### The engine

- **Metang (100 HP).** Metal Maker: once a turn, look at the top 4 cards of your deck and attach any Metal Energy you find there to your Pokémon however you like. Each Metang has its own Metal Maker. With 16 Metal in 60 cards, each look finds about one early on (an estimate).
- **Drilbur (70 HP).** Call for Family (one Energy) benches up to two Basic Pokémon from your deck.
- **Team Rocket's Petrel** finds any Trainer; **Brock's Scouting** finds two Basic Pokémon or one Evolution Pokémon; **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** benches two Basics with 70 HP or less.

### Support

- **Jumbo Ice Cream** (three): heal 80 from your Active Pokémon if it has 3 or more Energy. On a 340 HP Excadrill, that is often a whole extra turn.
- **Shaymin.** Flower Curtain: your benched Pokémon without a Rule Box take no damage from their attacks. That protects Beldum, Metang and Drilbur from Kyurem's Trifrost and Fezandipiti ex's Cruel Arrow. It doesn't stop damage counters.
- **Hero's Cape**: 100 more HP. Mega Excadrill ex with it has 440.
- **Gravity Mountain**: every Stage 2 has 30 less HP. Dragapult ex drops to 290, Hydrapple ex to 300, Meganium and Dusknoir to 130.
- **Energy Recycler** shuffles up to 5 Basic Energy from your discard pile into your deck, which keeps Metal Maker finding Energy late.
- **Kieran**: a switch, or 30 more damage to their Active Pokémon ex this turn.

## The first two turns

1. **Turn one:** bench Drilbur and two Beldum (Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Brock's Scouting). Attach Metal to Drilbur.
2. **Turn two:** Drilbur into Mega Excadrill ex, Beldum into Metang. Use every Metal Maker before attaching by hand, so you know what you still need. Undermine is ready with two Energy; Maximum Drilling needs three.
3. **Turn three:** five Energy on Excadrill, Maximum Drilling for 330.

> Genesect ex's Metallic Signal finds Mega Excadrill ex and Metang together. If your opening has Genesect but no Drilbur, it is still a turn-two Excadrill as long as you find a Drilbur on turn one.

## The middle game

- **Metal Maker first, always.** Look at the top before you draw or search; the Energy you see might change which Pokémon gets your hand attachment.
- **Keep the bench Energy on Excadrill.** Metal Maker attaches anywhere, but Maximum Drilling's bonus only counts Energy on Excadrill.
- **Heal on the turn it matters.** Jumbo Ice Cream needs 3 Energy on the Active Pokémon. Use it right after they hit Excadrill and before you attack.
- **Undermine is a choice, not a filler.** Against decks that stack the top of their deck (Slowking), Undermine throws away the card they set up. Against Dhelmise, it puts Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon in their discard pile, which is exactly what they want.

## Closing the game

- **Two Mega Knock Outs.** Against Mega Rayquaza or Basic Box, two Maximum Drillings on Megas are all 6 prizes.
- **Sonic Ripper** takes a damaged or low-HP Pokémon ex off their bench for the last 2 prizes.
- **Special Red Card** when they are on 3 or fewer prizes.
- **Watch your own Megas.** Skarmory and Excadrill are 3 prizes each. Keep only one of them exposed late.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Undermine or wait.** Undermine on turn two takes a small Knock Out (90) and mills two cards; holding Energy for Maximum Drilling next turn can be better against big Basics.
2. **How many Metang.** Two is the target, three if you face Hammers or Pyroduct.
3. **Where Metal Maker's Energy goes.** Excadrill first; the second attacker only when Excadrill already has five.
4. **When to bring out Skarmory.** When 220 to a benched Pokémon takes 2 prizes or a key engine piece, and you can retreat it (Retreat 0) before it is attacked.
5. **Gravity Mountain or not.** Play it against Stage 2 decks; it also shrinks your own Metagross.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Turn-three 330 into Dragapult

```play
setup: You go first. Drilbur Active.
you 1: Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches two Beldum. Attach Metal to Drilbur. No Supporter going first.
opp 1: Dreepy Active, two more Dreepy and Budew benched.
you 2: Drilbur into Mega Excadrill ex, both Beldum into Metang. Metal Maker twice: one Metal each, both onto Excadrill (3 Energy). Attach a fourth. Undermine does 90 to their Active Dreepy (Knocked Out, 1 prize) and discards their top 2 cards.
opp 2: Two Drakloak. Crushing Hammer: heads, Excadrill drops to 3 Energy.
you 3: Metal Maker twice finds one Metal (4), and your attachment makes 5. The Active Drakloak has 90 HP, so Undermine takes the same Knock Out as Maximum Drilling: 2 prizes taken, two more of their cards milled, and five Energy stay on Excadrill for next turn.
opp 3: Dragapult ex. Phantom Dive does 200 to Excadrill (140 left) and 6 counters to a Metang (40 left).
you 4: Jumbo Ice Cream heals 80 (220 left). Metal Maker. Maximum Drilling does 330 to Dragapult ex: Knocked Out, 4 prizes taken.
opp 4: Another Dragapult ex. Phantom Dive does 200 to Excadrill: it survives on 20.
lesson: Metal Maker makes five Energy by turn three realistic even through a Hammer, and Jumbo Ice Cream turns a 340 HP Excadrill into a Pokémon that survives two Phantom Dives. Use the cheaper attack whenever it takes the same Knock Out.
```

### Undermine against a stacked deck

```play
setup: Slowking stacks its own deck: Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking puts two chosen cards on top (one for this turn, one for next), and Academy at Night puts one card from hand on top. Kyurem's Trifrost, copied by Slowking, does 110 to three of your Pokémon.
you 1: Shaymin on the bench. Flower Curtain: Trifrost's damage can't touch your benched Beldum, Metang or Drilbur.
opp 2: Ciphermaniac's: Kyurem on top, Metagross second. Slowking flips Kyurem: Trifrost does 110 to your Active Excadrill and nothing to the two benched Metang.
you 2: Undermine their Active Slowking (90, 30 short of its 120) and discard their top 2 cards: the Metagross they stacked for next turn is gone.
opp 3: Their top card is now random. They must spend another Supporter on stacking instead of drawing.
you 3: Maximum Drilling Knocks Out Slowking.
lesson: Shaymin turns off their spread against your engine, and Undermine throws away the second card of every two-turn stack. Make them rebuild their plan every turn.
```

### Sonic Ripper into N's Zoroark

```play
setup: N's Zoroark ex copies attacks from a benched N's Zekrom (130 HP). Pecharunt ex (190 HP) is their switch and the source of their Poison bonus.
opp 2: Zoroark ex's copied Rampaging Thunder does 250 to Excadrill (90 left).
you 2: Jumbo Ice Cream heals 80 (170 left). Maximum Drilling Knocks Out the Active Zoroark ex: 2 prizes.
opp 3: Pecharunt ex's Chains brings in a second Zoroark; Rampaging Thunder with Binding Mochi does 290 and Knocks Out Excadrill (3 prizes for them).
you 3: Mega Skarmory ex with two Metal and one more (Metal Maker helps). Sonic Ripper does 220 to the benched N's Zekrom: Knocked Out. Their Zoroarks have nothing big to copy until they find the second Zekrom. Skarmory's Energy goes back into your deck; next turn it retreats for free.
lesson: Mega Skarmory ex is a Mega, so use it for the snipe that changes the game, then get it out of the Active Spot. Against Zoroark that snipe is N's Zekrom.
```
