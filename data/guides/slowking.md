Slowking is a puzzle deck. Its attacker has no attack of its own worth using: Seek Inspiration discards the top card of your deck and, if that card is a Pokémon without a Rule Box, uses one of its attacks. Played blind, that is a coin flip with worse odds. Played well, you decide what is on top before you attack, every turn. This 60 is the most-copied Slowking list: Limitless links it for Alejandro Luciano Eijo (9th at LAIC), Aarni Karjala (who won Regional Gdańsk with Slowking) and several others.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a control deck that picks its attack each turn by stacking the top of its deck.
- **Wins by:** Kyurem's Trifrost (110 to three Pokémon at once) wiping evolution lines, Zeraora's Thunder Raid (210 to a benched Pokémon ex) picking off 2-prize support, and Metagross's Metallic Hammer (150) for single targets.
- **Prize profile:** Slowpoke and Slowking give up 1. The deck also runs ten Pokémon ex (four Mega Kangaskhan ex at 3 prizes each), so it is not a single-prize deck.
- **Fastest attack:** turn two, Slowpoke into Slowking.
- **What beats it:** anything that wrecks the top of your deck or Knocks Out a 120 HP Slowking cheaply: Dusknoir's Cursed Blast, N's Zoroark ex (Slowking is Weak to Darkness), and Shaymin's Flower Curtain against the spread.

## Card by card

### Slowking and its library

- **Slowpoke (80 HP).** Dangle Tail (one Energy) puts a Pokémon from your discard pile into your hand: a used Kyurem or Zeraora comes back to be stacked again.
- **Slowking (120 HP, Weak to Darkness, Retreat 3).** Seek Inspiration costs Psychic plus one. You use the flipped Pokémon's attack without paying its cost. Super Psy Bolt (Psychic, Psychic, one more) does 120 when the stack fails.

| Card on top | Attack you get | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Kyurem | Trifrost | 110 to each of 3 of their Pokémon; discard all Energy from Slowking |
| Zeraora | Thunder Raid | 210 to one benched Pokémon ex; discard all Energy from Slowking |
| Metagross | Metallic Hammer | 150 (the extra 150 needs Metal Energy on Slowking, which it never has) |
| Metagross | Bounce Back | 60, and their Active goes to the bench; they choose the new Active |
| Slowking | Super Psy Bolt | 120 |
| Slowking | Seek Inspiration | flips the next card and uses that one instead |
| Slowpoke | Dangle Tail | a Pokémon from your discard pile to your hand |

Weakness and Resistance don't apply to benched Pokémon, and Tera Pokémon on the bench take no damage from attacks, so Trifrost and Thunder Raid can't touch a benched Dragapult ex or Teal Mask Ogerpon ex.

### Controlling the top card

- **Academy at Night** (four copies): once during each player's turn, that player may put a card from their hand on top of their deck. Your opponent can use it too.
- **Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking** (four copies): search your deck for two cards and put them on top in any order. That sets up this turn and next.
- **Poké Pad** (any Pokémon without a Rule Box) and **Ultra Ball** find Kyurem, Zeraora or Metagross; **Night Stretcher** and **Dangle Tail** get them back from the discard pile.

### Energy

- **Telepathic Psychic Energy**: when you attach it from your hand to a Psychic Pokémon, bench up to two Basic Psychic Pokémon from your deck. On turn one that is two more Slowpoke.
- **Boomerang Energy**: if an attack's effect discards it, it comes back after the attack. Trifrost and Thunder Raid discard all of Slowking's Energy; Boomerang survives it.
- **Wondrous Patch** attaches a Basic Psychic Energy from your discard pile to a benched Psychic Pokémon: the next Slowking gets the Energy the last one lost.

### The rest

- **Mega Kangaskhan ex** draws 2 a turn from the Active Spot and is the backup attacker. It is also 3 prizes; keep only one in play when you can.
- **Mew ex (Retreat 0).** Memory Helix lets it use the attacks of your benched Pokémon, so a Mew in the Active Spot can use a benched Slowking's Seek Inspiration (you still need Psychic and one more on Mew). It is a free pivot in a deck whose attacker has Retreat 3.
- **Latias ex** gives your Basics free retreat (not Slowking, which is a Stage 1). **Lillie's Clefairy ex** gives their Dragons Psychic Weakness, and Slowking is a Psychic Pokémon. **Lucky Helmet** draws 2 when the Pokémon it's on is damaged in the Active Spot.

## Stacking, step by step

Every search card and every draw Supporter shuffles your deck. So the order inside a turn is fixed:

1. **Draw first.** Run Errand, Lillie's Determination, Lucky Helmet draws.
2. **Search second.** Poké Pad, Ultra Ball and Secret Box shuffle your deck; that's fine, you haven't stacked yet. Night Stretcher doesn't shuffle, but play it now too.
3. **Stack last.** Academy at Night puts the card you just found on top. Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking stacks two, but it is your Supporter for the turn, so you can't also play Lillie's Determination.
4. **Attack.** Seek Inspiration flips exactly what you chose.

> If you go into Seek Inspiration without knowing the top card, you are hoping for 8 good cards out of what's left of your deck (2 Kyurem, 1 Zeraora, 2 Metagross, 3 Slowking). Don't.

## The first two turns

1. **Turn one:** attach Telepathic Psychic Energy to your Active Slowpoke (or another Psychic Pokémon) and bench two more Slowpoke. Bench Kangaskhan only if you need Run Errand.
2. **Turn two:** evolve to Slowking. Make sure it has Psychic plus one more (Boomerang is ideal). Find Kyurem and stack it.
3. **Pick the first target.** Against evolution decks, Trifrost on turn two hits their Basics and Stage 1s before they evolve. That is your best turn of the game.

## The middle game

- **Choose the library card by the board.** Three targets with 110 HP or less: Kyurem. A benched Pokémon ex with 210 HP or less: Zeraora. One big Active: Metagross for 150, or Super Psy Bolt for 120.
- **Plan two turns with Ciphermaniac's.** Kyurem on top, Zeraora second, for example. Your opponent's Judge, Unfair Stamp or Special Red Card shuffles your deck and erases the second card, so keep a copy of the next attack card in hand when you can.
- **Recycle.** Your library is small: two Kyurem, one Zeraora, two Metagross. Night Stretcher, Dangle Tail and Academy at Night turn one Zeraora into several Thunder Raids.
- **Keep Slowking powered.** After Trifrost or Thunder Raid, Boomerang Energy comes back; attach a Psychic and the same Slowking attacks again. Otherwise Wondrous Patch powers a benched Slowking and Mew ex or Switch brings it in.

## Closing the game

- **Count your prizes in ex.** Thunder Raid on a benched Meowth ex, Fezandipiti ex or Latias ex is 2 prizes for one attack.
- **Their bench is your prize pile.** Trifrost on three damaged benched Pokémon can take three prizes in one turn.
- **Protect against their last hit.** Slowking gives up only 1 prize; your Kangaskhan gives up 3. Late in the game, let Slowking be the Pokémon they have to Knock Out.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Lillie's Determination or Ciphermaniac's this turn.** Draw when you need resources, stack when you know the attack you need for two turns.
2. **Which Pokémon gets Telepathic Psychic Energy.** It must be a Psychic Pokémon, and it is your attachment for the turn.
3. **Trifrost targets.** Knock Outs first, then Pokémon that will evolve next turn, then damage toward next turn's Knock Outs.
4. **How many Pokémon ex to put in play.** Every Kangaskhan, Latias or Mew is prizes for them and, against Dhelmise, 60 more for Dudunsparce ex's Tenacious Tail.
5. **When to use Academy at Night on their turn's behalf.** It is their Stadium too. If they are a deck that benefits from stacking, consider replacing it late.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Turn-two Trifrost into Dragapult

```play
setup: You go first. Slowpoke Active.
you 1: Attach Telepathic Psychic Energy to Slowpoke and bench two more Slowpoke from your deck. Play Academy at Night. No Supporter or attack going first.
opp 1: Budew Active, with three Dreepy benched from Buddy-Buddy Poffin and Poké Pad. Itchy Pollen does 10 to Slowpoke: no Items for you next turn.
you 2: No Poké Pad or Ultra Ball this turn, so Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking does the work: Kyurem on top, Metagross under it. Evolve the Active Slowpoke into Slowking and attach Boomerang Energy (Psychic plus one more). Seek Inspiration flips Kyurem: Trifrost does 110 to all three Dreepy (70 HP each). Three Knock Outs, 3 prizes. Slowking's Energy is discarded, but Boomerang comes back.
opp 2: Their board is Budew and whatever they can bench this turn. No Dragapult ex is coming soon.
you 3: Attach a Psychic: Slowking has Psychic and Boomerang again. Metagross is on top, as stacked. Seek Inspiration, Metallic Hammer, 150 to whatever is Active.
lesson: Trifrost on turn two, before Dreepy evolve, is the best turn this deck has. When Itchy Pollen stops your Items, Ciphermaniac's still stacks two turns of attacks, and Boomerang keeps the same Slowking attacking.
```

### Thunder Raid, again and again

```play
setup: Against Mega Rayquaza (or Basic Box), their bench is full of Pokémon ex with 210 HP or less: Meowth ex (170), Latias ex (210), Fezandipiti ex (210). Zeraora's Thunder Raid does 210 to one of them.
you 3: Poké Pad finds Zeraora; Academy at Night puts it on top. Seek Inspiration: Thunder Raid Knocks Out the benched Latias ex. 2 prizes, and their Basics lose free retreat.
opp 3: Storm Emerald Knocks Out Slowking. They take 1 prize.
you 4: Promote Mew ex. Night Stretcher puts Zeraora back in your hand; Academy at Night puts it on top. Mew ex, with Psychic and one more, uses Memory Helix to borrow a benched Slowking's Seek Inspiration: Thunder Raid Knocks Out Meowth ex. 4 prizes taken.
opp 4: They Knock Out Mew ex (2 prizes for them).
you 5: Wondrous Patch and your attachment power a benched Slowking, and Switch brings it in. The second Night Stretcher and Academy at Night put Zeraora back on top: Thunder Raid Knocks Out Fezandipiti ex. 6 prizes.
lesson: One Zeraora is enough if you treat it as a card you'll reuse. Night Stretcher, Dangle Tail and Academy at Night turn it into a 2-prize attack every turn against decks built from Pokémon ex.
```

### Into Talonflame Dusknoir: spread before they evolve

```play
setup: Dusknoir's Cursed Blast puts 13 counters on any Pokémon, enough for any Slowking anywhere. Dusknoir is Knocked Out doing it, which gives you a prize. Their engine is a Duskull and Dusclops line on the bench, plus Rare Candy.
you 2: Stack Kyurem and Trifrost their two benched Duskull and the Active: both Duskull are Knocked Out (2 prizes), and their Rare Candy has nothing to evolve.
opp 2: Talon Hunt (150) Knocks Out Slowking. They take 1 prize.
you 3: Next Slowking from the bench (Wondrous Patch powered it; Switch or Mew ex gets it in). Trifrost again, aimed at the next Duskull and two damaged targets.
opp 3: Their last Duskull becomes Dusknoir with Rare Candy. Cursed Blast Knocks Out your benched Slowking (you take 1 prize for their Dusknoir) and Talon Hunt Knocks Out the Active one.
lesson: You can't stop Cursed Blast, but you can stop Dusknoir from existing. Spend Trifrost on the Duskull line early, and every Cursed Blast they do use pays you a prize.
```
