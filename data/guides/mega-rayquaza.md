Mega Rayquaza is Delta Reign's new deck and the second most-played deck online. It has the simplest damage formula in the format and the most dangerous prize math: every Pokémon in the list is a Pokémon ex, and the two Megas give up three prizes each.

## At a glance

- **Plays like:** a Colorless toolbox of Basic Pokémon ex (plus Talonflame ex, which skips its evolution) that counts Energy across the whole board.
- **Wins by:** Storm Emerald, 50 damage for every Fire and Lightning Energy attached to all of your Pokémon. Seven of them is 350, more than any Pokémon in the format has.
- **Prize profile:** Mega Rayquaza ex and Mega Kangaskhan ex give up 3. Everything else gives up 2. Your opponent can win with two Knock Outs.
- **Fastest attack:** your first turn if you go second; Mega Rayquaza ex is a Basic.
- **What beats it:** single-prize decks that make you take six Knock Outs, Crushing Hammer (every Fire or Lightning removed is 50 damage), and Team Rocket's Watchtower (Colorless Pokémon lose their Abilities).

## Card by card

### The attackers

- **Mega Rayquaza ex (280 HP, Weak to Lightning, resists Fighting, Retreat 2).** Ruler's Roar: when you play it from your hand onto your bench, look at the top 4 cards, attach a Basic Energy you find there to it, and put the rest on the bottom. Storm Emerald costs Fire, Lightning and one more, and does 50 for each Fire and each Lightning Energy on all of your Pokémon, the bench included.
- **Mega Kangaskhan ex (300 HP, Weak to Fighting, Retreat 3).** Run Errand draws 2 while it is your Active Pokémon (only one Run Errand a turn). Rapid-Fire Combo costs three Energy: 200, plus 50 for each heads until you flip tails.
- **Talonflame ex (280 HP, Retreat 0).** A Stage 2 you never evolve: Excited Dive moves it from your hand to your bench whenever you have a Mega Evolution Pokémon ex in play. Talon Hunt costs two Energy, does 150 and searches your deck for any 2 cards.

### The Energy engine

- **Ethan's Ho-Oh ex (230 HP, Fire).** Golden Flame attaches up to 2 Fire from your hand to one of your benched Ethan's Pokémon, once a turn. The only Ethan's Pokémon in the list are the two Ho-Oh, so a benched Ho-Oh loads itself. That is +100 on Storm Emerald without touching your attachment for the turn.
- **Crispin** finds a Fire and a Lightning: one attached, one in hand.
- **Energy Switch** moves a Basic Energy between your Pokémon. The damage doesn't care where Energy sits; the attack cost does (Rayquaza needs a Fire and a Lightning on itself).

### Support Pokémon

- **Latias ex.** Skyliner: your Basic Pokémon have no Retreat Cost. Kangaskhan's Retreat 3 and Rayquaza's 2 become free, so Kangaskhan can draw from the Active Spot and step aside for Rayquaza the same turn. Latias's own attack needs Psychic Energy, which the list doesn't play.
- **Iron Thorns ex.** While it is Active, Pokémon with a Rule Box (yours too) have no Abilities. It turns off Teal Dance, Fairy Zone, Ripening Charge, Trade, Subjugating Chains, Metallic Signal and Memory Helix, but also your Run Errand, Golden Flame and Skyliner. Use it for a turn when their whole plan runs on a Pokémon ex's Ability.
- **Meowth ex** (Supporter search when benched) and **Fezandipiti ex** (draw 3 after a Knock Out; Cruel Arrow for 100 anywhere).

### Trainers

- **Cyrano** finds three Pokémon ex, which in this deck means any three Pokémon.
- **Legendary Pyroduct** (four copies): Evolution Pokémon in play have no Abilities. Your only Evolution is Talonflame ex, whose Ability works from your hand. Theirs include Drakloak, Dudunsparce, Meganium, Hydrapple ex, Kadabra, Alakazam, Dusclops, Dusknoir, N's Zoroark ex, Metang, Banette and Sinistcha.
- **Blowtorch**: discard a Fire from your hand to discard a Pokémon Tool or Special Energy from one of their Pokémon, or a Stadium. Binding Mochi, Lillie's Pearl, Hero's Cape, Battle Cage and Team Rocket's Watchtower are all fair game.
- **Unfair Stamp** after you lose a Pokémon; **Special Red Card** when they have 3 or fewer prizes left.

## The first two turns

### Count everything

Write the count down if you have to: Fire plus Lightning on all your Pokémon, times 50.

| Count | Storm Emerald | Knocks Out |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 150 | Dreepy, Drakloak, Budew, Abra, Kadabra, Slowking (120), Alakazam and Dhelmise (140) |
| 4 | 200 | Meganium and Dusknoir (160), Meowth ex (170), Lillie's Clefairy ex and Pecharunt ex (190) |
| 5 | 250 | Teal Mask Ogerpon ex (210), Genesect ex (220), Raging Bolt ex (240) |
| 6 | 300 | N's Zoroark ex, Talonflame ex, Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Kangaskhan ex |
| 7 | 350 | Dragapult ex (320), Hydrapple ex (330), Mega Excadrill ex (340) |

### Going second

1. Start Kangaskhan if you can; Run Errand draws 2.
2. Play Mega Rayquaza ex from your hand to the bench for Ruler's Roar. Searching it out with Ultra Ball or Cyrano is fine, but it must be played from your hand to trigger.
3. Crispin: Fire and Lightning, one onto Rayquaza. Attach the other. With Ruler's Roar's Energy that is three, enough to pay and do 150.
4. Bench Latias ex, retreat Kangaskhan for free, and attack.

### Going first

You can't attack or play a Supporter, so build for turn two: Rayquaza onto the bench for Ruler's Roar, a benched Ho-Oh, a manual attachment, and Kangaskhan Active for Run Errand. On turn two Golden Flame adds two more, so Ruler's Roar, two attachments, Crispin and Golden Flame can reach a count of 6.

> Ruler's Roar misses when there is no Basic Energy in the top 4 cards. With about 12 Energy left in a 47-card deck it hits roughly 7 times in 10 (an estimate). Don't build a turn around it hitting.

## The middle game

### Keep your damage on the bench

When they Knock Out Rayquaza, every Energy on it goes to the discard and your count drops with it. Energy on a benched Ho-Oh or Talonflame still counts and survives. Put the extra Energy on the bench and keep only the attack cost (Fire, Lightning, one more) on the Active Rayquaza; Energy Switch fixes the cost of the next attacker.

### Spend your prizes carefully

They need only two Mega Knock Outs. Keep one Mega exposed at a time:

- If Talonflame ex's 150 takes the Knock Out, attack with Talonflame (2 prizes if it falls) instead of Rayquaza (3).
- Don't bench a second Kangaskhan "for Run Errand" if the first one is still drawing.
- Kangaskhan's Rapid-Fire Combo is a fine finisher against 200-HP targets; it doesn't need your Fire and Lightning count.

### The Stadium war

You run four Pyroduct, one Blowtorch and no other Stadiums. Their Watchtower, Battle Cage, Jamming Tower or Forest of Vitality all come off the moment you play a Pyroduct, and each of those is a reason to keep one in hand.

## Closing the game

- **Two-prize targets first.** If they are on 2 prizes, Boss's Orders onto a benched Pokémon ex ends it.
- **Count their Hammers.** Against Dragapult, keep one Energy above the count you need; each heads costs you 50.
- **Iron Thorns ex** as a last-turn blanket: if their only out is an Ability on a Pokémon ex (Flip the Script, Teal Dance, Fairy Zone), Thorns in the Active Spot turns it off for their turn.

## Decisions that decide games

1. **Which Pokémon carries the extra Energy.** The one hardest for them to Knock Out, never the Active Rayquaza.
2. **Pyroduct or keep theirs.** Play Pyroduct whenever their engine is an Evolution's Ability; hold it when their Stadium hurts them more than you.
3. **Rayquaza or Talonflame.** Attack with the Pokémon that takes the Knock Out for the fewest prizes at risk.
4. **Iron Thorns for one turn.** It switches off your own engine too, so only when it stops something that would otherwise win them the game.
5. **Blowtorch target.** Binding Mochi against Zoroark (it is 40 damage), Lillie's Pearl against Alakazam, Battle Cage or Watchtower when they are locking you.

## Playthroughs

These are worked examples built from the cards' text, not records of real games.

### Counting to 350 against Dragapult

```play
setup: You go second. You open Mega Kangaskhan ex with Mega Rayquaza ex, Ethan's Ho-Oh ex and Latias ex in hand.
opp 1: Dreepy Active; Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches a second Dreepy and Budew. Psychic attached. No attack going first.
you 1: Run Errand draws 2. Rayquaza from hand to bench: Ruler's Roar attaches a Lightning. Bench Ho-Oh and Latias. Crispin: Fire onto Rayquaza, Lightning to hand, and attach that Lightning to Rayquaza. Count 3, and Rayquaza holds Fire, Lightning and Lightning. Retreat Kangaskhan for free and Storm Emerald the Dreepy for 150: Knocked Out, 1 prize.
opp 2: They promote Budew and evolve their other Dreepy, plus a third from the deck, into Drakloak. Two Crushing Hammers, one heads: a Lightning comes off Rayquaza (count 2). Itchy Pollen: no Items for you next turn.
you 2: No Energy Switch this turn, so plan around it. Golden Flame puts two Fire from hand on the benched Ho-Oh (count 4). Attach Lightning to Rayquaza (count 5; it holds Fire, Lightning, Lightning again). Boss's Orders the Drakloak holding Fire and Psychic and Storm Emerald it for 250: Knocked Out, 2 prizes taken.
opp 3: Their other Drakloak becomes Dragapult ex. Phantom Dive does 200 to Rayquaza (80 left) and 6 counters to your bench.
you 3: Attach Fire to Ho-Oh: count 6, which is 300, short of Dragapult ex's 320. Golden Flame again with two Fire from hand: count 8, 400 damage. Dragapult ex is Knocked Out: 4 prizes taken.
lesson: Count Fire and Lightning on every Pokémon each turn and keep a margin when they have Hammers. Golden Flame adds 100 a turn without using your attachment, and the Energy it puts on the bench is still there when Rayquaza falls.
```

### Pyroduct into Talonflame Dusknoir

```play
setup: Talonflame Dusknoir's combo is Talon Hunt (150) plus Dusknoir's Cursed Blast (13 damage counters on any Pokémon). Dusknoir and Dusclops are Evolution Pokémon.
you 2: Play Legendary Pyroduct. Evolution Pokémon in play have no Abilities: Cursed Blast is off for both Dusclops and Dusknoir, and your Talonflame ex's Excited Dive still works from your hand.
opp 2: Without Cursed Blast, Talon Hunt does 150 to your Active Rayquaza (130 left). They search for Jamming Tower to replace your Stadium.
you 3: Storm Emerald with a count of 6 Knocks Out their Talonflame ex: 2 prizes.
opp 3: Jamming Tower replaces Pyroduct. Cursed Blast puts 13 counters on Rayquaza: Knocked Out, they take 3 and give up 1 for Dusknoir.
you 4: Play your next Pyroduct, which discards Jamming Tower. Their next Dusknoir can't Cursed Blast while it is in play.
lesson: Four Pyroduct is a lock their deck has to break every turn. Keep one in hand, replay it the turn after they replace it, and make them spend Supporter searches on Stadiums instead of attackers.
```

### Into N's Zoroark: answer the Mochi

```play
setup: N's Zoroark ex's Rampaging Thunder does 250; Binding Mochi on a Poisoned Zoroark adds 40 for 290, enough for Mega Rayquaza ex's 280. Their Team Rocket's Watchtower turns off Ruler's Roar, Run Errand and Meowth ex.
opp 2: Watchtower goes down. Zoroark A's Rampaging Thunder hits your Active Kangaskhan for 250 (50 left). Zoroark B waits on the bench with Binding Mochi; next turn Pecharunt ex's Chains will bring it in Poisoned for 290, enough for Rayquaza.
you 2: Blowtorch (discarding a Fire from your hand) discards B's Binding Mochi. Play Pyroduct: Watchtower is gone, Run Errand works again, and N's Zoroark ex, an Evolution Pokémon, loses Trade. Retreat Kangaskhan for free with Latias. Storm Emerald with a count of 6 does 300 to Zoroark A: Knocked Out, 2 prizes.
opp 3: Chains brings Zoroark B in, Poisoned but with no Mochi. Rampaging Thunder does 250 to Rayquaza, which survives on 30.
you 3: Storm Emerald Knocks Out Zoroark B: 4 prizes taken, and they are down to their last two Zoroarks.
lesson: Against Zoroark the 40 from Binding Mochi is the difference between surviving and giving up 3 prizes. Blowtorch it, and use Pyroduct to swap out their Watchtower.
```
